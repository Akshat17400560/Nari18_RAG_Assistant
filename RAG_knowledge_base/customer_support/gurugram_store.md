# Nari18 Gurugram Store

## 1. Overview

Nari18 operates its physical designer boutique in **SS Omnia, Sector 86, Gurugram, Haryana**.

The boutique is designed as an in-person destination where customers can explore Nari18's fabrics and ethnic wear, receive styling guidance, discuss custom stitching, and work with the tailoring team on personalised outfits. Nari18 also welcomes customers who want to visit the store instead of shopping entirely online.

## 8. Fabric Selection

One of the main advantages of visiting the physical boutique is being able to inspect fabrics in person.

Customers can evaluate characteristics that may be difficult to judge from photographs, such as:

- Texture
- Hand feel
- Weight
- Drape
- Sheen
- Opacity
- Embroidery detail
- Colour appearance
- Border and finishing details

Nari18's website specifically promotes the Gurugram boutique for fabric selection.

For customers who are uncertain between fabrics such as Silk, Organza, Chanderi, Georgette, Muslin, Cotton or Linen, an in-person visit can help with the final selection.


## 9. Unstitched Suit Shopping

The Gurugram boutique offers Nari18's unstitched suit and ethnic-wear collection.

Customers can visit to:

1. Browse available fabrics and designs.
2. Compare different suit sets.
3. Inspect colours and detailing.
4. Discuss the intended occasion.
5. Select a suitable fabric.
6. Discuss stitching requirements.
7. Arrange custom tailoring where offered.

Nari18 currently describes its collection as premium unstitched suits and ethnic wear with custom stitching available across India.


## 10. Custom Stitching and Tailoring

Nari18 provides in-house tailoring and made-to-measure stitching.

The About page states that its in-house master tailors specialise in made-to-measure ethnic wear. After ordering, customers can also communicate with the design team through WhatsApp regarding tailoring preferences.

At the Gurugram boutique, customers can discuss:

- Measurements
- Fit
- Silhouette
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Lining
- Finishing
- Other suitable design preferences

The exact customization available depends on the specific product and fabric.

Customers who choose custom tailoring can discuss measurements and fit preferences with the Nari18 team.

This can be especially useful when a customer:

- Is unsure how to measure themselves.
- Wants a particular fit.
- Needs advice about kurta length.

Nari18 states that its team can guide customers regarding tailoring and measurements through its boutique and contact channels.


## 13. Boutique Visit vs Online Shopping

Nari18 supports both physical and online shopping.

### Visit the Gurugram boutique when:

- You want to feel the fabric before purchasing.
- You want in-person styling advice.
- You need help selecting a fabric.
- You want to discuss custom tailoring face-to-face.
- You want to inspect embroidery or finishing.
- You prefer personalised boutique assistance.

### Shop online when:

- You already know the product you want.
- You are comfortable selecting fabric online.
- You live outside Gurugram.
- You want to browse the wider online catalogue.
- You want products delivered to your location.

Nari18 states that its online collection is available across India, while the Gurugram boutique provides an in-person experience.

Customers visiting specifically for custom stitching should be prepared to discuss:

- Required measurements
- Desired silhouette
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Fit preference
- Lining requirements
- Finishing preferences

## 16. What to Check Before Leaving the Store

When purchasing fabric or arranging custom tailoring, customers may want to confirm:

- Product/fabric selected
- Colour
- Fabric components
- Available pieces
- Embroidery or decorative details
- Required measurements
- Stitching requirements
- Lining requirements
- Expected tailoring details
- Any applicable charges
- Delivery or collection arrangements

Customers should confirm product-specific details with the Nari18 team rather than relying on assumptions based only on the fabric category.
