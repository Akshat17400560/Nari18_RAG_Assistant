# Nari18 Contact Information

## 1. Overview

Nari18 provides customer support through phone/WhatsApp, email, an online contact form, and its physical boutique in Gurugram.

Customers can contact Nari18 for:

- Online order support
- Product and fabric questions
- Custom stitching and tailoring
- Measurements and fit guidance
- Styling consultations
- Exchange-related questions
- Return-related assistance
- Boutique appointments and visits
- Product availability and general shopping guidance

The official Contact page specifically highlights WhatsApp for custom stitching, tailoring appointments, and online order support.

## 10. Custom Stitching Support

Nari18 provides an in-house tailoring service for custom stitching.

Depending on the product and available service, discussions may include:

- Measurements
- Silhouette
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Fit preferences
- Lining
- Finishing
- Other design preferences

Nari18's current website directs customers to WhatsApp for custom-stitching consultation and measurement guidance.

For product-specific stitching availability, the current product page or checkout option should take priority over general website statements.

- Which fabric is better for summer?
- Which fabric is more comfortable?
- Is a particular fabric suitable for office wear?

## When to Contact Nari18 Before Ordering

Customers should consider contacting Nari18 before placing an order when they are unsure about:

- Fabric characteristics
- Colour or product details
- Custom stitching availability
- Measurements
- Lining
- Fit
- Styling
- Product suitability for an occasion
- Fabric care
- Bespoke tailoring requirements

Nari18 specifically recommends contacting the team for guidance where product or fabric details need clarification before doing anything irreversible such as stitching or cutting.


## When to Contact Nari18 After Ordering

After placing an order, customers may contact the team regarding:

- Custom stitching
- Measurements
- Neckline selection
- Sleeve selection
- Fit preferences
- Tailoring requirements
- Order questions
- Product concerns
- Return/exchange guidance

For custom stitching, Nari18's current guides specifically instruct customers to contact the design team through WhatsApp after ordering.

## 19. Information Customers Should Keep Ready

For faster support, customers should provide relevant information instead of sending only a general message.

### Existing order

Provide:

- Order number
- Customer name
- Registered phone/email
- Product name
- Issue or question
- Photos/videos where relevant

### Custom stitching

Provide:

- Order number
- Product name
- Measurements
- Preferred silhouette
- Neckline preference
- Sleeve preference
- Fit preference
- Lining or finishing requirements

### Product enquiry

Provide:

- Product name
- Product link or screenshot if available
- Desired colour, fabric, or style
- Occasion
- Specific question
