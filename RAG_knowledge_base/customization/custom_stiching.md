# Nari18 Custom Stitching & Tailoring

## Overview

Nari18 provides custom stitching and made-to-measure tailoring for its unstitched suit collection. The service is designed to convert an unstitched fabric set into a finished ethnic outfit based on the customer's measurements, preferred silhouette, neckline, sleeve style, and fit preferences.

Nari18 states that its in-house tailoring team specialises in premium ethnic wear and provides boutique-style customisation. Customers can coordinate measurements and styling preferences with the Nari18 design team after placing an order.

The service is particularly useful for customers who want the flexibility of an unstitched suit while avoiding the need to find a separate tailor.

## How Nari18 Custom Stitching Works

The general process is:

1. **Select an unstitched suit**
   - Choose an available unstitched suit or fabric set from Nari18.
   - Review the product details, fabric, components, and any product-level stitching information.

2. **Place the order**
   - Complete the online purchase of the selected unstitched suit.
   - Stitching availability and tailoring options should be confirmed at checkout or with Nari18 where required.

3. **Contact Nari18 after ordering**
   - Nari18 directs customers to WhatsApp the design team after ordering for custom-stitching coordination.
   - The customer can discuss measurements, silhouette, neckline, sleeves, and fit preferences with the team.

4. **Provide measurements**
   - The customer provides the measurements required for tailoring.
   - Nari18's published measurement guidance includes bust, waist, hip, shoulder width, kurta length, sleeve length, and bottom inseam.

5. **Confirm design preferences**
   - Customers can discuss the desired silhouette, neckline, sleeve style, and fit.
   - The Nari18 design team can guide customers when they are unsure which styling or construction option is suitable.

6. **Tailoring and finishing**
   - Nari18's in-house tailoring team handles the stitching.
   - The goal is a made-to-measure, boutique-finish garment rather than a standard ready-to-wear size.

## Measurements Used for Custom Stitching

Nari18's published custom-stitching guide identifies the following measurements as important:

| Measurement | Purpose |
| Bust circumference | Determines upper-body garment fit |
| Waist circumference | Controls fit around the waist |
| Hip circumference | Helps determine lower-body/kurta fit |
| Shoulder width | Controls shoulder and sleeve positioning |
| Kurta length | Determines finished garment length |
| Sleeve length | Determines sleeve size and proportion |
| Bottom inseam | Helps determine the length of the bottom garment |

Measurements should be taken while wearing the type of undergarment intended to be worn with the finished suit. Nari18 recommends avoiding measurement over thick clothing because inaccurate measurements can affect the final fit.

For online orders, Nari18 states that it can provide a detailed measurement guide to assist customers with the process.

## Fabric-Specific Tailoring

Custom stitching is particularly important when working with premium or delicate ethnic fabrics because different materials can require different construction and finishing approaches.

Nari18 specifically highlights fabrics such as:

- Silk
- Chanderi
- Organza
- Georgette
- Cotton
- Linen
- Muslin
- Tissue and other festive fabrics

The way a garment is constructed can depend on the fabric's drape, structure, weight, transparency, surface decoration, embroidery, and finishing requirements. Nari18's tailoring guides emphasise that premium fabrics should be handled by a tailor familiar with their specific characteristics.

For example, Organza, Silk, Chanderi, and Tissue may require more careful handling than a basic cotton fabric. Product-specific fabric and care information should therefore be checked before cutting or stitching.

## Important Customer Guidance

### Custom stitching is different from buying an unstitched suit

An unstitched suit is fabric that has not yet been cut and sewn into the customer's final garment. Purchasing an unstitched suit does not by itself mean that the garment has already been stitched.

Custom stitching is an additional tailoring/customisation process where the fabric is converted into a finished garment according to the customer's measurements and preferences.

### Confirm product-level stitching availability

Nari18's general website promotes custom stitching across its unstitched collection, while individual collection pages can describe stitching as available where offered or selectable during checkout. Therefore, product-level checkout information should be treated as the final reference for whether tailoring is available for a particular item.

### Measurements should be accurate

The final fit depends heavily on the measurements supplied to the tailoring team. Customers should follow Nari18's measurement guidance and provide measurements carefully rather than estimating them.

### Discuss preferences before stitching

Customers should communicate important preferences such as:

- Desired silhouette
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Neckline
- Fit preference
- Lining requirements
- Bottom style
- Any specific finishing requirements

This gives the tailoring team the information required to produce the intended final garment.
