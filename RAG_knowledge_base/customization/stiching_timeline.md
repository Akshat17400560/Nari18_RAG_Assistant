# Nari18 Stitching Timeline

## Overview

Nari18 provides custom stitching for unstitched suit sets through its in-house tailoring team. Customers can order an unstitched suit, share their measurements and preferred design, and have the suit custom stitched before it is delivered.

Nari18's current custom-stitching guide states that an unstitched suit generally requires **3–7 days for stitching**. The same guide separately mentions **5–7 business days for delivery** for online orders.

These timeframes should be treated as general guidance rather than a guaranteed delivery date because the actual timeline can depend on the order, measurements, design complexity, fabric handling, and order volume.


# Current Timeline Guidance

| Stage | Nari18 guidance |
| Custom stitching | Approximately 3–7 days |
| Online delivery mentioned in current guide | 5–7 business days |
| Measurement/design confirmation | Before stitching begins |
| Final delivery | After stitching and order processing |
| Exact delivery date | Confirm with Nari18 for the individual order |

The **3–7 day stitching period** is the most direct current timeline published by Nari18 for converting an unstitched suit into a stitched garment.


# What Does the 3–7 Day Stitching Period Mean?

The 3–7 day period refers to the time required for the custom stitching process.

It should not automatically be interpreted as the complete time from placing an online order to receiving the finished suit.

The overall order timeline can include:

1. Order placement
2. Order processing
3. Fabric preparation
4. Measurement confirmation
5. Design confirmation
6. Cutting
7. Stitching
8. Finishing
9. Final quality check
10. Dispatch
11. Delivery

Therefore:

**Stitching time ≠ total order-to-delivery time**

Nari18's current guide separately states that online delivery is 5–7 business days, so customers should distinguish the tailoring period from the shipping/delivery period.


# Custom Stitching Process and Timeline

## Step 1 — Select the Suit

The customer selects an unstitched suit from Nari18's collection.

The suit normally contains the fabric components required for the intended outfit, depending on the individual product.

The customer should review:

- Fabric
- Colour
- Embroidery
- Borders
- Dupatta
- Bottom fabric
- Product-specific details
- Availability


## Step 2 — Place the Order

The customer places the order through Nari18.

Nari18 currently promotes custom stitching through its in-house tailoring service and asks customers to contact the team after ordering.


## Step 3 — Contact the Design Team

After placing the order, the customer contacts Nari18's design team through WhatsApp for custom-stitching requirements.

Nari18's current guides state that customers should share:

- Measurements
- Preferred silhouette
- Neckline preference
- Sleeve style
- Fit preferences

The design team can guide the customer if they are unsure about the styling choices.


# Step 4 — Measurement Confirmation

Measurements are required before custom stitching begins.

Nari18's current measurement guidance identifies measurements including:

- Bust
- Waist
- Hip
- Shoulder width
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Bottom inseam where applicable

The measurement process is important because custom-stitched orders are made to measure.

Nari18 states that custom-stitched orders are not eligible for exchange under its custom-stitching policy.

Customers should therefore verify measurements carefully before stitching begins.


# Step 5 — Design Confirmation

The customer confirms the desired garment design.

This can include:

- Silhouette
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Sleeve length
- Kurta length
- Bottom style
- Fit preference
- Lining requirements
- Finishing preferences

Nari18's current guides specifically describe choosing the silhouette before the fabric is cut because the silhouette affects how the fabric is used.


# Step 6 — Fabric Inspection and Preparation

Before cutting, the fabric should be checked for:

- Embroidery
- Printed motifs
- Borders
- Zari
- Decorative panels
- Fabric condition
- Available length
- Available width
- Design placement

This step is particularly important for premium fabrics such as Silk, Chanderi, Organza, and Tissue.

Nari18's current guidance emphasizes specialist handling of premium fabrics and recommends planning the garment before cutting.


# Step 7 — Cutting

Once the measurements and design are confirmed, the fabric can be cut according to the selected construction.

The cutting stage depends on:

- Customer measurements
- Silhouette
- Kurta length
- Sleeve style
- Neckline
- Bottom style
- Fabric dimensions
- Embroidery placement
- Border placement

A simple straight-cut suit generally involves less complex cutting than a heavily flared Anarkali, palazzo, or sharara construction.


# Step 8 — Stitching

The main stitching stage forms the garment according to the approved measurements and design.

Nari18 states that its in-house tailoring team handles custom stitching for its unstitched suits.

The published stitching timeframe is approximately:

**3–7 days**

This is a general timeframe and can vary depending on the individual order.


# Step 9 — Finishing

After the main construction is complete, finishing work may include:

- Seam finishing
- Hem finishing
- Neckline finishing
- Sleeve finishing
- Bottom finishing
- Lining attachment where applicable
- Placement of decorative elements
- Final pressing

The amount of finishing required depends on the garment design and fabric.


# Step 10 — Final Review

The completed garment should be checked before dispatch.

Important checks include:

- Overall measurements
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Neckline
- Fit
- Bottom construction
- Seam finishing
- Embroidery placement
- Border placement
- General garment appearance

Because Nari18 states that custom-stitched orders are made-to-measure and are not eligible for exchange, accurate measurement and design confirmation are particularly important.


# Step 11 — Dispatch and Delivery

After stitching and order processing are complete, the finished suit is dispatched for delivery.

Nari18's current custom-stitching guide mentions **5–7 business days for delivery** for online orders.

Delivery time should not be confused with the 3–7 day stitching estimate.

The final arrival date can depend on:

- Order processing
- Tailoring completion
- Dispatch
- Courier transit
- Customer location
- Business days
- Operational conditions

For an urgent requirement, customers should confirm the expected delivery date with Nari18 before placing the order.


# Factors That Can Affect Stitching Time

## 1. Garment Complexity

Simple garments can generally be easier to construct than highly detailed designs.

Potentially more complex constructions include:

- Flared Anarkali
- Palazzo
- Sharara
- Layered garments
- Multiple panels
- Elaborate finishing

Nari18 states that stitching charges vary according to garment complexity, and the same complexity can also affect the practical tailoring workflow.


## 2. Embroidery

Heavily embroidered or embellished fabrics may require additional care during cutting and stitching.

Important elements can include:

- Zari
- Thread embroidery
- Pearls
- Crystals
- Decorative borders
- Handwork
- Embroidered panels

The fabric may need to be positioned carefully so important decorative work remains correctly aligned.


## 3. Fabric Type

Different fabrics require different handling.

Nari18 specifically notes that premium materials such as Silk, Chanderi, and Organza require specialist handling.

Organza, Tissue, Silk, and heavily embellished fabrics may require more careful construction than a simple everyday Cotton suit.


## 4. Measurement Issues

Incomplete or unclear measurements can delay the start of custom stitching.

Customers should provide the required measurements clearly and confirm unusual fit preferences before cutting.

If the tailoring team needs clarification, the stitching process may need to wait until the design requirements are confirmed.


## 5. Design Changes

Changes requested after the design has already been finalized can affect the timeline.

Customers should confirm the following before cutting:

- Silhouette
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Sleeve length
- Kurta length
- Bottom style
- Fit preference
- Lining
- Decorative placement

This reduces the possibility of delays caused by changes during construction.

# Urgent Orders

Customers who need a suit for a specific date should not assume that the standard 3–7 day stitching estimate guarantees arrival before that date.

For an urgent requirement, provide Nari18 with:

- Required date
- Product name
- Order details
- Measurements
- Preferred silhouette
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Any special requirements

Ask the team to confirm whether the required delivery date is achievable before relying on the order for an event.

Nari18 provides WhatsApp support for custom stitching and order assistance.


# Stitching Timeline vs Delivery Timeline

These terms should be kept separate in customer conversations.

### Stitching Timeline

The time required by the tailoring team to convert the unstitched material into the finished garment.

**Current Nari18 guidance: approximately 3–7 days.**

### Delivery Timeline

The time associated with getting the order to the customer's address after processing and dispatch.

**Current Nari18 guide: 5–7 business days for delivery.**

### Total Order Timeline

The complete customer experience can include both processing/tailoring and delivery.

Therefore, the two published timeframes should not simply be added together unless Nari18 specifically confirms that they apply sequentially to the individual order.

# Custom Stitching and Exchange Policy

Nari18 states that custom-stitched orders are **made-to-measure and are not eligible for exchange**.

Manufacturing defects are handled under a separate policy. Nari18 currently states that exchange requests for manufacturing defects can be made within 7 days, with photos or videos required for verification.

Because of this policy, customers should carefully verify their measurements and design preferences before stitching begins.


# Recommended Customer Timeline

For customers planning around an event, a safer approach is:

1. Choose the suit well before the event.
2. Confirm that the product is available.
3. Place the order.
4. Contact Nari18 for custom stitching.
5. Submit measurements promptly.
6. Finalize silhouette, neckline, and sleeves.
7. Confirm any lining or finishing requirements.
8. Ask Nari18 for the expected completion and delivery date.
9. Allow additional time for unexpected delays.
10. Check the finished garment immediately after delivery.

This is planning guidance, not a guarantee of a particular turnaround time.

# Important Timeline Limitations

- Nari18 currently publishes approximately **3–7 days for stitching**.
- The current guide separately mentions **5–7 business days for delivery**.
- Do not describe 3–7 days as a guaranteed delivery date.
- Do not automatically add the stitching and delivery ranges together.
- Complex garments may require more tailoring work.
- Embroidery, delicate fabrics, lining, special finishing, and design complexity can affect the practical timeline.
- Missing or unclear measurements can delay the start of stitching.
- Design changes after confirmation can affect the timeline.
- Urgent orders should be confirmed directly with Nari18 before relying on a specific event date.
- The homepage currently promotes custom stitching but does not publish a separate fixed stitching SLA, so the individual order confirmation should take priority.
- Website timelines can change, so current order/checkout communication should be treated as the final source for a specific order.
