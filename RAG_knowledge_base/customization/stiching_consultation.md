# Nari18 Stitching Consultation

## Overview

Nari18 provides custom stitching consultation for customers who purchase unstitched suit sets and want them converted into made-to-measure garments. The consultation is designed to help customers make decisions about measurements, silhouette, neckline, sleeve style, fit preferences, lining, finishing, and fabric handling.

Nari18 promotes an in-house tailoring team and designer-led consultation rather than requiring the customer to find a separate tailor. Customers can contact the Nari18 team through WhatsApp after ordering and share their measurements and preferred design choices. The current website also describes concierge support for sizing and styling consultations.

## How the Consultation Works

The typical online consultation follows this sequence:

1. **Select an unstitched suit**
   - Choose the desired suit and fabric from the Nari18 collection.
   - Review the product details, available fabric pieces, design, colour, and current availability.

2. **Place the order**
   - After ordering, contact Nari18 for custom stitching consultation.
   - The current website specifically directs customers to WhatsApp for custom stitching support.

3. **Share measurements**
   - Provide the measurements requested by the Nari18 tailoring team.
   - Current Nari18 guides identify measurements such as bust, waist, hip, shoulder width, kurta length, and sleeve length; some guides also include bottom inseam.

4. **Discuss the silhouette**
   - Customers can discuss the preferred garment shape with the designer.
   - Documented options include straight-cut, A-line, flared Anarkali, palazzo, sharara, and short-kurta/co-ordinated styling where suitable.

5. **Discuss neckline and sleeves**
   - Nari18's current guide documents round, V-neck, boat neck, keyhole, and mandarin-collar options.
   - Sleeve options include full, three-quarter, half, and sleeveless.

6. **Discuss fit preferences**
   - The consultation can be used to clarify the customer's preferred level of comfort, ease, and overall fit.
   - Customers should communicate any specific fit preferences before the fabric is cut.

7. **Discuss lining and finishing**
   - Nari18's homepage specifically mentions consultation for measurements, lining, and finishing guidance.
   - Lining requirements can be especially important for fabrics that are lightweight, sheer, delicate, or structured.

8. **Fabric-specific tailoring discussion**
   - Premium fabrics can require different construction and finishing techniques.
   - Nari18 specifically highlights specialist handling for fabrics such as Silk, Chanderi, and Organza.

9. **Tailoring and finishing**
   - Once measurements and design preferences are confirmed, the in-house tailoring team handles construction and finishing.
   - The finished garment is intended to be delivered to the customer rather than requiring the customer to arrange a separate tailor.

## What Customers Can Discuss During Consultation

### 1. Measurements

The consultation can help customers understand how measurements should be taken and submitted.

Common measurements documented by Nari18 include:

- Bust circumference
- Waist circumference
- Hip circumference
- Shoulder width
- Kurta length from shoulder
- Sleeve length
- Bottom inseam where required

Nari18 recommends taking measurements while wearing the undergarment intended to be worn with the suit and avoiding measurement over thick clothing.

If a customer is unsure about a measurement, they should ask the Nari18 team before submitting it rather than guessing.

### 2. Silhouette

The consultation can help match the fabric and design to an appropriate silhouette.

Documented examples include:

- Straight-cut kurta
- A-line kurta
- Flared Anarkali-style kurta
- Palazzo styling
- Sharara styling
- Short kurta
- Co-ordinated/contemporary styling where suitable

The final choice may depend on the available fabric quantity, borders, embroidery placement, fabric drape, and the customer's desired look.

### 3. Neckline

Customers can discuss neckline preferences with the Nari18 designer.

Documented neckline options include:

- Round neck
- V-neck
- Boat neck
- Keyhole
- Mandarin collar

The most suitable neckline can depend on the existing embroidery, fabric structure, neckline border, lining, and chosen silhouette.

### 4. Sleeve Style

Documented sleeve options include:

- Full sleeves
- Three-quarter sleeves
- Half sleeves
- Sleeveless

Sleeve length and construction should be discussed together with the overall silhouette and neckline so that the finished garment has a balanced appearance.

### 5. Fit Preferences

Customers can communicate preferences regarding the final fit during consultation.

Useful information to discuss includes:

- Preferred overall fit
- Comfortable versus closer fit
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Ease around the bust, waist, and hips
- Preferred bottom style
- Desired overall silhouette

Measurements should remain the primary basis for made-to-measure construction; a preference such as "loose fit" or "slightly fitted" should be communicated as an additional instruction.

### 6. Lining and Opacity

Lining can be discussed before stitching, particularly when the selected fabric is lightweight or translucent.

The consultation may address:

- Whether lining is required
- Which garment sections require lining
- Comfort and breathability
- Opacity
- How lining may affect drape
- Interaction between lining and embroidery or decorative details

Nari18's homepage specifically identifies lining guidance as part of the support available for custom stitching.

### 7. Finishing Details

Customers can also discuss finishing requirements before stitching.

Possible discussion areas include:

- Hem finishing
- Sleeve finishing
- Neckline finishing
- Border placement
- Lining finish
- Overall garment structure
- Placement of existing decorative work

Exact finishing options depend on the individual fabric and suit set.

## What to Prepare Before a Consultation

Customers can make the consultation more effective by preparing:

- Order details
- Selected suit/product name
- Accurate body measurements
- Preferred silhouette
- Preferred neckline
- Preferred sleeve length/style
- Desired kurta length
- Bottom preference
- Fit preference
- Lining requirement
- Any specific styling preference
- Event or occasion for which the suit is being stitched

If the customer has a reference outfit or preferred style, it can be useful to communicate that preference clearly to the designer.

## Consultation and Design Decisions

A consultation should not be treated as a guarantee that every design option can be applied to every suit.

The final design may depend on:

- Fabric quantity
- Fabric width
- Fabric behaviour
- Existing embroidery
- Border placement
- Decorative panels
- Dupatta design
- Available bottom fabric
- Need for lining
- Desired silhouette
- Customer measurements

For example, changing a heavily embroidered neckline into a substantially different neckline may not always be practical without affecting the existing design.

The Nari18 designer or tailoring team should therefore be treated as the final source for product-specific feasibility.

## Important Policy Information

Nari18 states that **custom-stitched orders are made-to-measure and are not eligible for exchange**. Customers who need sizing assistance are encouraged to obtain guidance before the garment is stitched.

Nari18 separately states that exchange requests for manufacturing defects are accepted within **7 days**, subject to the stated verification process and supporting photos/videos. This defect policy should not be confused with a general exchange right for fit or preference changes.
