# Nari18 Measurement Options

## Overview

Nari18 offers custom stitching for unstitched suits based on customer-provided measurements. Because an unstitched suit is supplied as fabric rather than a ready-made standard-size garment, measurements are used by the tailoring team to create the finished garment according to the customer's body proportions and preferred fit.

Nari18's current website states that customers can receive tailored measurements through its in-house team and can contact the design team for measurement guidance. The site also recommends contacting Nari18 when customers need help with measurements, lining, or finishing.

## Nari18's Core Measurement Set

Nari18's published custom-stitching guidance identifies the following measurements as the core measurements needed for a custom-stitched suit:

| Measurement | What it controls |
| Bust circumference | Fit across the bust and upper body |
| Waist circumference | Fit around the waist |
| Hip circumference | Fit around the hip and lower kurta area |
| Shoulder width | Shoulder and sleeve positioning |
| Kurta length | Finished length of the kurta |
| Sleeve length | Finished sleeve length |
| Bottom inseam | Length of the bottom garment |

Nari18's measurement guide recommends taking measurements while wearing the undergarment intended to be worn with the finished suit and avoiding measurement over thick clothing.

## 1. Bust Measurement

Bust measurement determines the circumference around the fullest part of the bust.

For a custom kurta, this measurement helps establish the upper-body width and contributes to the overall comfort and fit of the garment.

### Measurement guidance

- Stand naturally.
- Keep the measuring tape horizontal around the fullest part of the bust.
- Do not pull the tape excessively tight.
- Do not intentionally add extra looseness while taking the body measurement.
- Measure over the fitted undergarment intended for the outfit.
- Record the measurement accurately.

The tailoring team can then account for the appropriate garment ease during construction rather than the customer artificially increasing the body measurement.

## 2. Waist Measurement

Waist circumference is used to determine the fit around the waist area.

### Measurement guidance

- Stand naturally without pulling the stomach in.
- Place the tape around the natural waist area.
- Keep the tape level.
- Keep the tape comfortably fitted rather than tight.
- Record the actual body measurement.

The desired final fit can be discussed separately with the Nari18 tailoring team.

## 3. Hip Measurement

Hip circumference helps determine the fit and available ease around the lower portion of the kurta.

### Measurement guidance

- Measure around the fullest part of the hips.
- Keep the tape horizontal.
- Stand naturally.
- Do not compress the body with the tape.
- Record the actual measurement rather than estimating it.

Hip measurement becomes particularly relevant when selecting a relatively fitted straight-cut silhouette.

## 4. Shoulder Width

Shoulder width determines the positioning of the shoulder seam and influences how sleeves sit on the body.

An inaccurate shoulder measurement can affect:

- Shoulder fit
- Sleeve placement
- Arm movement
- Overall garment balance

Customers should provide the shoulder measurement according to Nari18's measurement instructions rather than estimating it from a standard clothing size.

## 5. Kurta Length

Kurta length determines how long the finished kurta will be.

The length should be communicated according to the customer's preferred finished garment rather than simply selecting XS, S, M, L, or XL.

Possible preferences can include:

- Short kurta
- Standard-length kurta
- Longer kurta
- Ankle-length or extended silhouettes where the fabric and design permit

Nari18's custom-stitching guidance specifically identifies kurta length from the shoulder as an important measurement.

The available fabric length and existing embroidery or border placement should also be considered before confirming an unusually long or short finished length.

## 6. Sleeve Length

Sleeve length determines the finished length of the sleeves.

Nari18's tailoring guidance identifies several sleeve options:

- Full sleeves
- Three-quarter sleeves
- Half sleeves
- Sleeveless

The desired sleeve style should be communicated along with the sleeve measurement.

For decorated fabrics, sleeve construction should also account for embroidery, borders, motifs, and the amount of fabric available.

## 7. Bottom Inseam

Bottom inseam is used to determine the length of the bottom garment.

It is particularly relevant when the unstitched set is being converted into:

- Straight pants
- Salwar-style bottoms
- Other suitable bottom constructions

The final bottom design should be confirmed with the tailoring team because the amount and type of bottom fabric can affect which styles are practical.

## Taking Measurements Correctly

Accurate measurements are essential for made-to-measure tailoring.

Nari18 recommends:

- Measuring over the undergarment intended for the finished outfit.
- Avoiding measurement over thick clothing.
- Providing measurements carefully.
- Using the Nari18 measurement guide when ordering custom stitching online.

A measurement should represent the customer's body rather than the dimensions of a favourite ready-made garment.

## Body Measurement vs Garment Measurement

These two measurements should not be confused.

### Body measurement

The actual circumference or length of the customer's body.

### Garment measurement

The finished dimensions of the stitched garment after accounting for ease, construction, seams, and design.

When Nari18 asks for customer measurements, the customer should provide the requested body measurements rather than trying to calculate the finished garment dimensions independently.

The tailoring team can determine the appropriate garment ease based on the selected silhouette and desired fit.

## Fit Preferences

Measurements alone do not completely determine how a garment should fit.

Customers should also communicate their preferred fit, such as:

- Comfortable fit
- Regular fit
- More fitted silhouette
- Relaxed silhouette

The same body measurements can result in different finished garments depending on the chosen silhouette and fit preference.

Nari18's custom-stitching process allows customers to discuss their preferred silhouette and styling with the design team.

## Measurements for Different Silhouettes

The importance of individual measurements can vary according to the chosen design.

### Straight-Cut Kurta

Bust, waist, hip, shoulder, and kurta length are particularly important because the silhouette follows a relatively direct shape.

### A-Line Kurta

Bust, shoulder, and kurta length remain important, while the lower portion of the garment is designed with additional flare.

### Flared / Anarkali Style

Kurta length and the intended flare become particularly important. The available fabric must be sufficient for the desired construction.

### Short Kurta

Kurta length becomes a key design measurement.

### Palazzo or Sharara Styling

Bottom measurements and available fabric become especially important when creating wider or more elaborate bottom constructions.

These styling options should be confirmed with Nari18 before cutting because not every unstitched set necessarily provides the same amount of usable fabric.

## Neckline and Measurement Coordination

Neckline selection is not normally represented by a single body measurement.

Nari18's custom-stitching guide identifies options such as:

- Round neck
- V-neck
- Boat neck
- Keyhole neckline
- Mandarin collar

The customer should communicate the preferred neckline to the design team.

For embroidered or decorative suits, the neckline should also be checked against the existing embroidery or panel placement before cutting.

## Sleeve Style and Measurement Coordination

Customers can communicate their preferred sleeve style together with sleeve length.

Common Nari18 options include:

- Full
- Three-quarter
- Half
- Sleeveless

The final sleeve design depends on the available fabric and existing design elements.

For example, an embroidered border may restrict how a sleeve can be cut without disrupting the original design.

## Measurement and Lining

Lining is a construction decision rather than simply a measurement.

Whether lining is needed can depend on:

- Fabric transparency
- Fabric weight
- Embroidery
- Garment construction
- Customer preference
- Intended use

Nari18's current website specifically states that customers can contact the team for guidance regarding measurements, lining, and finishing.

For sheer fabrics such as Organza, lining requirements should be confirmed before cutting.

## Online Measurement Option

Customers ordering online do not necessarily need to visit the Nari18 boutique to coordinate custom measurements.

Nari18's published process allows customers to:

1. Purchase an unstitched suit.
2. Contact the Nari18 design team after ordering.
3. Provide the required measurements.
4. Communicate silhouette and styling preferences.
5. Receive guidance if clarification is required.
6. Have the suit custom stitched by the in-house tailoring team.

Nari18 states that it provides a detailed measurement guide for customers ordering custom stitching online.

## When a Customer Is Unsure About Measurements

If a customer is unsure about how to take a particular measurement, the recommended approach is to contact Nari18 rather than guessing.

Nari18's website specifically offers measurement guidance through its design team.

For online orders, customers can coordinate with the team after ordering through the contact channel provided by Nari18.

## Existing Garment as a Reference

A customer may have an existing garment that fits well, but its measurements should not automatically be substituted for the body measurements requested by Nari18.

A finished garment includes:

- Wearing ease
- Seam allowances
- Design ease
- Construction differences
- Fabric behaviour

Therefore, a garment measurement and a body measurement are not necessarily identical.

If an existing garment is used as a reference, it should be treated as an additional fit reference rather than automatically assumed to be the required Nari18 measurement.

## Measurement Verification Before Stitching

Before the fabric is cut, customers should verify:

- Bust measurement
- Waist measurement
- Hip measurement
- Shoulder width
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Bottom inseam
- Preferred fit
- Preferred silhouette
- Neckline
- Sleeve style

This is especially important because cutting unstitched fabric is an irreversible step.

Nari18's tailoring guidance emphasises the importance of accurate measurements and selecting the silhouette and styling before the tailoring process begins.

## Measurements and Fabric Availability

A requested finished measurement does not guarantee that every silhouette or length can be produced from every unstitched set.

The available fabric must be sufficient for:

- The selected kurta length
- Sleeve construction
- Desired flare
- Bottom length
- Borders and decorative placement
- Seam allowances
- Lining where required

This is particularly important for heavily decorated or panelled fabrics because cutting options may be constrained by the existing design.

The Nari18 team should be consulted if a customer requests a substantially different silhouette or unusually long/short garment.

## Important Measurement Limitations

Do not assume that:

- Standard XS/S/M/L/XL sizing applies to an unstitched suit.
- A customer's ready-to-wear size can replace body measurements.
- Every customer requires the same amount of garment ease.
- Every silhouette can be created from every fabric set.
- Every product has identical fabric dimensions.
- A measurement automatically guarantees a particular finished design.
- A customer can request unlimited measurement-based modifications.

The exact tailoring outcome depends on the supplied measurements, fabric, product design, available material, selected silhouette, and preferences confirmed with Nari18.

## RAG Retrieval Rules

When answering Nari18 measurement questions:

1. The core measurement set is **bust, waist, hip, shoulder width, kurta length, sleeve length, and bottom inseam**.
2. Nari18 recommends taking measurements over the undergarment intended for the outfit.
3. Avoid measuring over thick clothing.
4. Do not invent numeric measurement values when Nari18 has not supplied them.
5. Do not convert a customer's standard ready-to-wear size into body measurements without actual measurements.
6. Distinguish **body measurements** from **finished garment measurements**.
7. Fit preference should be treated separately from the raw body measurement.
8. Neckline and sleeve choices are styling options, not replacements for body measurements.
9. Product-specific fabric dimensions can limit the final silhouette or length.
10. For sheer or delicate fabrics, lining and construction requirements should be confirmed separately.
11. If a customer is unsure how to measure, recommend using Nari18's measurement guide or contacting the Nari18 design team.
12. For online orders, Nari18 states that a detailed measurement guide is provided for custom stitching.
13. Do not claim that every product has identical measurement or tailoring options.
14. Current product/checkout information should take priority when determining whether custom tailoring is available for a particular item.
