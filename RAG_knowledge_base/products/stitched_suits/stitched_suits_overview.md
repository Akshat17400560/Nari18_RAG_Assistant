# Nari18 Stitched Suits Overview

## Overview

Nari18's stitched-suit offering is closely connected to its core unstitched-suit business. The website primarily presents **premium unstitched suit sets** and provides an **in-house custom stitching and tailoring service** for customers who want the purchased fabric converted into a finished suit.

The Nari18 homepage describes this model as **"Buy Unstitched — Get It Stitched. All in One Place."** Customers can purchase an unstitched suit and have the Nari18 in-house tailoring team stitch it according to their measurements and preferred design.

Therefore, in the Nari18 knowledge base, "stitched suits" should generally be understood as **custom-stitched versions of Nari18's unstitched suit sets**, unless a specific product page identifies a garment as already stitched or ready-to-wear.

## Nari18's Stitched-Suit Model

The typical Nari18 process is:

1. Customer selects an unstitched suit from the Nari18 catalog.
2. Customer places the order.
3. Custom stitching is selected or requested where available.
4. Customer provides measurements and preferred design details.
5. Nari18's in-house tailoring team prepares the garment.
6. The finished suit is delivered to the customer.

Nari18 states that customers can consult with its designer through WhatsApp after ordering for custom-stitching requirements. The website provides WhatsApp contact number **+91 8826446755** for this service.

## Custom Stitching vs Ready-to-Wear

A stitched suit and a ready-to-wear suit should not automatically be treated as the same thing.

### Nari18 Custom-Stitched Suit

A custom-stitched Nari18 suit starts as an unstitched fabric set and is then tailored according to the customer's measurements and preferences.

Key characteristics include:

- Made from the customer's selected Nari18 fabric set
- Tailored to provided measurements
- Choice of neckline
- Choice of sleeve style
- Choice of kurta length
- Choice of silhouette
- Choice of bottom style
- Designer/tailoring consultation
- In-house stitching service

### Ready-to-Wear Stitched Suit

A conventional ready-to-wear suit is normally manufactured as a completed garment in predetermined sizes.

Key characteristics include:

- Already cut and stitched
- Standard sizing
- Faster to wear after purchase
- Less scope for structural customization
- Fit depends on the available size and garment construction

Nari18's published buying guide similarly describes the distinction: unstitched suits provide fabric that can be tailored to the customer's measurements, while stitched suits are pre-made garments in standard sizes.

## What Can Be Customized?

The final stitched garment can be planned around the customer's preferred fit and styling.

Important customization areas include:

### Measurements

Nari18's measurement guidance includes:

- Bust
- Waist
- Hip
- Shoulder width
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Bottom inseam

Accurate measurements are important because the finished garment is created from the selected fabric rather than chosen from a standard ready-made size chart.

### Neckline

Possible neckline preferences discussed by Nari18 include:

- Round neck
- V-neck
- Boat neck
- Keyhole
- Mandarin collar

The exact neckline offered may depend on the fabric, design, and tailoring requirements.

### Sleeves

Customers can discuss sleeve preferences such as:

- Full sleeves
- Three-quarter sleeves
- Half sleeves
- Sleeveless

Specific sleeve construction should be confirmed with Nari18 before stitching.

### Silhouette

Nari18's guides describe several commonly used suit silhouettes:

- Straight-cut kurta with straight-leg bottom
- A-line kurta with churidar
- Flared Anarkali-style kurta with palazzo
- Short kurta with sharara

The selected silhouette affects how the fabric is cut and should therefore be decided before tailoring begins.

## Custom Stitching Process

### Step 1 — Select the Suit

Choose an available unstitched suit from the Nari18 catalog.

The catalog provides product images, product names, fabric categories, prices, reviews, variants, and availability.

### Step 2 — Confirm Stitching Availability

Custom stitching is available on many Nari18 products, but customers should verify the option for the specific product before assuming that stitching is included.

### Step 3 — Decide the Design

Before the fabric is cut, determine the desired:

- Silhouette
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Kurta length
- Bottom style
- Other finishing requirements

### Step 4 — Provide Measurements

Provide the required measurements using Nari18's measurement guidance.

Customers should ideally take measurements while wearing the undergarment normally used with the finished suit and avoid taking measurements over bulky clothing.

### Step 5 — Discuss Special Requirements

For unusual designs, lining, finishing, or other tailoring requirements, customers can discuss the requirements with Nari18's team through the available support/WhatsApp channel.

### Step 6 — Tailoring

Nari18's in-house tailoring team prepares the finished suit according to the confirmed measurements and design requirements.

## Why Choose Nari18 Custom Stitching?

The main benefit is the combination of **designer fabric selection and personalized tailoring**.

Customers can:

- Select the exact fabric and design they prefer
- Avoid relying entirely on standard ready-made sizing
- Request personalized measurements
- Select a preferred silhouette
- Discuss neckline and sleeve preferences
- Receive tailoring guidance from Nari18
- Have the suit stitched through Nari18 rather than finding a separate tailor

Nari18 positions its service as a combined shopping and tailoring experience rather than simply selling dress material.

## Important Customer Considerations

Before choosing custom stitching, customers should verify:

- Whether stitching is available for the selected product
- Required measurements
- Preferred silhouette
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Kurta length
- Bottom style
- Lining requirements, if applicable
- Any special finishing requirements
- Applicable stitching charges
- Applicable exchange/return conditions

Custom stitching charges can vary depending on garment complexity. Nari18's published guide states that stitching charges are discussed after ordering and that more complex or layered garments may cost more than basic stitching.

## RAG Usage Notes

When answering questions about Nari18 stitched suits:

- Treat Nari18's stitched offering primarily as **custom stitching of its unstitched suit sets**.
- Do not claim that Nari18 maintains a separate large ready-to-wear stitched-suit catalog unless a current product page confirms it.
- Do not assume that custom stitching is available for every product without checking.
- Do not assume a fixed stitching price; charges can depend on the garment and complexity.
- Do not invent measurements or claim a particular fit without product/customer-specific information.
- When a customer asks for a stitched version of a specific suit, identify the underlying product and verify whether custom stitching is available.
- Use current product information for fabric, colour, price, availability, and design details.
- Distinguish between the **unstitched product price** and any applicable **custom-stitching charges**.
- For measurements, use Nari18's measurement guidance rather than inventing a size chart.
- For special tailoring requests, direct the customer to Nari18's current custom-stitching support.
- Do not describe an unstitched product as already stitched merely because custom stitching is available.
- Do not assume that all stitched garments have identical silhouettes, necklines, sleeves, or finishing.
