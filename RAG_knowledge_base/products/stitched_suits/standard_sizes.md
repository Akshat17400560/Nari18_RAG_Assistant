# Nari18 Standard Sizes

## Overview

Nari18's core product range consists primarily of **unstitched suit sets**, which are sold as fabric rather than as finished garments in fixed S, M, L, XL, or XXL sizes.

For Nari18 custom stitching, the final garment is made according to the customer's measurements and selected styling preferences. This means customers should generally think in terms of **body measurements and garment measurements**, rather than selecting a standard ready-to-wear size.

Nari18's own buying guide explains that an unstitched suit is not purchased as a finished garment in S/M/L; the fabric is later tailored to the customer's measurements.

## Does Nari18 Have Standard S/M/L Sizes?

For its unstitched suit sets, **standard ready-to-wear sizes are not the primary sizing system**.

An unstitched suit is supplied as fabric components, typically including:

- Kurta fabric
- Bottom fabric
- Dupatta

The customer then has the fabric tailored to the required measurements.

This is different from a conventional ready-to-wear suit where the customer selects a pre-stitched garment from standard sizes such as S, M, L, or XL.

Nari18's collection pages also state that unstitched sets are sold as fabric and that tailoring is based on measurements supplied by the customer or tailor.

## How Nari18 Custom Sizing Works

For customers using Nari18's custom-stitching service, the important information is the customer's actual measurements.

Nari18's measurement guidance includes:

- Bust
- Waist
- Hip
- Shoulder width
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Bottom inseam

These measurements are used to create the finished garment rather than simply selecting a standard size label.

## Key Measurements

### Bust

Bust measurement is used to determine the required width of the upper garment around the chest.

For accurate measurement:

- Stand naturally.
- Keep the measuring tape level around the fullest part of the bust.
- Do not pull the tape excessively tight.
- Measure while wearing the type of undergarment normally worn with the suit.

### Waist

The waist measurement helps determine the fit around the waist area.

The measurement should be taken without compressing the body or holding the stomach in.

### Hip

Hip measurement is particularly important for longer kurtas and fitted or semi-fitted silhouettes.

Measure around the fullest part of the hips while standing naturally.

### Shoulder Width

Shoulder width affects how the kurta sits across the upper body.

An incorrect shoulder measurement can affect:

- Sleeve position
- Arm movement
- Overall garment balance
- Neckline placement

### Kurta Length

Kurta length should be specified according to the desired final style.

The appropriate length depends on:

- Height
- Silhouette
- Bottom style
- Occasion
- Personal preference

A short kurta with sharara requires a different proportion from a long straight kurta with straight pants.

### Sleeve Length

Sleeve length should be provided according to the desired sleeve style.

Common preferences include:

- Full sleeve
- Three-quarter sleeve
- Half sleeve
- Sleeveless

Nari18's styling guidance discusses these sleeve options as part of custom tailoring.

### Bottom Inseam

Bottom inseam is used when creating the lower garment and should be provided according to the selected bottom style.

The required measurement may vary depending on whether the customer chooses:

- Straight pants
- Salwar
- Churidar
- Palazzo
- Sharara
- Wide-leg pants

## Why Nari18 Uses Measurement-Based Stitching

The purpose of custom stitching is to provide a garment that is shaped around the individual customer instead of an assumed average body shape.

Nari18's website emphasizes that its custom-stitching service allows the customer's selected unstitched fabric to be converted into a garment using the customer's exact measurements.

This can be particularly useful for customers whose measurements do not correspond closely to conventional ready-to-wear size charts.

## Recommended Measurement Process

Customers preparing measurements for Nari18 custom stitching should:

1. Wear a fitted undergarment normally worn with the suit.
2. Stand naturally without changing posture.
3. Use a flexible measuring tape.
4. Keep the tape comfortably fitted rather than excessively tight.
5. Measure each required body dimension carefully.
6. Record measurements consistently.
7. Confirm preferred garment lengths separately.
8. Specify sleeve and neckline preferences.
9. Confirm the desired bottom style.
10. Share the measurements with Nari18 according to the current ordering process.

Nari18 states that its team can guide customers through measurements and styling requirements for custom stitching.

## Standard Sizes Should Not Be Invented

A common mistake when answering Nari18 sizing questions is to create an assumed size chart such as:

| Size | Bust | Waist | Hip |
| S | 36" | 30" | 38" |
| M | 38" | 32" | 40" |
| L | 40" | 34" | 42" |
| XL | 42" | 36" | 44" |

**This should not be presented as an official Nari18 size chart.**

Nari18's published guidance does not establish these values as an official standard sizing table. The correct approach is to use the customer's actual measurements for custom stitching.

## Size and Fit Are Different

A size label alone does not determine whether a garment will fit well.

For custom stitching, Nari18 can use individual measurements to determine the garment dimensions.

Fit can also depend on:

- Desired ease
- Silhouette
- Fabric behaviour
- Sleeve design
- Shoulder measurement
- Kurta length
- Bottom style
- Lining
- Construction

Therefore, two customers with the same nominal ready-to-wear size may still require different measurements for a personalized garment.

## Measurements and Silhouette

Measurements should be considered together with the selected silhouette.

For example:

### Straight-Cut Suit

Usually requires careful consideration of:

- Bust
- Waist
- Hip
- Shoulder
- Kurta length

### A-Line Suit

The upper-body measurements remain important, while the lower portion gradually expands toward the hem.

### Anarkali

The amount of flare and length can significantly affect the final garment.

### Short Kurta + Sharara

Kurta length and bottom measurements become particularly important because the proportions of both garments need to work together.

### Palazzo or Wide-Leg Bottom

Bottom length, waist, and inseam should be considered along with the desired width and fall.
