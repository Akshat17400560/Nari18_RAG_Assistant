# What Is a Stitched Suit?

## Definition

A stitched suit is a **finished ethnic outfit that has already been cut and sewn into a wearable garment**. Unlike an unstitched suit, where the customer receives fabric and later decides how it should be tailored, a stitched suit has already been converted into garments such as a kurta, bottom, and coordinated outfit components.

In the context of Nari18, stitching is primarily connected to its **custom-stitching service**. Nari18's main catalog focuses on unstitched suit sets, and customers can have selected fabrics converted into a finished suit through the brand's in-house tailoring service.

## Stitched Suit vs Unstitched Suit

The main difference is the condition of the garment when the customer receives it.

### Unstitched Suit

An unstitched suit is supplied as fabric rather than as a finished garment. A typical set contains:

- Kurta fabric
- Bottom fabric
- Dupatta

The customer or tailor then cuts and stitches the fabric according to the desired measurements and design.

### Stitched Suit

A stitched suit has already been cut and sewn into its intended garment form.

The customer receives a garment that is ready to wear after any necessary finishing or minor adjustments.

## How Nari18's Custom-Stitched Suit Works

Nari18 follows a model where the customer can purchase an unstitched suit and have it custom stitched by the brand's in-house tailoring team.

The basic process is:

1. Select an unstitched Nari18 suit.
2. Place the order.
3. Choose or request custom stitching where available.
4. Provide the required measurements.
5. Discuss preferred design details with the Nari18 team.
6. Nari18's tailoring team stitches the garment.
7. The finished, made-to-measure suit is delivered to the customer.

Nari18 states that customers can contact its design team through WhatsApp after ordering to discuss measurements, neckline, sleeve style, and fit preferences.

## What Makes a Stitched Suit Different?

A stitched suit provides a finished garment rather than fabric that still requires tailoring.

Important characteristics include:

- Finished garment construction
- Defined garment shape
- Selected neckline
- Selected sleeve construction
- Defined kurta length
- Defined bottom style
- Completed stitching and finishing
- Ready-to-wear convenience

With Nari18's custom-stitching service, the garment can be made according to the customer's measurements rather than relying only on standard ready-made sizing.

## Custom-Stitched Does Not Mean Standard Ready-to-Wear

A useful distinction is between **custom-stitched** and **standard ready-to-wear** suits.

### Custom-Stitched

A custom-stitched suit is made after the customer's measurements and design preferences are provided.

For example, the customer may specify:

- Bust measurement
- Waist measurement
- Hip measurement
- Shoulder width
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Bottom inseam
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Overall silhouette

Nari18's custom-stitching guidance specifically recommends these types of measurements and design decisions when creating a made-to-measure suit.

### Standard Ready-to-Wear

A conventional ready-to-wear suit is manufactured in predetermined sizes and is already stitched before purchase.

The customer generally selects the closest available size instead of providing a complete set of individual measurements.

## Common Parts of a Stitched Suit

A completed stitched suit can contain several coordinated garment elements.

### Kurta

The kurta is the main upper garment.

Its construction can include:

- Neckline
- Front and back panels
- Sleeves
- Side seams
- Hemline
- Decorative borders
- Embroidery or print placement

### Bottom

The bottom portion can be tailored in different styles depending on the selected design.

Possible styles include:

- Straight-leg pants
- Salwar
- Churidar
- Palazzo
- Sharara
- Wide-leg pants

The exact style depends on the fabric, available material, and customer's requested design.

### Dupatta

The dupatta generally remains a separate coordinated component of the suit.

It may feature:

- Printed designs
- Embroidery
- Zari
- Borders
- Tassels
- Decorative finishing
- Contrast colour or fabric

The exact dupatta design depends on the original Nari18 suit set.

## Why Choose a Stitched Suit?

A stitched suit is useful for customers who want the fabric and design choice of an unstitched suit without having to arrange separate tailoring.

Benefits can include:

- Finished garment
- Greater convenience
- Personalized measurements when custom stitched
- Preferred neckline
- Preferred sleeve style
- Preferred silhouette
- Coordinated outfit
- Professional tailoring support

For Nari18 customers, the additional advantage is that fabric selection and tailoring can be handled through the same boutique/service experience.

## Important Consideration: Custom-Stitched Orders

Nari18 states that **custom-stitched orders are made-to-measure and are not eligible for exchange**.

Customers who are uncertain about measurements are therefore encouraged to seek sizing guidance before the stitching process.

This is particularly important because a custom-stitched garment is produced according to the customer's supplied measurements and requested design.

## RAG Usage Notes

- Distinguish it from an unstitched suit, which is supplied as fabric.

- Do not describe every Nari18 product as a pre-stitched ready-to-wear suit.

- Explain the difference between custom-stitched and standard ready-to-wear garments.
- Do not assume standard S/M/L sizing for Nari18 custom-stitched orders.
- Mention that Nari18 custom stitching uses customer measurements and design preferences.
- For exchange questions, remember that Nari18 states custom-stitched orders are not eligible for exchange.
- Use the current Nari18 product page for exact fabric, colour, availability, pricing, and product-specific stitching information.

## Key Takeaway

A **stitched suit is a completed ethnic outfit**, whereas an **unstitched suit is fabric that still needs to be tailored**.

At Nari18, the stitched-suit experience is primarily delivered through its custom-stitching service: the customer selects an unstitched suit, provides measurements and design preferences, and Nari18's in-house tailoring team converts the fabric into a personalized finished garment.

This gives customers the convenience of a finished suit while retaining much of the personalization associated with unstitched ethnic wear.
