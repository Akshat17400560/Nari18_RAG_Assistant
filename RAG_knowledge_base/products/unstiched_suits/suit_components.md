# Nari18 Suit Components

## 1. Overview

A typical Nari18 unstitched suit set consists of three primary components:

1. **Kurta fabric**
2. **Bottom fabric**
3. **Dupatta**

The components are provided as fabric and are intended to be stitched into a complete suit. The exact fabric, design, length, and detailing can vary by product.

## 2. Kurta Fabric

The kurta fabric forms the main part of the suit and determines much of the garment's appearance, drape, and overall character.

Depending on the product, the kurta may feature:

- Printed designs
- Floral or geometric motifs
- Embroidery
- Zari work
- Chikankari
- Decorative borders
- Embroidered necklines or panels

Nari18 products use fabrics such as Silk, Organza, Georgette, Cotton, Chanderi, Tissue, Muslin, and other catalog-listed materials.

## 3. Bottom Fabric

The bottom component is supplied separately for tailoring into trousers, pants, churidar, salwar, or another suitable bottom style.

Depending on the product, the bottom may be:

- Matching the kurta
- Coordinated in colour
- Made from a complementary fabric
- Designed for a particular silhouette

For example, Nari18 product listings may specify cotton silk shantoon, crape, santoon, or other coordinated bottom fabrics.

## 4. Dupatta

The dupatta is the third major component of a traditional unstitched suit set.

Nari18 dupattas may include:

- Printed designs
- Floral motifs
- Embroidery
- Zari borders
- Contrast colours
- Tassel detailing
- Decorative edging

The dupatta fabric can differ from the kurta fabric. For example, a suit may combine an organza kurta with an organza/silk-blend dupatta or a silk-blend kurta with a contrasting decorative dupatta.

## 5. Typical Three-Piece Structure

| Component | Purpose |
| Kurta fabric | Used to create the main upper garment |
| Bottom fabric | Used to create pants, salwar, churidar, or another bottom |
| Dupatta | Completes the traditional suit ensemble |

Nari18's buying guide describes a complete unstitched suit as a three-piece set, although the exact specifications must be verified for each product.

## 6. Component Details Can Vary

Not every Nari18 suit uses identical materials for all three components.

For example:

- Kurta may be Silk while the bottom is Cotton Silk.
- Kurta may be Organza while the dupatta is Organza or a silk blend.
- Kurta and dupatta may share a fabric but have different embroidery or print details.
- Bottom fabric may be selected for comfort and structure rather than being identical to the kurta.

Therefore, customers should check the **Fabric Details** section of the individual product page before ordering.

## 7. Customization

After purchasing an eligible unstitched suit, Nari18 can provide custom stitching according to the available tailoring service.

Customers may customize aspects such as:

- Measurements
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Silhouette
- Bottom styling

This allows the three fabric components to be transformed into a personalized suit.
