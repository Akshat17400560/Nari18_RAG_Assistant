# Nari18 Unstitched Suits Overview

## 1. Overview

Nari18 specializes in **premium unstitched suit sets** designed around Indian fabrics, traditional craftsmanship, and personalized tailoring.

The unstitched format allows customers to select the fabric and have the final garment tailored according to their measurements and preferred styling, where custom stitching is available.

## 2. What Is an Unstitched Suit?

An unstitched suit is supplied as fabric rather than as a finished, ready-to-wear garment.

Depending on the product, a suit set may include fabric components intended for:

- Kurta
- Bottom
- Dupatta

The exact components, fabric lengths, and design details should always be verified from the individual product listing.

## 3. Nari18 Unstitched Suit Collection

Nari18 currently promotes unstitched collections across several fabric categories, including:

- Silk

The live catalog also contains products with handcrafted embroidery, printed designs, handwork, and other decorative details.

## 4. Occasion-Based Selection

Nari18's unstitched suits can be selected according to the intended occasion.

### Everyday & Casual Wear

Cotton, Mul-Cotton, Muslin, and Linen are suitable options for comfortable everyday or summer-oriented ethnic wear.

### Office & Daytime Wear

Linen, Cotton, Muslin, and selected Chanderi designs can work well for polished daytime and office styling.

### Festive Wear

Chanderi, Georgette, Organza, and Silk provide options for festive dressing.

### Weddings & Special Occasions

Silk, Tissue, embroidered pieces, and more elaborate designs can be considered for weddings and other formal celebrations.

These are general fabric and occasion guidelines; the individual product should be checked for its actual design and suitability.

## 5. Custom Stitching

Custom stitching is available on many Nari18 unstitched suits.

Customers can provide measurements and discuss preferences such as:

- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Sleeve length
- Kurta length
- Fit
- Silhouette
- Other tailoring requirements

Nari18's in-house tailoring process can be coordinated through the brand after ordering.

## 6. Why Choose an Unstitched Suit?

The unstitched format provides greater flexibility than a fixed ready-to-wear garment.

Customers can have more control over:

- Garment measurements
- Silhouette
- Neckline
- Sleeve design
- Garment length
- Overall styling

It can therefore be useful for customers who prefer personalized fits or have specific styling requirements.
