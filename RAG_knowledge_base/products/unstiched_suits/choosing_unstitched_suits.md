# Nari18 Choosing Unstitched Suits

## Overview

Choosing an unstitched suit is primarily a decision about **fabric, occasion, comfort, design, customization, and available tailoring options**. Unlike a ready-to-wear suit, an unstitched suit provides fabric that can be cut and stitched according to the wearer's measurements and preferred design.

Nari18's catalog focuses on premium unstitched suit sets across fabrics such as Silk, Chanderi, Organza, Georgette, Cotton, Muslin, Linen, Tissue, Chiffon, Crepe, and Mul-Cotton. The website also offers custom stitching on many products.

The right choice should therefore begin with the intended use and fabric, followed by design, colour, measurements, and stitching preferences.

## 1. Start With the Occasion

The occasion is one of the most useful ways to narrow down an unstitched suit.

| Occasion | Suitable Fabric Choices | Typical Style Direction |
| Wedding guest | Chanderi, Silk | Elegant, festive, refined |
| Wedding or family celebration | Silk, Tissue | Rich, luxurious, statement look |
| Diwali and festive events | Chanderi, Georgette, Silk | Traditional festive styling |
| Office or professional wear | Linen, Cotton | Comfortable, polished, structured |
| Everyday wear | Cotton, Muslin | Lightweight and comfortable |
| Summer ethnic wear | Mul-Cotton, Muslin, Cotton | Breathable and easy to wear |
| Evening events | Organza, Georgette | Dressier, statement appearance |
| Mehendi/Haldi | Chiffon, Georgette | Flowing and lighter festive styling |

These are general selection guidelines. The actual suitability of a product depends on its fabric composition, embroidery, lining requirements, colour, and design.

## 2. Choose the Fabric Based on the Desired Look

### Silk

Silk is suited to customers looking for a rich and formal ethnic appearance. Nari18 positions silk suits for weddings, celebrations, festive occasions, and elevated styling.

Choose Silk when the priority is:

- Rich festive appearance
- Weddings and special occasions
- Elegant drape
- Traditional luxury
- More formal ethnic styling

Nari18's current Silk collection includes multiple designs and colours, with custom stitching available on the collection.

### Chanderi

Chanderi is a strong choice when the customer wants a lightweight fabric with a refined festive appearance.

Choose Chanderi when the priority is:

- Wedding-guest dressing
- Daytime celebrations
- Lightweight festive wear
- Elegant traditional styling
- A balance between comfort and occasion wear

### Georgette

Georgette is suitable for customers looking for a flowing, dressier ethnic appearance.

Choose Georgette for:

- Festive occasions
- Evening events
- Mehendi and similar celebrations
- Flowing silhouettes
- Embroidered or decorative designs

### Organza

Organza can provide a more structured and statement-oriented appearance.

Choose Organza when the priority is:

- Evening wear
- Party wear
- Dressier ethnic outfits
- Structured silhouettes
- A more noticeable festive look

### Cotton

Cotton is practical for customers who prioritize comfort and everyday usability.

Choose Cotton for:

- Daily wear
- Office wear
- Family outings
- Warm-weather dressing
- Comfortable ethnic styling

### Muslin and Mul-Cotton

Muslin and Mul-Cotton are particularly useful when lightweight comfort is important.

Choose these fabrics for:

- Summer wear
- Everyday use
- Casual occasions
- Breathable ethnic outfits
- Long periods of wear

### Linen

Linen is a suitable option for customers who prefer natural texture, comfort, and understated sophistication. Nari18 specifically presents its linen collection for summer wear, workwear, travel, casual gatherings, and everyday elegance.

### Tissue

Tissue is better suited to customers seeking a more luxurious and occasion-focused appearance, particularly for weddings and celebrations.

### Chiffon

Chiffon can work well for lighter festive looks and flowing silhouettes. Nari18's buying guide specifically recommends it for occasions such as Mehendi and Haldi when used in suitable colours and designs.

## 3. Consider Comfort and Climate

Fabric selection should also reflect the weather and duration of wear.

For hot Indian summers, lightweight fabrics such as **Mul-Cotton, Muslin, Cotton, and Linen** are generally better choices. For festive summer occasions, Chanderi can provide a more dressed-up alternative.

For cooler seasons or grander occasions, customers can consider fabrics such as **Silk and Tissue**, depending on the desired look.

Comfort should also be evaluated against the complete outfit, including lining, embroidery, silhouette, and the type of bottom chosen.

## 4. Choose the Design According to Personal Style

After selecting the fabric, consider the visual character of the suit.

Important design elements include:

- Embroidery
- Handwork
- Chikankari
- Block printing
- Floral or geometric prints
- Zari work
- Decorative borders
- Contrast dupattas
- Tassels and finishing details
- Neckline design
- Sleeve style
- Overall silhouette

For example, a customer looking for understated daily wear may prefer a simpler Cotton, Muslin, or Linen design, while someone shopping for a wedding may prefer Silk, Tissue, Chanderi, or embellished Organza.

## 5. Select the Colour Carefully

Colour should be selected according to the occasion, personal preference, season, and existing wardrobe.

Nari18's catalog includes a wide range of colour families, including neutrals, creams, black, blue, pink, green, mustard, beige, and other seasonal and designer shades.

When shopping online, customers should use the product images and listed colour/variant information rather than relying only on the product name.

Colour appearance can vary between screens and real fabric, so product photography should be considered an indication rather than an exact colour guarantee.

## 6. Check What Is Included in the Set

Before purchasing, verify the product description and confirm the components supplied with that particular product.

A typical unstitched suit set consists of:

1. Kurta/top fabric
2. Bottom fabric
3. Dupatta

Customers should also check:

- Fabric used for each component
- Approximate fabric measurements
- Embroidery or print placement
- Dupatta design
- Borders or embellishments
- Whether lining is included
- Any product-specific exclusions

Nari18 advises customers to verify that all three components are included because product compositions can vary.

## 7. Think About the Final Silhouette Before Ordering

An important advantage of an unstitched suit is that the final garment does not have to follow a standard ready-made shape.

Common silhouettes include:

- Straight-cut kurta with straight-leg bottom
- A-line kurta with churidar
- Flared Anarkali with palazzo
- Short kurta with sharara
- Short kurta with wider-leg pants

The preferred silhouette should be considered before stitching because it affects fabric usage and cutting.

## 8. Plan Measurements for Custom Stitching

If custom stitching is selected, accurate measurements are essential.

Important measurements can include:

- Bust
- Waist
- Hip
- Shoulder width
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Bottom inseam

Nari18's custom-stitching guidance recommends taking measurements while wearing the undergarment normally worn with the suit and avoiding measurement over bulky clothing.

Customers should also communicate their preferred:

- Neckline
- Sleeve length
- Sleeve style
- Kurta length
- Silhouette
- Bottom style
- Other tailoring preferences

Nari18 provides measurement guidance for customers using its custom-stitching service.

## 9. Check the Product Listing Before Buying

When choosing an unstitched suit online, customers should review the product page carefully.

Check:

- Exact fabric name
- Colour
- Product images
- Embroidery or printing technique
- Components included
- Fabric measurements
- Current price
- Discount, if displayed
- Availability
- Reviews and ratings
- Custom-stitching availability
- Return/exchange conditions

Nari18's catalog allows customers to browse products by fabric and collection, while individual product pages provide product-specific information.

## 10. Consider Your Budget

Nari18's current catalog contains products across multiple price levels.

The catalog currently provides these broad price filters:

- Under ₹5,000
- ₹5,000–₹10,000
- Above ₹10,000

Prices and discounts are dynamic and should be checked on the individual product page before purchase.

Customers should consider the total expected cost, including any applicable custom-stitching or other service charges, rather than evaluating the fabric price alone.

## 11. Choose Between Convenience and Customization

An unstitched suit is particularly useful for customers who value personalization.

It allows the customer to influence:

- Fit
- Length
- Neckline
- Sleeves
- Silhouette
- Overall styling

Nari18 combines its unstitched suit collection with custom stitching on many products, allowing customers to order fabric and have the outfit tailored according to provided measurements.

For customers who do not want customization or tailoring, a ready-to-wear stitched garment may be more convenient.

## 12. Simple Nari18 Selection Framework

A customer can use the following sequence when choosing a suit:

**Step 1 — Identify the occasion**  
Wedding, festive event, office, everyday wear, summer outing, or evening event.

**Step 2 — Select the fabric**  
Choose Silk, Chanderi, Georgette, Organza, Cotton, Muslin, Linen, Tissue, Chiffon, or another suitable fabric.

**Step 3 — Select the design**  
Consider embroidery, print, handwork, borders, contrast elements, and overall style.

**Step 4 — Select the colour**  
Choose according to personal preference, occasion, season, and wardrobe.

**Step 5 — Verify the components**  
Confirm kurta fabric, bottom fabric, and dupatta details.

**Step 6 — Decide the silhouette**  
Select straight, A-line, Anarkali, sharara-oriented, or another preferred shape.

**Step 7 — Confirm measurements**  
Prepare accurate measurements if custom stitching is required.

**Step 8 — Check availability and price**  
Use the current product page because inventory, pricing, and discounts can change.

**Step 9 — Review stitching and policy information**  
Confirm whether custom stitching is available and understand the applicable exchange/return conditions.

## RAG Usage Notes

When answering customer questions about choosing an unstitched suit:

- Recommend fabric based on the customer's **occasion, comfort requirement, climate, and desired appearance**.
- Do not claim that one fabric is universally best.
- Use product-specific information when recommending an actual Nari18 product.
- Verify current price and availability from the current catalog/product page.
- Do not assume every product has the same components, fabric composition, or stitching availability.
- For custom stitching questions, confirm that stitching is offered for the specific product.
- When discussing fit, explain that unstitched suits are tailored rather than sold in standard ready-to-wear sizes.
- Do not invent fabric properties, measurements, discounts, stock levels, or product details.
- If a customer is unsure between two fabrics, compare them according to the customer's occasion, weather, comfort, drape, and desired formality.

## Key Takeaway

The best Nari18 unstitched suit is determined by matching **fabric + occasion + comfort + design + colour + desired fit**.

For formal and grand occasions, Silk and Tissue can provide a richer appearance. Chanderi and Georgette are useful for festive elegance, while Cotton, Muslin, Mul-Cotton, and Linen are strong choices for comfortable everyday or summer wear. Organza and Chiffon can be considered for evening and party-oriented styling.

The main advantage of choosing an unstitched suit is the ability to have the final garment shaped around the customer's measurements and preferred style through tailoring.
