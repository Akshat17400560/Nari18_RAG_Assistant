# Unstitched vs Stitched Suits

## 1. Overview

Nari18 primarily specializes in **unstitched suit sets**, with custom stitching available on many products.

The main difference is that an unstitched suit is purchased as fabric and can be tailored according to the customer's measurements and preferences, while a stitched suit is already constructed and generally follows predetermined sizing.

## 2. Unstitched Suits

An unstitched suit provides the fabric components that are later tailored into the desired garment.

### Advantages

- Personalized measurements
- Greater control over fit
- Freedom to choose the silhouette
- Choice of neckline and sleeve style
- Flexibility in garment length
- More control over the final design
- Suitable for customers with specific fitting requirements

Nari18 supports custom stitching through its in-house tailoring team for eligible products. Customers can discuss measurements, neckline, sleeves, fit, and styling requirements with the team.

## 3. Stitched Suits

A stitched suit is already constructed and can generally be worn with little or no additional tailoring.

### Advantages

- Faster to wear
- More convenient
- No need to find a tailor
- Easier for customers who prefer ready-made clothing

However, the customer has less control over the final construction compared with an unstitched suit.

## 4. Quick Comparison

| Feature | Unstitched Suit | Stitched Suit |
| Garment condition | Fabric to be tailored | Ready-made |
| Fit | Can be customized | Based on available size |
| Silhouette | Customer can choose | Predetermined |
| Neckline | Can be customized | Usually predetermined |
| Sleeves | Can be customized | Usually predetermined |
| Tailoring required | Yes | Usually no |
| Convenience | Requires tailoring | Quick to wear |
| Personalization | High | Limited |

## 5. Why Choose Nari18 Unstitched Suits?

Nari18's unstitched model allows customers to combine **premium fabrics with personalized tailoring**.

The brand's collection includes fabrics such as Silk, Chanderi, Organza, Georgette, Cotton, Linen, Muslin, and Tissue. These fabrics can serve as the foundation for different styles and occasions.

## 6. Custom Stitching at Nari18

For eligible products, Nari18's in-house tailoring service can accommodate preferences such as:

- Bust, waist, and hip measurements
- Shoulder width
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Bottom inseam
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Silhouette

Customers can contact Nari18 through WhatsApp after ordering to discuss their requirements.

## 7. Which Should a Customer Choose?

### Choose Unstitched if you:

- Want a personalized fit
- Have specific measurements
- Prefer a particular neckline or sleeve style
- Want control over the final silhouette
- Enjoy customizing your ethnic wear

### Choose Stitched if you:

- Want convenience
- Need something ready to wear
- Prefer not to arrange tailoring
- Are comfortable with predetermined sizing

## 8. Important Nari18 Policy

Custom-stitched orders are **made-to-measure and are not eligible for exchange**. Customers who need sizing assistance should seek guidance before ordering.
