# Nari18 New Arrivals Products

## Overview

Nari18's New Arrivals section showcases recently added and freshly curated ethnic-wear products, with a strong focus on unstitched suit sets.

The collection is dynamic. Products can be added, removed, sold out, repriced, or moved between collections. Therefore, this document should be used as a **knowledge reference for the types and examples of products found in New Arrivals**, while the current Nari18 product page should be used for exact price, availability, variants, ratings, and other changing information.

Nari18 describes New Arrivals as **"Freshly Curated Unstitched Suit Sets"**, highlighting new handcrafted fabrics and colours for the modern Indian wardrobe.

## New Arrivals and Custom Stitching

Nari18's overall product model combines unstitched suit sets with custom stitching.

Therefore, a customer interested in a New Arrivals product should check whether **custom stitching is available for that specific product**.

- Bust
- Waist
- Hip

- Neckline
- Sleeve style

Custom-stitching availability should not be assumed for every New Arrivals product.
