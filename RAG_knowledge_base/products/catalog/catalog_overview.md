# Nari18 Catalog Overview

## 1. Catalog Purpose

The Nari18 catalog is the main product-browsing area for discovering the brand's **premium unstitched suit sets and fabric-led ethnic-wear collections**.

Customers can browse products, filter the collection, view product details, check availability, and add eligible products to cart.

## 2. Main Product Focus

The catalog primarily features:

- Unstitched suit sets
- Designer unstitched suits
- Women's ethnic suits
- Fabric-based suit collections
- Handcrafted and embroidered suit sets

Nari18 also offers custom stitching on eligible products.

## 3. Catalog Fabric Categories

The current catalog includes categories such as:

- Silk
- Organza
- Tissue
- Muslin
- Cotton
- Linen
- Georgette
- Chanderi
- Crepe
- Chiffon
- Lycra Net
- Mul-Cotton

The catalog also displays categories such as Party Wear and Handprinted Suits, although their current catalog counts may be zero.

## 4. Catalog Filters

Customers can currently refine products using:

### Fabric

Products can be filtered according to fabric category.

### Price

The catalog currently provides these price ranges:

- Under ₹5,000
- ₹5,000–₹10,000
- Above ₹10,000

Prices and product availability are dynamic and should always be retrieved from the current product listing.

## 5. Product Information

Individual catalog listings may provide:

- Product name
- Fabric/category
- Product images
- Available variants
- Price
- Discounted price
- Availability
- Customer ratings
- Add-to-cart / purchase options
- Custom-stitching availability where applicable

## 6. Current Catalog Size

The website currently markets the collection as **125+ handcrafted unstitched suit sets**, while the live catalog filter counts can change as products are added, removed, or updated.

Therefore, the RAG assistant should not treat catalog counts as permanent facts.

## 7. Custom Stitching

Custom stitching is available on many catalog products.

Where offered, customers can select tailoring options during the ordering process. For specific measurements or special tailoring requirements, customers can contact Nari18 for guidance.
