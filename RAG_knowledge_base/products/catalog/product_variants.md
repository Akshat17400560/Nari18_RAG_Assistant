# Nari18 Product Variants

## 1. Overview

Nari18 products may be offered in different variants depending on the individual product. Variants can include differences in **colour, design, fabric presentation, and availability**.

Because Nari18 primarily sells unstitched suits, product variants should be understood separately from standard ready-made clothing sizes.

## 2. Colour Variants

Some Nari18 products are available in more than one colour or colour combination.

Examples visible across the catalog include:

- Ivory / Cream
- Black
- Teal
- Blue
- Pink
- Olive Green
- Dusty Rose
- Champagne / Beige
- Other product-specific colours

The exact colours available depend on the individual product.

## 3. Design Variants

A product may also have variations based on its design or detailing, such as:

- Embroidery
- Handwork
- Printed patterns
- Hand-block printing
- Contrast dupatta
- Decorative necklines
- Traditional or contemporary motifs

The design and detailing should always be taken from the specific product listing rather than inferred from the general category.

## 4. Fabric-Based Variants

Nari18 offers products across multiple fabric categories, including:

- Silk
- Chanderi
- Organza
- Georgette
- Cotton
- Muslin
- Linen
- Tissue
- Chiffon
- Crepe
- Mul-Cotton
- Other catalog-listed fabrics

Different variants may therefore represent different fabric or design combinations.

## 5. Unstitched vs. Stitched

Nari18's primary catalog consists of **unstitched suit sets**.

An unstitched product is sold as fabric, so it does not normally have conventional ready-made sizes such as S, M, L, or XL.

The final garment can be tailored according to customer measurements when custom stitching is available.

## 6. Custom-Stitching Variants

Where custom stitching is offered, customers may provide preferences such as:

- Bust, waist, and hip measurements
- Shoulder width
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Bottom inseam
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Garment silhouette

Common silhouettes include straight-cut, A-line, Anarkali-style, and short-kurta combinations.

Custom stitching availability is **product-specific** and should not be assumed for every catalog item.

## 7. Availability of Variants

Not every variant is necessarily available at the same time.

A product or variant may be:

- Available
- Sold out
- Temporarily unavailable
- Available for purchase but without a particular tailoring option

The live product page should be treated as the source of truth for current availability.

Never invent a variant that is not shown for the specific product.

If a customer asks for a particular colour, size, design, or stitching option, verify the **current product-level data** before answering.
