# Nari18 Price Ranges

## 1. Overview

Nari18's catalog groups products into three main price ranges. These ranges help customers narrow down unstitched suits based on their budget.

## 2. Current Catalog Price Ranges

### Under ₹5,000

This range contains the largest number of products in the current catalog.

Examples include:

- Cotton suits
- Chanderi suits
- Georgette suits
- Organza suits
- Other unstitched suit sets

### ₹5,000–₹10,000

This range includes a variety of mid-range unstitched suits, including products with premium fabrics, embroidery, or additional detailing.

### Above ₹10,000

This range contains higher-priced pieces, including selected premium and embroidered suit sets.

The current catalog shows **2 products** in this range, but the number can change.

## 3. Important Pricing Rules

- Product prices can change over time.
- Discounts and sale prices may change.
- The catalog may display both the current selling price and an original/compare-at price.
- Product availability can change independently of price.
- Custom stitching charges, where applicable, should be checked at the product or checkout level.
- The price range should not be used to determine the price of a specific product without checking its current listing.
