# Nari18 Product Categories

## 1. Overview

Nari18 organizes its products primarily around **unstitched suits, ethnic wear, suit styles, and fabric-based collections**.

The website currently presents both broad shopping categories and fabric-specific collections.

## 2. Main Product Categories

The main categories currently promoted by Nari18 include:

- Unstitched Suits
- Ladies Suits
- Cotton Suits
- Georgette Suits
- Custom Stitching

These categories are designed to help customers find products based on clothing type, fabric, or service requirement.

## 3. Collection Categories

Nari18 also highlights broader collections such as:

- Designer Unstitched Suits
- Women Suits
- Organza Suits
- Ethnic Wear

These collections can overlap with fabric-based categories.

## 4. Fabric-Based Categories

The current catalog provides filters for several fabric categories:

- Silk
- Organza
- Tissue
- Muslin
- Cotton
- Linen
- Georgette
- Chanderi
- Crepe
- Chiffon
- Lycra Net
- Mul-Cotton

The catalog also currently displays Party Wear Suit and Handprinted Suits as categories, although their live product counts may change.

## 5. Category Structure

For RAG purposes, Nari18 products can be understood through three main classification levels:

**Product Type → Collection/Style → Fabric**

Example:

**Unstitched Suit → Designer Collection → Chanderi**

A single product may therefore belong to more than one relevant category.
