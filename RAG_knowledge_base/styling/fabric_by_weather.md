# Nari18 Fabric by Weather Guide

## 1. Purpose

Weather is an important factor when choosing an unstitched suit because fabric affects breathability, moisture comfort, drape, structure, layering, and overall wearability.

For Nari18 styling, the simplest weather-based framework is:

- **Hot / peak summer:** Cotton, Mul-Cotton/Mulmul, Muslin
- **Warm but polished weather:** Linen, Muslin, Cotton
- **Summer festive occasions:** Chanderi, with lighter Cotton or Muslin for more comfort
- **Mild / pleasant weather:** Chanderi, Georgette, Silk, Organza depending on occasion
- **Cool / winter weather:** Silk, Tissue, Chanderi, Georgette and other richer fabrics, with layering where appropriate
- **Humid or unpredictable weather:** Prefer breathable, easy-to-manage fabrics and avoid choosing delicate fabrics solely on appearance

Nari18's current summer guide specifically identifies **cotton, especially mulmul, linen, and muslin** as its top summer choices, while Chanderi is positioned for festive summer occasions.


# 2. Quick Weather-to-Fabric Guide

| Weather | Best Fabric Choices | Suitable Use | Main Reason |
| Extreme summer / peak heat | Mulmul, Cotton, Muslin | Daily wear, casual outings | Lightweight and breathable |
| Hot summer | Cotton, Muslin, Linen | Daily, office, brunch | Comfort with good airflow |
| Warm summer | Linen, Cotton, Muslin | Office, travel, casual | Breathability with practical styling |
| Summer festive | Chanderi, lightweight Cotton | Pooja, festive lunch, daytime events | Festive appearance without heavy winter-like fabrics |
| Mild weather | Chanderi, Georgette, Cotton, Linen | Day events, office, gatherings | Flexible balance of comfort and style |
| Pleasant evening | Georgette, Chanderi, Silk | Dinner, festive events | More polished drape |
| Cool weather | Silk, Tissue, Chanderi, Georgette | Weddings, festive occasions | Richer appearance and layering potential |

This is a styling framework rather than a fixed Nari18 product rule. Actual comfort also depends on lining, embroidery, garment construction, fit, and the specific fabric composition. Nari18's catalog currently spans multiple fabric families including Silk, Organza, Tissue, Muslins, Cotton, Linen, Georgette, Chanderi, Crepe, Chiffon and Mul-Cotton.


# 3. Hot and Peak Summer

## Best Choices

### Cotton

Cotton is one of Nari18's strongest choices for hot-weather dressing. It is positioned for daily wear, casual occasions, summer family gatherings, and office ethnic days.

For peak heat, lightweight cotton varieties are particularly useful.

### Mulmul / Mul Cotton

Mulmul, also referred to as mul cotton in Nari18's summer guide, is described as extremely lightweight and breathable. It is particularly suitable when maximum comfort is the priority.

Best for:

- Very hot days
- Daily wear
- Casual outings
- Regular ethnic wardrobe
- Relaxed summer styling

Nari18's current Mul Cotton collection describes these suits as soft, breathable, lightweight, and suitable for summer, daily wear, office wear, and relaxed traditional styling.

### Muslin

Muslin is another strong choice for hot weather. Nari18 describes it as soft, lightweight, airy and fluid, making it suitable for elevated daily wear and warm-weather occasions.

For sheer or lightweight Muslin, a cotton slip or lining may be useful where additional opacity is required.


# 4. Warm Summer and Everyday Heat

When the weather is hot but the outfit needs to look more polished, consider:

1. **Linen** — structured and sophisticated
2. **Muslin** — softer and more fluid
3. **Cotton** — practical and versatile

### Linen

Linen is particularly suitable for:

- Office wear
- Formal lunches
- Brunches
- Travel
- Smart-casual summer dressing

Nari18 describes linen as breathable with a naturally relaxed texture and polished appearance. Its current Linen collection specifically positions linen suits for summer wear, workwear, travel, casual gatherings and everyday elegance.

### Weather Styling Tip

For hot weather, relaxed silhouettes and lighter colours can improve the overall feeling of comfort. Avoid unnecessarily heavy layering when the temperature is high.


# 5. Summer Festive Weather

Summer does not mean that festive dressing has to be limited to plain cotton.

## Chanderi

Chanderi is one of Nari18's key choices for festive summer dressing.

It provides:

- Lightweight festive appearance
- Subtle shine
- Traditional character
- More structure than very soft summer fabrics
- A polished look for daytime celebrations

Nari18 specifically recommends Chanderi for festive lunches, Roka ceremonies, temple visits and summer weddings, while noting that it is lighter than pure silk.

## Recommended Summer Festive Hierarchy

**Maximum comfort:** Cotton / Muslin  
**Comfort + polish:** Chanderi  
**More formal evening look:** Georgette  
**More luxurious look:** Silk, where weather and venue permit


# 6. Mild or Pleasant Weather

When temperatures are comfortable, fabric selection can be based more strongly on occasion and desired appearance.

Suitable options include:

- Chanderi
- Georgette
- Cotton
- Linen
- Muslin
- Silk for formal occasions

This is the most flexible weather category because both lightweight everyday fabrics and richer festive fabrics can work.

For daytime dressing, Chanderi, Linen, Cotton and Muslin can provide a balanced look. For evenings, Georgette, Silk or Organza can create a more formal appearance.


# 7. Cool Weather and Winter

Nari18's fabric guides position richer fabrics such as **Silk and Tissue** toward weddings, bridal events and major celebrations. These fabrics can be especially useful when the weather is cooler and the styling goal is formal, rich and festive.

## Silk

Silk is particularly appropriate for:

- Winter weddings
- Sangeet
- Reception
- Diwali
- Bridal trousseau
- Formal evening events

It provides a richer visual presence than lightweight summer fabrics.

## Tissue / Tissue Silk

Tissue is particularly suitable when a glamorous festive appearance is desired.

Nari18's fabric guidance positions Tissue for:

- Weddings
- Karwa Chauth
- Poojas
- Evening celebrations

Its metallic or shimmering appearance makes it especially useful for formal festive styling.

## Chanderi

Chanderi remains useful in cooler weather when a lighter festive outfit is preferred instead of a heavier Silk or Tissue look.


# 8. Winter Day vs Winter Evening

## Winter Day

For daytime winter events:

- Chanderi
- Silk
- Cotton with suitable layering
- Linen where the temperature permits

can work depending on the occasion.

## Winter Evening

For colder evenings and formal celebrations:

- Silk
- Tissue
- Organza
- Georgette
- Rich Chanderi

can create a more appropriate formal appearance.

The final choice should consider the actual venue temperature. An indoor heated venue may allow lighter fabrics, while an outdoor winter event may require additional layering.


# 9. Humid Weather

Humidity changes how comfortable a fabric feels even when the temperature is not extremely high.

### Preferred Options

**Cotton**
- Practical for everyday use
- Suitable for repeated wear
- Good choice when comfort is the priority

**Mul-Cotton**
- Lightweight
- Suitable for warm-weather daily wear

**Muslin**
- Soft and airy
- Useful for relaxed summer dressing

**Linen**
- Good for polished warm-weather outfits
- Useful for office and travel

Nari18's summer guide places Cotton, Linen and Muslin among its strongest warm-weather choices.

### Styling Principle

In humid conditions, prioritize breathability and avoid unnecessarily heavy layers or dense embellishment for daytime wear.


# 10. Weather and Fabric Weight

A useful way to think about weather is not simply "summer vs winter" but **lightness vs richness**.

### Very Lightweight

- Mulmul / Mul-Cotton
- Lightweight Cotton
- Muslin

Best for:
- Peak summer
- Daily wear
- Humid weather
- Travel

### Medium / Versatile

- Linen
- Chanderi
- Georgette
- Cotton

Best for:
- Mild weather
- Office
- Brunch
- Festive daytime events

### Rich / Formal

- Silk
- Tissue
- Organza

Best for:
- Weddings
- Receptions
- Formal evenings

# 11. Weather, Lining and Layering

Weather should influence not only the main fabric but also the construction of the finished suit.

## Summer

Consider:

- Lightweight lining where required
- Breathable cotton slips
- Relaxed silhouettes
- Minimal heavy layering
- Lightweight dupattas

Nari18 specifically recommends cotton slips or linings with Muslin where opacity is needed while keeping the outfit breathable.

## Winter

Consider:

- Appropriate lining
- Fuller silhouettes where comfortable
- Dupattas that provide additional coverage
- Layering with suitable outerwear where required

Do not assume that every unstitched set requires the same lining. The fabric, transparency, embroidery and intended silhouette should be assessed before stitching.


# 12. Weather and Dupatta Selection

The dupatta can significantly change the weather suitability of an outfit.

### Hot Weather

Prefer:

- Lightweight Cotton
- Muslin
- Chiffon
- Lightweight Georgette

### Mild Weather

Consider:

- Chanderi
- Georgette
- Cotton
- Linen

### Cooler / Formal Weather

Consider:

- Silk
- Tissue
- Organza
- Richer Chanderi

The dupatta should remain proportionate to the main fabric. A very heavy dupatta can make an otherwise lightweight summer outfit feel less comfortable.

# 15. Weather and Fabric Care

Weather suitability does not replace product-specific care instructions.

Nari18's latest care guidance emphasizes identifying the fabric and decoration first and checking the care information supplied with the product before washing, ironing or cutting. Different components of an unstitched set may respond differently to water, heat and friction.

Important principles:

- Do not assume every cotton or Muslin piece is machine washable.
- Do not assume every delicate fabric should be washed at home.
- Protect embellished fabrics from excessive moisture and friction.
- Avoid strong heat on delicate weaves or decorative surfaces.
- Keep unstitched fabric clean and completely dry.
- Store delicate fabrics away from prolonged direct light.
- Follow the product-specific care instructions whenever available.
