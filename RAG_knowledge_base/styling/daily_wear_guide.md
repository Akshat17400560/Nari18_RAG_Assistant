# Nari18 Daily Wear Guide

## 1. Overview

Nari18's everyday-wear range focuses on ethnic suits that are comfortable, breathable, practical and easy to style repeatedly. The brand specifically positions **Cotton, Muslin, Mul-Cotton and Linen** toward everyday dressing, while selected Crepe styles can also work for daily and casual wear.

Daily wear should generally balance:

- Comfort
- Breathability
- Easy movement
- Practical maintenance
- Simple styling
- Appropriate fabric weight
- Repeat-wear versatility
- A fit suitable for normal daily activities

The best daily-wear suit is not necessarily the simplest-looking one. A printed or lightly detailed suit can still be practical if the fabric, construction and care requirements suit regular use.

---

## 2. Best Nari18 Fabrics for Daily Wear

### Cotton

Cotton is Nari18's strongest general-purpose choice for everyday wear. The brand describes Cotton as practical, breathable and suitable for regular wear, casual occasions and summer dressing.

Cotton is particularly useful for:

- Everyday home-to-outing dressing
- Regular office days
- Casual meetings
- Shopping
- Family visits
- Daily errands
- Summer wear
- Casual gatherings

Nari18's current Cotton guidance also highlights printed Cotton and Mulmul Cotton as practical everyday options.

### Muslin

Nari18 describes Muslin as exceptionally soft, lightweight and breathable, making it a strong option for **daily wear, office ethnic days, brunches and casual social occasions**.

Muslin is especially useful when the customer prioritizes:

- Softness
- Lightweight comfort
- Fluid drape
- Warm-weather dressing
- Relaxed daily styling

Nari18's current storefront includes several Muslin everyday options, including Midnight Chevron Muse, Royal Teal Muse, Rustic Petals and Heritage Arch Muslin suits.

### Mul-Cotton / Mulmul

Mul-Cotton is positioned by Nari18 as a lightweight, breathable option for **summer wear, daily wear, office wear and relaxed traditional styling**.

It is particularly suitable for:

- Very warm weather
- Frequent wear
- Casual daytime outfits
- Summer travel
- Relaxed home-to-outing styling

Nari18's summer guide describes Mulmul as one of the lightest and most breathable forms of Cotton.

### Linen

Linen can work well for everyday dressing when the customer wants a slightly more polished or structured appearance. Nari18 positions Linen toward workwear, summer wear, travel and everyday elegance.

Compared with softer Muslin, Linen generally creates a more structured visual impression.

### Crepe

Nari18's current Crepe collection describes its suits as lightweight, soft-drape options suitable for **daily wear, office wear, casual occasions and easy festive dressing**.

Crepe can therefore be considered when the customer wants:

- A softer drape
- A slightly more contemporary appearance
- Comfortable casual styling
- An alternative to Cotton or Muslin

Exact fabric composition and care should always be checked on the individual product.

## 5. Daily-Wear Prints

Printed suits are especially useful for building a repeat-wear wardrobe.

Suitable directions include:

- Small floral prints
- Botanical patterns
- Paisley
- Geometric prints
- Traditional motifs
- Fine all-over patterns
- Block-inspired prints
- Muted multicolour prints

Nari18's Cotton guidance specifically highlights hand block-printed Cotton as an everyday summer option, while its current product range includes printed Muslin and Cotton styles.

For daily wear, the customer should consider the print together with the base colour and styling rather than selecting purely by motif.

---

## 6. Embroidery for Daily Wear

Embroidery can work in everyday suits, but lighter detailing generally makes the garment easier to repeat.

### Good daily-wear detailing

- Small floral embroidery
- Tonal threadwork
- Minimal neckline embroidery
- Small motifs
- Light borders
- Limited decorative panels

### More suitable for occasional wear

- Heavy zari
- Dense embroidery
- Extensive mirror work
- Large crystal embellishment
- Heavy bridal-style borders
- Highly elaborate festive motifs

The same fabric can move between daily and festive categories depending on the amount and type of embellishment.

## 7. Daily-Wear Silhouettes

### Straight-Cut Kurta

A highly versatile daily silhouette.

Benefits:

- Easy movement
- Clean appearance
- Simple to accessorize
- Works well with different bottoms
- Suitable for repeated wear

### A-Line

A-Line suits offer additional movement while retaining a polished appearance.

Useful for:

- Everyday outings
- Family visits
- Casual office settings
- Daytime gatherings

### Relaxed Kurta

A relaxed fit can be especially comfortable for home-to-outing use and warm weather.

### Short Kurta

Short kurtas can be paired with suitable pants, palazzos or other coordinated bottoms for a contemporary everyday outfit.

### Palazzo Styling

Palazzo bottoms can provide comfort and movement, particularly for casual and summer dressing.

### Anarkali

Anarkali silhouettes can certainly be worn casually, but heavily flared or heavily decorated versions generally move toward festive styling.

## 8. Daily-Wear Dupatta Styling

For regular wear, practicality matters as much as appearance.

A good everyday dupatta should ideally be:

- Lightweight
- Easy to drape
- Easy to manage
- Appropriate to the suit
- Comfortable during movement

### Simple daily drape

A basic shoulder drape is often the easiest option for:

- Shopping
- Travel
- Family visits
- Daily errands
- Casual workdays

### Printed dupatta

A printed dupatta can add visual interest to a plain or lightly detailed kurta.

### Minimal contrast

A contrasting dupatta can refresh a simple Cotton or Muslin suit without requiring heavy accessories.

## 10. Daily-Wear Footwear

Because daily suits may be worn for several hours, comfort should be a major consideration.

Suitable options include:

- Flats
- Juttis

## 11. Daily Wear by Season

### Summer

Nari18 identifies **Cotton, Linen and Muslin** among its strongest summer fabric choices, with Mulmul/Mul-Cotton particularly suited to intense heat.

Prioritize:

- Cotton
- Mul-Cotton
- Muslin
- Linen
- Light colours
- Lightweight dupattas
- Breathable silhouettes

### Monsoon

For everyday monsoon dressing:

- Prefer practical fabrics
- Choose manageable silhouettes
- Avoid unnecessarily delicate embellishments
- Consider maintenance requirements
- Check individual care instructions before washing

Nari18's care guide specifically warns that Cotton, Mul-Cotton and Muslin should not automatically be treated as machine-washable simply because they are commonly associated with daily wear. Prints, fine Muslin and embroidery can change the required care routine.

### Winter

Daily winter styling can use:

- Heavier Cotton
- Suitable Linen
- Selected Crepe
- Layer-friendly silhouettes
- Deeper colours

Silk can be incorporated for special winter occasions, but it is not Nari18's primary recommendation for routine everyday dressing.

## 14. Daily Wear and Custom Stitching

Unstitched suits can be customized to create a practical daily-wear fit.

Useful tailoring decisions include:

- Comfortable bust fit
- Comfortable waist fit
- Appropriate hip ease
- Practical kurta length
- Comfortable sleeve length
- Straight or A-Line silhouette
- Suitable bottom width
- Appropriate lining
- Easy-to-manage neckline

Nari18 promotes custom stitching through its in-house tailoring service, while individual product pages or checkout should be checked to determine whether tailoring is offered for a particular product.

For daily wear, avoid creating a garment so fitted that sitting, walking or commuting becomes uncomfortable.

## 15. Daily Wear and Fabric Care

Daily-wear suitability should include maintenance, not just appearance.

Nari18's current care guidance emphasizes that even commonly used daily fabrics can have different care requirements depending on printing, embroidery, fibre composition and finishing.

### Before stitching

Check:

- Fabric composition
- Print or embroidery
- Fabric length and width
- Care instructions
- Whether the bottom requires different care
- Whether the fabric needs special handling

### After stitching

Follow the individual product's care instructions.

Do not automatically assume:

- Every Cotton suit is machine washable
- Every Muslin suit can be washed aggressively
- Every printed suit is colourfast
- Every Linen suit should be ironed at maximum heat

Nari18 specifically recommends caution with printed, fine or embellished fabrics.

## 17. Daily Wear vs Office Wear

Daily wear and office wear overlap, but they are not identical.

| Requirement | Daily Wear | Office Wear |
|---|---|---|
| Comfort | Very high | High |
| Fabric priority | Cotton, Muslin, Mul-Cotton | Cotton, Linen, Muslin |
| Prints | Flexible | Usually more restrained |
| Embroidery | Flexible | Usually moderate |
| Silhouette | Relaxed or structured | More polished |
| Accessories | Personal preference | More restrained |
| Footwear | Maximum comfort | Comfort + polish |
| Formality | Low to moderate | Moderate to high |

A Cotton or Muslin suit can therefore work for both categories, with the difference coming largely from styling.

---

## 18. Daily Wear vs Festive Wear

Daily wear should not be confused with lightweight festive wear.

### Daily wear

- Comfortable
- Practical
- Repeatable
- Moderate detailing
- Easy accessories

### Festive wear

- Richer fabrics
- More elaborate embroidery
- More decorative dupattas
- Stronger jewellery
- More formal silhouettes

For example, Cotton can serve both daily and festive wardrobes depending on print, border and embellishment. Nari18 specifically notes Banarasi Cotton with woven metallic borders as a practical fabric with a festive edge.

## 20. Important RAG Retrieval Rules

1. **For general daily-wear questions**, prioritize Cotton, Muslin, Mul-Cotton and Linen. Nari18 explicitly connects these fabrics with everyday, office, summer or relaxed dressing.

2. **For extreme summer comfort**, Mul-Cotton/Mulmul, Cotton and Muslin are strong recommendations.

3. **For a polished everyday look**, Linen can be recommended.

4. **For soft contemporary drape**, Crepe can be considered because Nari18's current Crepe collection explicitly includes daily wear among its intended uses.

5. Do not classify every product made from a daily-wear fabric as automatically suitable for every daily situation.

6. Do not infer exact fibre composition from a product name.

7. Do not infer that every Cotton or Muslin product has identical washing instructions. Product-specific care information takes priority.

8. Do not assume that unstitched means stitching is included in the displayed product price.

9. Custom stitching should be described as available **where offered** unless the specific product or current Nari18 guidance confirms otherwise.

10. Current product prices, discounts, ratings and stock availability are dynamic. Retrieve the current product page when answering transactional questions.

11. Avoid recommending Silk or Tissue as default everyday fabrics. Nari18 positions these more strongly toward weddings, festive events and formal occasions.

12. Daily-wear suitability depends on the combination of fabric, colour, print, embellishment, silhouette and workplace/social setting.
