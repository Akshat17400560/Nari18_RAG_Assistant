# Nari18 Office Wear Guide

## 1. Overview

Nari18 positions ethnic suits as a practical option for professional and everyday workwear, with particular emphasis on **Cotton and Linen** for office dressing. Muslin and Mul-Cotton are also useful when comfort and warm-weather breathability are the priority.

A good office-wear suit should balance:

- Comfort for extended working hours
- Breathability
- A polished and professional appearance
- Appropriate colour intensity
- Practical maintenance
- A silhouette that allows movement
- Moderate rather than excessive embellishment
- Fabric weight appropriate to the season
- A fit that is comfortable without looking oversized

For Nari18's unstitched suits, custom tailoring can further adapt the garment to the wearer's measurements and preferred silhouette where stitching is offered.


## 2. Best Nari18 Fabrics for Office Wear

### Cotton — Practical Everyday Office Choice

Nari18 describes Cotton as a staple fabric for everyday ethnic wear and specifically recommends it for **office wear, casual outings and elevated daily fashion**. Cotton is breathable, versatile and available in multiple styles including printed and lightweight options.

Cotton works particularly well for:

- Regular office days
- Long working hours
- Summer office wear
- Casual Fridays
- Daily commuting
- Business-casual environments
- Offices where ethnic wear is commonly accepted

Nari18's cotton collection includes different constructions and styles, so the exact product composition and care instructions should always be checked before purchase.

### Linen — Polished and Professional

Nari18 specifically describes Linen suits as suitable for **workwear, summer wear, travel, casual gatherings and everyday elegance**. Its summer guide also recommends Linen for office ethnic days, formal lunches and travel-friendly summer wardrobes.

Linen is particularly useful when the desired office look is:

- Sophisticated
- Structured
- Minimal
- Breathable
- Professional without being overly formal

Nari18 notes that Linen naturally develops some creasing. This is part of the character of the fabric, although tailoring and pressing can influence whether the final garment appears more relaxed or crisp.

### Muslin — Comfort-Focused Workwear

Nari18 describes Muslin as lightweight, soft and airy. It is especially useful for warm weather and situations where comfort is important.

Muslin is a good choice for:

- Hot-weather offices
- Casual professional environments
- Long workdays
- Summer commuting
- Comfortable everyday ethnic styling

It generally creates a softer, more fluid appearance than Linen, making it appropriate when the office environment is professional but not highly formal.

### Mul-Cotton — Lightweight Everyday Option

Nari18's Mul-Cotton collection describes these suits as lightweight, breathable and suitable for **summer wear, daily wear, office wear and relaxed traditional styling**.

Mul-Cotton is especially useful when the priority is:

- Maximum summer comfort
- Lightweight dressing
- Regular office use
- Easy everyday styling
- Relaxed professional appearance


## 3. Office-Wear Fabric Selection

| Office Requirement | Recommended Fabric | Why |
| Regular daily office | Cotton | Breathable and versatile |
| Formal-looking office outfit | Linen | Structured and sophisticated |
| Peak summer comfort | Muslin / Mul-Cotton | Lightweight and airy |
| Summer professional wear | Linen / Cotton | Balance of polish and comfort |
| Casual office | Cotton / Muslin | Comfortable and easy to style |
| Travel + office | Linen / Cotton | Practical and polished |
| Very formal corporate environment | Linen / understated Cotton | More restrained appearance |
| Traditional but relaxed workplace | Cotton / Mul-Cotton | Comfortable ethnic styling |

This is a styling framework rather than a strict product rule. Exact suitability depends on the specific fabric, design, colour and workplace environment.


## 4. Office-Wear Colour Direction

For professional environments, understated colours generally create a more versatile wardrobe.

### Recommended colour families

- Ivory
- Off-white
- Cream
- Beige
- Taupe
- Soft grey
- Dusty blue
- Powder blue
- Muted teal
- Olive
- Soft green
- Mauve
- Dusty rose
- Muted mustard
- Deep navy
- Maroon in restrained designs

### Neutral colours

Neutral suits are particularly useful for repeat office styling because they can be paired with different dupattas, footwear and accessories.

Examples:

- Ivory kurta + printed dupatta
- Beige suit + muted contrast dupatta
- Off-white suit + dark footwear
- Soft grey suit + minimal jewellery

### Pastels

Pastels work particularly well for daytime offices and summer.

Useful choices include:

- Powder blue
- Soft pink
- Blush
- Sage or muted green
- Light lavender
- Cream
- Soft peach

### Deeper colours

Deep colours can also work for office wear when the overall design remains restrained.

Suitable directions include:

- Navy
- Deep teal
- Wine
- Maroon
- Forest green
- Charcoal

The colour should be considered together with embroidery, print scale and accessories. A dark colour with heavy embellishment can become more festive than professional.


## 5. Office-Wear Prints

Prints can make an ethnic office outfit more interesting without requiring heavy embellishment.

Good office directions include:

- Small floral prints
- Subtle geometric prints
- Traditional motifs in moderate scale
- Block-inspired prints
- Paisley patterns in restrained colours
- Tonal prints
- Fine all-over patterns

Large, highly dramatic prints can still work in creative or relaxed workplaces, but they are less universally suitable for conservative professional environments.

For example, Nari18 currently has a Warm Mustard-Beige Printed Cotton Suit described as suitable for office wear, casual outings and everyday festive styling. Its design combines an all-over floral/paisley-inspired print with a contrasting maroon dupatta.


## 6. Office-Wear Embroidery and Embellishment

Office wear generally benefits from **controlled detailing** rather than maximum embellishment.

### Suitable detailing

- Small embroidered motifs
- Tonal embroidery
- Minimal neckline work
- Fine borders
- Subtle threadwork
- Small floral motifs
- Simple decorative panels

### Use more carefully

- Heavy zari
- Large crystal or sequin work
- Dense embroidery
- Large metallic borders
- Heavy bridal-style embellishment
- Dramatic partywear detailing

The goal is not to eliminate decoration. Instead, the decorative elements should support a polished professional appearance.


## 7. Office-Wear Silhouettes

A comfortable, clean silhouette is generally the safest choice for work.

### Straight-cut Kurta

One of the most versatile office silhouettes.

Advantages:

- Clean appearance
- Easy movement
- Professional visual structure
- Works with trousers, pants or traditional bottoms
- Suitable for regular office use

### A-Line

A-Line suits can provide slightly more movement while maintaining a polished appearance.

Suitable for:

- Regular office
- Client meetings
- Daytime events
- Semi-formal workplaces

### Short Kurta

A short kurta can work well with suitable trousers or coordinated bottoms in contemporary office environments.

### Palazzo-Based Styling

Palazzo bottoms can be comfortable for long working hours, but the overall outfit should remain structured enough for the workplace.

### Very Flared Silhouettes

Heavily flared Anarkali-style silhouettes are generally more festive than everyday office wear. They can be appropriate for workplaces with a more traditional dress culture or for special office celebrations.


## 8. Office-Wear Dupatta Styling

The dupatta can significantly change the formality of a suit.

For everyday office wear:

- Use a lightweight dupatta
- Prefer controlled prints
- Keep borders moderate
- Avoid excessive metallic detailing
- Use a clean shoulder drape
- Avoid styling that requires constant adjustment

### Practical office drapes

A simple shoulder drape or balanced front drape is usually easier to manage during:

- Desk work
- Meetings
- Commuting
- Presentations
- Client interactions

For very busy workdays, a lightweight and manageable dupatta may be more practical than a highly embellished or delicate one.


## 9. Office-Wear Jewellery

Jewellery should complement the outfit rather than dominate it.

### Everyday office

Suitable choices include:

- Small earrings
- Studs
- Small hoops
- Simple pendant
- Minimal bracelet
- Simple watch

### Client meetings or presentations

A slightly more polished combination can include:

- Elegant earrings
- Simple necklace
- Watch
- One understated bracelet

### Avoid over-accessorising

A highly embellished suit combined with heavy jewellery, dramatic footwear and a statement dupatta can quickly shift the look from office wear to festive wear.


## 10. Office-Wear Footwear

Comfort and practicality are important because office outfits are often worn for several hours.

Suitable footwear directions include:

- Flats
- Minimal ethnic sandals
- Low block heels
- Simple loafers
- Comfortable closed footwear
- Minimal juttis

For commuting-heavy schedules, comfortable footwear is generally more practical than very high heels.


## 11. Office Styling by Environment

### Conservative Corporate Office

Prioritise:

- Linen or understated Cotton
- Neutral or muted colours
- Straight-cut silhouettes
- Minimal prints
- Limited embroidery
- Simple jewellery
- Clean footwear

### Business-Casual Office

More flexibility is possible.

Consider:

- Cotton
- Linen
- Muslin
- Small prints
- Soft colour contrasts
- A-Line silhouettes
- Contemporary bottoms
- Moderate jewellery

### Creative Workplace

You can experiment more with:

- Colour
- Prints
- Contemporary silhouettes
- Textured fabrics
- Statement dupattas
- More distinctive accessories

The outfit should still remain comfortable and appropriate for the work environment.

### Traditional or Ethnic-Friendly Workplace

Traditional cotton, Mul-Cotton, Muslin and Linen suits can be particularly effective. More colour and moderate ethnic detailing can be incorporated while maintaining a professional silhouette.


## 12. Office Wear by Season

### Summer

Prioritise:

- Cotton
- Mul-Cotton
- Muslin
- Linen
- Light colours
- Breathable silhouettes
- Lightweight dupattas

Nari18's summer guide specifically identifies Linen for office ethnic days and Cotton/Muslin as practical warm-weather options.

### Monsoon

Consider:

- Practical Cotton
- Lightweight fabrics
- Easy-care designs
- Less delicate embellishment
- Manageable dupattas

Avoid choosing particularly delicate pieces for days involving significant rain exposure or difficult commuting.

### Winter

Office styling can shift toward:

- Heavier Cotton
- Linen blends where applicable
- Silk for selected formal office occasions
- Deeper colours
- Layering with suitable outerwear

Silk is positioned by Nari18 primarily toward festive and formal occasions, so it is generally better suited to special office events than routine daily office wear.


## 13. Office Wear for Meetings

For important meetings, presentations or client interactions, the outfit should look deliberate and polished.

Recommended formula:

**Structured fabric + restrained colour + clean silhouette + minimal accessories**

Examples:

- Ivory Linen + simple jewellery
- Navy Cotton + minimal printed dupatta
- Beige Linen + muted contrast
- Dusty blue Cotton + clean straight-cut silhouette
- Olive Linen + understated accessories

Avoid combining too many statement elements at once.


## 14. Office Wear for Formal Lunches

Linen is particularly relevant for formal daytime dressing. Nari18's summer guide specifically identifies Linen for **formal lunches**, as well as office wear and travel-friendly wardrobes.

A suitable formula is:

- Linen suit
- Soft or neutral colour
- Structured kurta
- Minimal jewellery
- Comfortable polished footwear
- Lightweight dupatta

For a slightly more festive professional lunch, restrained Chanderi or a lightly detailed Cotton suit may also work, depending on the environment.


## 15. Office Wear for Summer Commuting

When commuting is significant, prioritise practicality over decoration.

Recommended:

1. Cotton or Mul-Cotton for everyday comfort
2. Muslin for very hot conditions
3. Linen for a more structured professional appearance
4. Lightweight dupatta
5. Comfortable footwear
6. Easy-to-manage silhouette

Nari18 specifically positions Mul-Cotton for summer, daily and office wear, while Muslin is positioned around lightweight comfort.


## 16. Custom Stitching for Office Wear

Unstitched suits provide an opportunity to create a workwear silhouette around individual measurements.

Useful tailoring decisions include:

- Comfortable bust and waist fit
- Appropriate kurta length
- Practical sleeve length
- Straight or A-Line silhouette
- Functional neckline
- Comfortable shoulder fit
- Suitable bottom width
- Appropriate lining where required

Nari18 states that tailoring can be selected where offered on individual products, while bespoke measurement or styling questions can be discussed with the team.

For office clothing, the best fit is generally one that allows comfortable sitting, walking, commuting and arm movement rather than an excessively tight or highly structured fit.


## 17. Office-Wear Fabric and Care Considerations

The fabric should be selected with maintenance requirements in mind.

Nari18's current care guidance emphasizes that fabric type, prints, embroidery and finishing can affect how an unstitched piece should be handled. Customers should check product-specific care instructions rather than assuming all pieces made from the same broad fabric category have identical care requirements.

For frequently worn office suits:

- Check washing instructions before purchase.
- Consider how often the garment will be worn.
- Avoid selecting extremely delicate fabrics for routine commuting if maintenance would be inconvenient.
- Check whether embroidery or prints require special handling.
- Follow the exact care instructions for the individual product.
