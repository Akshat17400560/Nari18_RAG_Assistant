# Nari18 Party Wear Guide

## 1. Overview

Nari18's party-wear styling is centered around fabrics and silhouettes that create a more polished, elegant and visually distinctive appearance than everyday ethnic wear.

For party and evening dressing, Nari18 particularly positions:

- Organza
- Georgette
- Chiffon
- Silk
- Tissue Silk
- Selected Chanderi
- Selected Crepe

Organza is described by Nari18 as a statement fabric for evening and party wear, while Georgette is positioned toward festive occasions, parties, family functions and elegant evening looks. Tissue Silk is more suited to grand and highly festive events.

Party wear should generally balance:

- Fabric richness
- Elegant drape
- Appropriate embellishment
- Colour impact
- Silhouette
- Comfort
- Jewellery
- Footwear
- Dupatta styling

The objective is to create a noticeable occasion-ready look without necessarily making every party outfit heavily embellished.


## 2. Best Nari18 Fabrics for Party Wear

### Organza

Organza is one of Nari18's strongest party-wear choices.

Nari18 describes Organza as lightweight, graceful and festive, with delicate textures and premium detailing. Its crisp body and ability to hold shape make it particularly suitable for evening and party silhouettes.

Organza works particularly well for:

- Cocktail-style ethnic parties
- Evening parties
- Receptions
- Formal dinners
- Wedding functions
- Special celebrations

The structured nature of Organza makes it suitable for:

- A-Line silhouettes
- Flared silhouettes
- Structured kurtas
- Statement sleeves
- Layered styling where the fabric permits

Because Organza can be sheer and delicate, lining and finishing should be considered during custom stitching.


### Georgette

Nari18 positions Georgette as a flowy, lightweight and graceful fabric suitable for:

- Festive occasions
- Parties
- Family functions
- Elegant evening looks
- Weddings

Its softer drape creates a different visual effect from Organza. While Organza provides more structure, Georgette generally provides more fluid movement.

Georgette is particularly useful when the customer wants:

- Movement
- A softer silhouette
- Flowing dupattas
- Draped styling
- Feminine evening wear


### Chiffon

Nari18 includes Chiffon among fabrics suitable for evening and party-oriented dressing. Its lightweight character can work especially well for:

- Evening gatherings
- Summer parties
- Mehendi-style celebrations
- Festive lunches
- Lightweight occasion wear

Chiffon can create a softer, more fluid appearance than Organza.

Because Chiffon is delicate, product-specific care and tailoring requirements should be checked before stitching.


### Silk

Nari18's Silk collection is positioned around rich festive styling and celebrations. It is particularly appropriate when a party has a more formal ethnic character.

Silk can work well for:

- Formal parties
- Wedding celebrations
- Sangeet
- Festive dinners
- Premium evening events
- Special family functions

Silk creates a richer appearance than everyday Cotton or Muslin, making it more appropriate when the event requires elevated dressing.


### Tissue Silk

Tissue Silk is particularly suitable for high-impact party dressing.

Nari18 describes Tissue as having a shimmering or metallic finish and positions it toward weddings, receptions, grand events and other high-formality occasions.

Tissue is useful when the customer wants:

- Shine
- Glamour
- A statement appearance
- Metallic visual interest
- A grand evening look

Because Tissue can already provide significant visual impact through its surface, additional jewellery and embellishment should be balanced carefully.


### Chanderi

Chanderi is more versatile than some of the dedicated evening fabrics.

Nari18 positions Chanderi primarily toward:

- Weddings
- Poojas
- Festive events
- Daytime celebrations

It can therefore work for daytime parties or elegant family gatherings where a lighter, more refined appearance is preferred.


### Crepe

Nari18's current Crepe collection can also support party and casual-evening styling. Crepe can be considered when the customer wants a softer drape and a contemporary appearance without the structured effect of Organza.

| Georgette | Flowing, graceful | Fluid | Very high | Parties and evening functions |
| Chiffon | Lightweight, soft | Fluid | High | Summer/evening parties |
| Silk | Rich, luxurious | Moderate to fluid depending on type | Very high | Formal celebrations |

### Daytime Party

Prefer:

- Chanderi
- Light Georgette
- Chiffon
- Lightweight Cotton with elevated detailing
- Soft Organza

Colours can include:

- Ivory
- Blush
- Powder blue
- Soft pink
- Sage
- Peach
- Lavender
- Light beige

Keep jewellery and embellishment moderate.

### Evening Party

Evening events allow stronger visual impact.

Consider:

- Organza
- Georgette
- Silk
- Tissue
- Chiffon

Suitable colour directions include:

- Deep teal
- Wine
- Maroon
- Emerald
- Navy
- Black
- Deep purple
- Rich red

Metallic or embellished accessories can be added depending on the garment.

### Night Party

For a more glamorous night look:

- Tissue Silk
- Silk
- Embroidered Organza
- Embellished Georgette
- Darker colour palettes
- Statement jewellery

The outfit should still maintain balance between fabric, embroidery and accessories.


## 5. Party-Wear Colour Guide

Pastels create an elegant and softer party look.

Good directions include:

- Powder blue
- Blush pink
- Dusty rose
- Lavender
- Soft peach
- Ivory
- Champagne
- Mint or soft green

Pastels work particularly well with:

- Organza
- Chiffon
- Light Georgette
- Chanderi

### Jewel Tones

Jewel colours are particularly effective for evening parties.

Consider:

- Emerald green
- Royal blue
- Deep teal
- Magenta
- Ruby red
- Wine
- Amethyst
- Deep purple

These colours can create a strong look even when embroidery is relatively restrained.

### Neutrals

Neutral party wear can look sophisticated when combined with texture or embellishment.

Examples:

- Ivory
- Champagne
- Beige
- Antique gold
- Black
- Soft cream

For neutral outfits, the visual interest can come from:

- Embroidery
- Zari
- Texture
- Dupatta
- Jewellery
- Metallic footwear


## 6. Party-Wear Embellishment

Party wear does not necessarily require heavy embroidery.

### Minimal Party Look

Use:

- Tonal embroidery
- Small motifs
- Fine borders
- Minimal neckline detailing
- Light threadwork

Best for:

- Brunch parties
- Day events
- Intimate gatherings
- Elegant dinners

### Moderate Party Look

Use:

- Embroidered neckline
- Decorative sleeves
- Floral embroidery
- Moderate borders
- Embellished dupatta

Best for:

- Evening parties
- Family functions
- Festive dinners
- Receptions

### Statement Party Look

Use:

- Dense embroidery
- Zari
- Mirror or crystal details where specifically present
- Elaborate borders
- Statement dupatta
- Rich fabrics

Best for:

- Grand celebrations
- Receptions
- Formal wedding functions
- High-glamour evening events

The exact embellishment must always be confirmed from the individual product rather than inferred from its fabric category.


## 7. Party-Wear Silhouettes

### A-Line

A-Line silhouettes work particularly well with Organza and other fabrics that can maintain some structure.

Advantages:

- Elegant shape
- Comfortable movement
- Suitable for daytime and evening
- Easy to accessorize

### Flared Anarkali

A flared Anarkali creates a more dramatic party appearance.

Particularly useful for:

- Wedding functions
- Evening celebrations
- Formal family parties
- Festive gatherings

The amount of flare should be matched to fabric width, available material and embroidery placement.

### Straight-Cut

A straight-cut suit can become party wear through:

- Rich fabric
- Embroidery
- Statement dupatta
- Jewellery
- Strong colour

This makes it useful for customers who prefer a more understated silhouette.

### Contemporary Short Kurta

A shorter kurta with suitable bottoms can create a modern party look.

It can work especially well with:

- Crepe
- Georgette
- Organza where appropriate
- Embellished fabrics

### Palazzo Styling

Palazzo bottoms can provide a contemporary party appearance while maintaining comfort.

### Sharara Styling

Sharara-inspired bottoms can create a more celebratory appearance, especially for wedding-related parties.

The final silhouette should always be checked against the available fabric quantity, borders and embroidery.


## 8. Party-Wear Dupatta Styling

The dupatta can be the strongest styling element in a party outfit.

### Statement Dupatta

Use a heavily detailed or decorative dupatta with a relatively simple kurta.

This creates balance.

### Simple Dupatta + Embellished Kurta

When the kurta already has significant embroidery, a simpler dupatta can prevent the outfit from looking overloaded.

### Sheer Dupatta

Organza and Chiffon dupattas can create a lighter and more elegant party appearance.

### Metallic Dupatta

Tissue or zari detailing can create a stronger evening look.

### Dupatta Draping

For parties:

- One-shoulder drape for a contemporary look
- Balanced shoulder drape for elegance
- Front drape for showcasing embroidery
- Controlled pleated drape for formal occasions

The most practical drape depends on the fabric and amount of embellishment.


## 9. Party-Wear Jewellery

Jewellery should complement the garment's level of decoration.

### Minimal Party Outfit

Use:

- Statement earrings
- Small necklace
- Bracelet
- Watch

### Embroidered Suit

Consider:

- Earrings
- Minimal necklace
- Bangles
- Rings

Avoid adding a large necklace if the neckline already contains heavy embroidery.

### High-Glamour Outfit

For Tissue, heavily embellished Silk or statement Organza:

- Chandelier earrings
- Statement necklace
- Bangles
- Cocktail rings

However, the customer should generally choose one or two major jewellery elements rather than making every accessory a statement.


## 10. Party-Wear Footwear

Footwear can reinforce the overall level of formality.

### Elegant options

- Embellished juttis
- Metallic sandals
- Heeled sandals
- Block heels
- Minimal pumps
- Dressy ethnic footwear

### Day parties

Prefer:

- Comfortable flats
- Juttis
- Low block heels

### Evening parties

Consider:

- Metallic heels
- Embellished sandals
- Statement footwear

Comfort remains important, especially for parties involving extended standing or dancing.

## 11. Party-Wear Styling by Event

### Cocktail-Style Ethnic Party

Recommended direction:

**Organza / Georgette + contemporary silhouette + statement earrings**

Keep the outfit sophisticated rather than heavily traditional.

### Evening Dinner

Recommended direction:

**Georgette / Organza / Silk + refined colour + elegant jewellery**

A simple silhouette can work particularly well.

### Family Party

Recommended direction:

**Georgette / Chanderi / Silk + moderate embroidery + traditional accessories**

The exact formality can be adjusted according to the event.

### Reception

Recommended direction:

**Organza / Silk / Tissue + statement detailing + elegant jewellery**

For highly formal receptions, richer fabrics and stronger embellishment can be appropriate.

### Festive Party

Recommended direction:

**Silk / Tissue / Organza / Georgette**

The choice should depend on how grand the event is.

### Summer Evening Party

Recommended direction:

**Chiffon / Georgette / lightweight Organza**

Prioritize lighter colours and breathable construction where possible.


## 12. Party Wear: Day vs Evening

| Feature | Day Party | Evening Party |
| Colour | Pastel, soft, neutral | Jewel, deep, metallic |
| Fabric | Chanderi, Chiffon, light Georgette | Organza, Silk, Tissue, Georgette |
| Embellishment | Light-moderate | Moderate-heavy |
| Jewellery | Minimal | Statement |
| Footwear | Flats / low heels | Heels / embellished footwear |
| Dupatta | Lightweight | Decorative / statement |
| Silhouette | A-Line / straight | A-Line / flared / statement |


## 13. Party Wear and Comfort

A party outfit should still allow movement.

Before selecting or stitching a suit, consider:

- Shoulder comfort
- Bust ease
- Arm movement
- Sleeve width
- Kurta length
- Bottom comfort
- Dupatta management
- Fabric weight
- Lining
- Embroidery placement

This is particularly important for Organza and embellished fabrics, where construction and finishing can affect comfort.


## 14. Custom Stitching for Party Wear

Nari18's unstitched format allows party outfits to be customized around individual measurements and styling preferences. The brand states that its in-house tailoring team can work with neckline, sleeve and fit preferences, while product-level stitching availability should still be verified where necessary.

Useful party-wear customization decisions include:

### Neckline

Possible documented choices include:

- Round neck
- V-neck
- Boat neck
- Keyhole
- Mandarin collar

The neckline should be coordinated with the embroidery and jewellery.

### Sleeves

Possible documented choices include:

- Full
- Three-quarter
- Half
- Sleeveless

For party wear, sleeve styling can be used to create a more distinctive appearance, but fabric structure and embroidery placement must be considered.

### Silhouette

Possible directions include:

- Straight-cut
- A-Line
- Flared Anarkali
- Palazzo
- Sharara
- Short kurta
- Coordinated styling

Not every product can necessarily support every silhouette.


## 15. Fabric-Specific Party Styling

### Organza

Best styling direction:

**Structured silhouette + refined embroidery + controlled jewellery**

Avoid overcrowding the look with too many heavy elements.

### Georgette

Best styling direction:

**Flowing silhouette + elegant drape + graceful jewellery**

Use the fabric's movement as part of the visual appeal.

### Chiffon

Best styling direction:

**Light silhouette + soft colours + delicate accessories**

Particularly useful for warm-weather parties.

### Silk

Best styling direction:

**Rich colour + classic silhouette + refined jewellery**

Let the fabric provide much of the luxury.

### Tissue

Best styling direction:

**Simple silhouette + metallic fabric + carefully selected jewellery**

Because Tissue can already provide shine, excessive accessories may overwhelm the look.

### Chanderi

Best styling direction:

**Elegant daytime silhouette + moderate embroidery + traditional accessories**


## 16. Party Wear and Accessories Balance

A useful rule is:

**If the fabric is dramatic, simplify the accessories.**

Examples:

- Heavy Tissue + simple earrings
- Embroidered Organza + minimal necklace
- Statement dupatta + restrained kurta
- Rich Silk + classic jewellery
- Simple Georgette + statement earrings

This creates a more intentional outfit than combining maximum decoration in every component.


## 17. Party-Wear Wardrobe Strategy

A versatile party wardrobe does not require every suit to be heavily embellished.

A useful combination can include:

1. **One elegant Organza suit** — statement evening wear
2. **One flowing Georgette suit** — versatile parties and family functions
3. **One Silk suit** — formal celebrations
4. **One Tissue suit** — grand events
5. **One lighter Chiffon/Georgette suit** — summer parties
6. **One Chanderi suit** — daytime celebrations

This gives the customer different levels of formality rather than multiple outfits with the same visual intensity.


## 18. Choosing the Right Party-Wear Look

Use this decision process:

### Step 1 — Identify the event

Is it:

- Casual party
- Family gathering
- Dinner
- Cocktail-style event
- Reception
- Grand celebration

### Step 2 — Identify the time

Daytime generally allows lighter colours and softer styling.

Evening allows richer colours, stronger embellishment and more dramatic accessories.

### Step 3 — Select fabric

- Structured → Organza
- Flowing → Georgette
- Lightweight → Chiffon
- Rich → Silk
- Shimmering → Tissue
- Refined daytime → Chanderi

### Step 4 — Select formality

Choose:

- Light
- Moderate
- Statement

### Step 5 — Balance accessories

If the suit is heavily decorated, reduce jewellery and vice versa.

### Step 6 — Consider comfort

Make sure the final silhouette works for sitting, standing, walking and movement.
