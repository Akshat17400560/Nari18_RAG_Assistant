# Nari18 Fabric by Occasion Guide

## 1. Overview

Choosing a Nari18 unstitched suit becomes easier when the **occasion, time of day, season, formality and desired appearance** are considered together.

Nari18's current fabric guides establish a clear overall pattern:

- **Silk and Tissue** → weddings, bridal events and grand celebrations
- **Chanderi and Georgette** → festive occasions and wedding-guest dressing
- **Organza and Chiffon** → evening, party and lighter occasion wear
- **Cotton, Linen and Muslin** → office and everyday wear
- **Mul-Cotton/Mulmul** → hot-weather and everyday comfort
- **Crepe** → parties, dinners and evening styling
- **Hand-printed/Block-print fabrics** → everyday, office and casual ethnic looks

These are starting points rather than rigid rules. The final suitability of an individual suit depends on its exact fabric composition, colour, embroidery, print, silhouette and overall design.

# 3. Weddings

## Wedding Guest

### Best choices
- Chanderi
- Silk
- Georgette

### Alternative
- Organza
- Chiffon for lighter daytime functions

Chanderi is particularly useful when the customer wants festive elegance without the weight of a heavier Silk outfit. Nari18 specifically recommends Chanderi and Silk for wedding guests.

### Styling direction

For a daytime wedding:

**Chanderi + pastel/rich colour + moderate jewellery**

For an evening wedding:

**Silk/Georgette + deeper colour + elegant jewellery**

---

## Wedding Host or Close Family

### Best choices
- Silk
- Tissue

### Alternative
- Organza
- Rich Chanderi

The host or close family member generally has more scope for richer fabrics and stronger detailing.

A suitable formula is:

**Rich fabric + statement silhouette + coordinated jewellery + decorative dupatta**

Nari18 specifically places Silk and Tissue toward weddings, bridal events and high-formality celebrations.

### Best choices
- Silk
- Tissue

### Alternative
- Organza

A practical trousseau can include:

- Silk for grand occasions
- Tissue for statement festive looks
- Organza for evening elegance
- Chanderi for lighter daytime functions

Can become:

- Daytime → lightweight dupatta
- Festive → zari/decorative dupatta

### Silk Suit

Can become:

- Formal → elegant Silk dupatta
- Grand → richly detailed statement dupatta

### Georgette Suit

Can become:

- Evening → flowing embellished dupatta
- Casual → lighter printed dupatta

### Organza Suit

Can become:

- Minimal → simple sheer dupatta
- Statement → embroidered/decorative Organza dupatta

Always verify the actual dupatta fabric and detailing from the product page.

# 30. Important Fabric-by-Occasion RAG Rules

1. **Use the occasion first, then fabric.** A fabric is not inherently suitable for every event.

2. For **weddings**, prioritize Silk or Chanderi for guests and Silk/Tissue for hosts or grand celebrations.

3. For **festive occasions**, Chanderi and Georgette are strong general recommendations, with Silk and Organza for more formal looks.

4. For **office wear**, prioritize Linen and Cotton. Muslin can be used for comfortable warm-weather professional dressing.

5. For **daily wear**, prioritize Cotton, Muslin and Mul-Cotton.

6. For **evening and party wear**, prioritize Organza, Georgette and Chiffon; Silk, Tissue and Crepe can be used for richer or more formal versions.

7. For **summer**, prioritize Cotton/Mulmul, Linen and Muslin; Chanderi is suitable when the occasion requires a festive look.

8. Do not infer that every product in a fabric collection is suitable for every occasion listed for that fabric.

9. Do not infer embroidery, zari, mirror work, sequins, pearls, crystals or other embellishments from the fabric category.

10. Do not infer exact fibre composition from a collection name. Use the individual product's fabric details.

11. Do not infer care instructions solely from the occasion. Individual product care instructions take priority.

12. Do not treat the general Nari18 price ranges in fabric guides as current product prices. Prices, discounts and availability can change.

13. Do not claim that a particular colour is available in a specific fabric unless confirmed on the current product page.

14. When a customer asks for a recommendation, combine **occasion + season + time + formality + comfort requirement** instead of recommending based on fabric alone.

15. If two fabrics are both appropriate, explain the difference in appearance or comfort rather than presenting one as universally better.

# 31. Short RAG Answers

**Q: Which fabric is best for a wedding guest?**  
A: Chanderi and Silk are Nari18's primary recommendations. Chanderi is lighter and versatile, while Silk gives a richer, more formal appearance.

**Q: Which fabric is best for a wedding host?**  
A: Silk or Tissue are strong choices for a richer, more prominent wedding look.

**Q: Which fabric is best for office wear?**  
A: Linen and Cotton are the strongest choices. Linen gives a more structured polished look, while Cotton prioritizes everyday comfort.

**Q: Which fabric is best for daily wear?**  
A: Cotton and Muslin are strong everyday choices, with Mul-Cotton particularly useful for hot weather.

**Q: Which fabric is best for a party?**  
A: Organza and Georgette are strong choices for parties. Silk or Tissue can be selected when the event is more formal or glamorous.

**Q: Which fabric is best for an evening dinner?**  
A: Organza or Georgette are excellent starting points; Silk or Crepe can be considered for richer or more formal looks.

**Q: Which fabric is best for summer?**  
A: Nari18 recommends Cotton, especially Mulmul, along with Linen and Muslin. Chanderi works well when you need festive summer dressing.

**Q: Which fabric is best for Mehendi or Haldi?**  
A: Chiffon and Georgette are strong choices for lightweight, flowing styling. Cotton and Muslin can be considered when comfort is the priority.

**Q: Which fabric should I choose for Diwali?**  
A: Chanderi, Georgette and Silk are strong choices, while Tissue or Organza can create a more glamorous look.

**Q: Which fabric is best for a brunch?**  
A: Muslin and Linen are strong options; Cotton and Chanderi can also work depending on the desired formality.

**Q: Which fabric gives the most glamorous look?**  
A: Tissue, Silk and statement Organza can provide a particularly rich and glamorous appearance.

**Q: Which fabric should I choose if comfort is my top priority?**  
A: Cotton, Mul-Cotton and Muslin are the strongest starting points, especially for warm weather.

**Q: Can the same fabric work for different occasions?**  
A: Yes. The occasion level can change significantly with colour, embroidery, silhouette, dupatta, jewellery and footwear. For example, Cotton can work for everyday wear or a more elevated daytime look, while Chanderi can range from festive brunches to wedding-guest styling.
