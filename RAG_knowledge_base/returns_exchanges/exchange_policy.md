# Nari18 Exchange Policy

## 1. Overview

Nari18's current exchange policy primarily covers **manufacturing defects**.

According to Nari18's current **Our Promise**:

- Exchange requests for manufacturing defects are accepted within **7 days**.
- Customers should provide **clear photos/videos** so Nari18 can verify the issue.
- Once the request is approved, Nari18 guides the customer through the exchange process.
- **Custom-stitched orders are not eligible for exchange.**

The Nari18 homepage currently summarizes the same policy as:

> Exchange within 7 days for manufacturing defects. Custom stitched orders are not exchangeable.

For complete legal conditions, the current Nari18 Terms & Conditions should be treated as the primary policy source.


# 2. Exchange Window

The current exchange window for a manufacturing defect is:

**7 days from receiving the order.**

A customer who discovers a possible manufacturing defect should contact Nari18 as soon as possible rather than waiting until the end of the 7-day period.


# 3. What Qualifies for Exchange?

The current Nari18 exchange policy specifically identifies:

**Manufacturing defects**

as the basis for exchange requests.

The website does not provide a complete public list defining every possible manufacturing defect.

Therefore, the final determination should be made by Nari18 after reviewing the customer's evidence.


# 4. Manufacturing Defect vs Change of Mind

An exchange for a manufacturing defect is different from a customer simply changing their mind.

Examples of reasons that should not automatically be described as manufacturing defects include:

- Customer no longer likes the colour.
- Customer changes their preferred style.
- Customer chooses a different fabric.
- Customer wants a different neckline.
- Customer wants a different sleeve style.
- Customer ordered the wrong standard size.
- Customer changes their occasion or styling preference.

The current Nari18 exchange promise specifically refers to **manufacturing defects**, not general change-of-mind exchanges.


# 5. How to Report a Defect

If a customer believes that a product has a manufacturing defect:

1. Inspect the product carefully.
2. Identify the suspected defect.
3. Take clear photographs.
4. Record a video if it helps demonstrate the issue.
5. Contact Nari18 within 7 days of receiving the order.
6. Provide the requested order/product information.
7. Submit the photos/videos for verification.
8. Wait for Nari18's approval and further exchange instructions.

The purpose of the photos/videos is to allow Nari18 to verify the reported issue before approving the exchange.


# 6. Photo Requirements

Nari18 specifically asks customers to share **clear photos/videos** for manufacturing-defect verification.

Customers should try to capture:

- The complete product
- The affected area
- A close-up of the defect
- Product tags where relevant
- Packaging if the issue appears related to shipping/handling
- Multiple angles where necessary

### Good evidence

A photograph should be:

- Clear
- Well lit
- In focus
- Close enough to show the defect
- Accompanied by wider photographs when context is needed


# 7. Why Evidence Is Important

A product may have:

- Embroidery
- Handwork
- Prints
- Zari
- Decorative borders
- Delicate fabrics
- Handcrafted details

Because of this, Nari18 needs to review the reported issue before determining whether it qualifies as a manufacturing defect.

The customer should therefore avoid removing evidence or altering the product before contacting Nari18.


# 8. Do Not Alter the Product Before Reporting a Defect

If a customer believes that a product has a manufacturing defect, they should avoid:

- Washing it
- Stitching it
- Cutting it
- Altering it
- Removing tags
- Modifying embroidery
- Changing the neckline
- Changing sleeves
- Adding lining
- Making other permanent alterations

This is especially important for unstitched products.

A customer should report a suspected issue before making changes to the product.


# 9. Custom-Stitched Orders

Nari18's current policy is explicit:

**Custom-stitched orders are not eligible for exchange.**

This is one of the most important exchange-policy rules for Nari18 because the brand offers custom tailoring.

Custom stitching can involve customer-specific:

- Measurements
- Silhouette
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Length
- Fit preferences
- Lining
- Finishing

Once the product has been made to those specifications, the standard exchange policy does not apply to the customised order.


# 10. Customisation and Exchange

Customers should carefully confirm their preferences before requesting custom stitching.

Important details can include:

- Bust measurement
- Waist measurement
- Hip measurement
- Shoulder width
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Bottom measurements
- Silhouette
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Fit preference
- Lining requirements

Nari18's current custom-stitching information describes WhatsApp-based coordination after ordering, including measurements and design preferences.


# 11. Standard Stitched Products vs Custom-Stitched Products

These should not automatically be treated as the same category.

### Standard stitched product

A ready-made product manufactured in a listed standard size.

### Custom-stitched product

A product tailored specifically according to customer measurements/preferences.

Nari18's current policy specifically excludes **custom-stitched orders** from exchange.

For a standard stitched product, the exact applicable policy should be checked based on the current order and Terms.


# 12. Unstitched Products

For an unstitched product, customers should inspect the fabric and components before:

- Cutting
- Stitching
- Washing
- Altering
- Customising

If a suspected manufacturing defect is discovered, the customer should report it within the 7-day exchange window with clear photos/videos.

If the customer subsequently requests custom stitching, the customised-product restriction becomes relevant.


# 13. Exchange vs Return

Nari18's website uses both return and exchange concepts, but they should not be treated as identical.

### Return

The current Terms describe a return process for eligible products, including a 7-day request period and store-credit outcome after quality checking.

### Exchange

The current **Our Promise** specifically describes exchange for **manufacturing defects within 7 days**.


# 14. Exchange Is Not an Automatic Refund

An exchange request should not be described as a cash refund.

The current Our Promise describes an exchange process after Nari18 verifies the manufacturing defect.


# 15. Exchange Approval

Submitting a defect request does not automatically guarantee an exchange.

The process includes:

1. Customer reports the issue.
2. Customer provides photos/videos.
3. Nari18 reviews the issue.
4. Nari18 determines whether it qualifies.
5. If approved, Nari18 provides the exchange instructions.


# 16. Exchange Process

The current website describes the process at a high level rather than publishing every operational step.

The RAG-friendly workflow is:

### Step 1 — Receive the order

Inspect the product as soon as possible.

### Step 2 — Identify the issue

Determine what appears defective.

### Step 3 — Document it

Take clear photos/videos.

### Step 4 — Contact Nari18

Raise the issue within 7 days.

### Step 5 — Verification

Nari18 reviews the submitted evidence.

### Step 6 — Approval

If the issue qualifies as a manufacturing defect, Nari18 approves the exchange.

### Step 7 — Exchange instructions

Nari18 guides the customer through the next steps.


# 17. Return Pickup and Exchange

The current Nari18 Terms describe reverse pickup for approved returns, while the Our Promise provides a high-level exchange process for manufacturing defects.

The exact logistics for an approved exchange should therefore be determined from Nari18's instructions for that specific order.


# 18. Exchange Delivery Timeline

The current Our Promise does not publish a fixed replacement-delivery SLA.

Therefore, the assistant should not invent an exchange timeline such as:

- 3 days
- 5 days
- 7 days
- 10 days

unless the current Nari18 support/order information confirms it.


# 19. Replacement Product Availability

An exchange can depend on the availability of the relevant replacement product.

The current website shows that Nari18's inventory is dynamic, with some products in stock and others sold out.

Therefore, do not guarantee that an identical replacement will always be available.



# 20. Sold-Out Product and Exchange

A product can become sold out after the customer's original purchase.

Therefore, if a manufacturing defect is reported against a product that is now sold out, the assistant should not automatically promise:

- Same-product replacement
- Refund
- Store credit
- Alternative product

The customer should contact Nari18 so the team can determine the appropriate resolution.


# 21. Exchange and Product Availability

Current Nari18 catalog data demonstrates that availability is dynamic. The catalog currently contains products with active **Add to cart/Buy now** controls as well as products marked **Sold out**.

This matters for exchange requests because replacement availability can change.


# 22. Exchange and Sale Products

Nari18's detailed Terms should be used to determine the treatment of sale products.

The current website's policy messaging should not be simplified into:

> "All discounted products can be exchanged."

Nari18's Terms currently contain exclusions for sale items, so a sale purchase should be checked against the applicable policy before promising an exchange.


# 23. Exchange and Custom-Stitched Products

This is a critical rule:

**Custom-stitched orders are not exchangeable.**

The customer should therefore confirm measurements and design preferences before custom stitching.

Nari18's current custom-stitching guide describes coordination through WhatsApp after ordering, including measurements, silhouette, neckline and sleeve preferences.


# 24. Fit Issues

A customer may receive a standard stitched garment and feel that the fit is not ideal.

This should not automatically be described as a manufacturing defect.

Similarly, for a custom-stitched order, a fit complaint does not automatically create an exchange right because custom-stitched orders are excluded from exchange.


# 25. Colour Differences

A customer may feel that the actual colour looks different from the website image.

This should not automatically be classified as a manufacturing defect.

Possible reasons for perceived differences can include:

- Lighting
- Screen settings
- Photography
- Fabric texture
- Natural variation

If the customer believes the product has an actual manufacturing issue, they should document it and contact Nari18 within the applicable 7-day period.


# 26. Handcrafted and Fabric Variation

Nari18 sells handcrafted and fabric-led ethnic products.

The website describes its collection as including handcrafted fabrics and artisan-made products.

Not every visual difference should automatically be classified as a manufacturing defect.


# 27. Exchange and Product Alteration

If a customer believes there is a defect, they should contact Nari18 before modifying the garment.

Avoid:

- Stitching
- Cutting
- Re-embroidering
- Altering
- Washing
- Dyeing
- Adding or removing lining

before the issue has been reported and instructions received.

This is particularly important for products where the exchange determination depends on their original condition.


# 28. Evidence Checklist

Before contacting Nari18 about a suspected manufacturing defect, prepare:

- Order number
- Product name
- Delivery date
- Clear defect photographs
- Close-up photographs
- Full-product photographs
- Video where useful
- Packaging photographs if relevant
- Product/tag photographs

The exact information requested by Nari18 can vary by case.


# 29. When to Contact Nari18

Customers should contact Nari18 as soon as possible when:

- A product appears to have a manufacturing defect.
- The delivered product appears damaged.
- There is a significant construction issue.
- A product issue needs verification.
- The customer is unsure whether an issue qualifies for exchange.

# 32. Exchange and Payment Method

Exchange eligibility is not determined simply by how the customer paid.

The customer may have used:

- COD
- UPI
- Debit/credit card
- Net banking

The exchange policy is based primarily on:

- Product condition
- Defect
- Timeline
- Product/order type
- Customisation status
- Applicable Terms


# 33. Exchange and Shipping

Shipping and exchange are separate policy areas.

Nari18 currently advertises pan-India delivery and provides separate exchange information for manufacturing defects.

Do not assume that:

- Free shipping means free exchange in every situation.
- A shipping charge creates an exchange entitlement.
- The original shipping charge is automatically refunded.


# 34. Exchange and Return Are Different Outcomes

A customer reporting a defect may ask for a "return" when they actually want a replacement.

The assistant should clarify the policy concept:

### Exchange

Replacement for an eligible manufacturing defect, subject to Nari18's verification.

### Return

Separate return process governed by Nari18's Terms and eligibility requirements.

### Refund/store credit

A separate financial outcome and should not be promised unless the applicable policy specifically provides it.


# 35. No Automatic Exchange for Preference Changes

Nari18's manufacturing-defect exchange policy should not be interpreted as a general product-change facility.

Do not promise exchange because the customer:

- Prefers another colour
- Wants another fabric
- Wants another silhouette
- Wants another neckline
- Wants another sleeve
- Changed their mind
- Ordered a product for an event that was cancelled
- Decided they no longer want the product

These are not automatically manufacturing defects.


# 36. Exchange for Wrong Product

If a customer believes they received a different product from what they ordered, they should contact Nari18 promptly with:

- Order details
- Product photographs
- Packaging photographs
- Product tags
- Clear explanation of the mismatch


# 37. Exchange for Damaged Product

If the product arrives damaged:

1. Photograph the damage.
2. Photograph the packaging.
3. Do not alter or wash the product.
4. Contact Nari18 within 7 days.
5. Submit the requested evidence.
6. Wait for verification.
7. Follow the approved exchange instructions.

The current Our Promise requires clear photos/videos for manufacturing-defect verification.


# 38. Exchange for Missing Components

If a customer believes a component of a suit set is missing, such as an expected garment component, the customer should contact Nari18 promptly.

The assistant should not automatically classify the issue as a manufacturing defect without reviewing:

- Product description
- What was ordered
- What was delivered
- Product composition
- Order details


# 39. Exchange and Product Description

Before declaring that an item is defective, compare the delivered product with the product description.

Nari18 product pages can specify individual components such as:

- Kurta
- Dupatta
- Bottom
- Fabric
- Embroidery
- Borders
- Design details

For example, current product pages separately describe fabric and component details for individual suits.

A feature not present in the product description should not automatically be treated as a missing component or defect.


# 40. Exchange and Product Reviews

Customer reviews are not a substitute for the official exchange policy.

A product can have many positive reviews and still be eligible for exchange if a genuine manufacturing defect occurs.

Conversely, a review mentioning a fit or styling concern does not automatically create an exchange entitlement.


# 41. Exchange and Product Availability

The Nari18 catalog currently contains both available and sold-out products.

Therefore, when handling an exchange request, availability should be checked separately.
