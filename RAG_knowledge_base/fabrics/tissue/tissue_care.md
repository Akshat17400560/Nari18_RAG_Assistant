# Nari18 Tissue Silk Care Guide

## 1. Overview

Tissue Silk is a delicate, festive fabric known for its shimmering or metallic appearance and crisp structure. Nari18 positions Tissue for weddings, Karwa Chauth, poojas, evening celebrations, and other premium festive occasions.

Nari18's current fabric guide specifically states:

**Care: Dry clean only.**

This recommendation is particularly important for Tissue products containing zari, embroidery, pearls, crystals, decorative borders, or other embellishments.

The exact care requirements of an individual product should always take priority if the product page provides more specific instructions.


# 2. Basic Tissue Care Rules

For Nari18 Tissue and Tissue Silk products:

- Prefer professional dry cleaning.
- Do not routinely machine wash Tissue.
- Avoid soaking the fabric.
- Do not wring or twist the material.
- Avoid aggressive rubbing.
- Keep the fabric away from harsh chemicals.
- Avoid bleach.
- Protect zari, embroidery, pearls, crystals, and decorative borders.
- Avoid unnecessary high heat.
- Store Tissue clean and completely dry.
- Protect the fabric from direct sunlight and moisture.
- Follow product-specific care instructions where provided.

Tissue should not be treated like everyday cotton or other casual washable fabrics.


# 3. Cleaning Tissue Silk

## Preferred cleaning method

Nari18's general Tissue guidance is **dry clean only**.

Professional dry cleaning is particularly appropriate for Tissue suits containing:

- Zari work
- Resham embroidery
- Pearls
- Crystals
- Embroidered panels
- Metallic borders
- Tassels
- Decorative motifs
- Multiple fabric combinations

A professional cleaner should be informed about the presence of metallic or embroidered decoration.


# 4. Why Tissue Requires Careful Cleaning

Tissue's appearance depends partly on its woven structure and reflective surface.

Aggressive cleaning can potentially affect:

- Surface sheen
- Fabric structure
- Colour appearance
- Embroidery
- Metallic decoration
- Borders
- Decorative attachments

A Tissue suit may also combine several materials.

For example, Nari18's current Dusty Rose-Copper Tissue Suit contains:

- Tissue kurta fabric
- Tissue/woven-zari dupatta
- Cotton silk shantoon bottom

Therefore, the complete suit should not automatically be treated as though every component has the same care requirement.


# 5. Washing Tissue at Home

Routine home washing should be avoided unless the exact product's care instructions explicitly permit it.

Avoid:

- Washing machines
- Hot water
- Long soaking
- Strong detergents
- Bleach
- Fabric treatments not approved for the product
- Scrubbing
- Wringing
- Twisting

The safest general rule for Nari18 Tissue is:

**Do not experiment with home washing when the product is labelled or described as dry-clean-only.**


# 6. Stain Handling

If a Tissue suit develops a stain:

1. Do not immediately scrub the affected area.
2. Do not use bleach.
3. Do not soak the entire suit.
4. Do not apply an aggressive household stain remover.
5. Identify whether the stain has affected embroidery or zari.
6. For significant stains, use professional cleaning.
7. Inform the cleaner about metallic or embroidered decoration.

Aggressive stain removal can cause more visible damage to a delicate festive fabric than the original stain.

For expensive or heavily decorated Tissue suits, professional treatment is preferable to experimenting with household cleaning methods.


# 7. Caring for Zari on Tissue

Zari is one of the most important decorative elements to protect on heavily embellished Tissue suits.

Nari18's current Dusty Rose-Copper Tissue Suit, for example, includes:

- Zari embroidery
- Resham embroidery
- Pearl highlights
- Crystal highlights
- Scattered zari floral butis
- Gold-zari dupatta borders
- Zari floral motifs

These decorative components require careful handling.

### Avoid

- Aggressive rubbing
- High heat
- Harsh chemicals
- Excessive moisture
- Rough surfaces
- Repeated friction
- Heavy compression

The metallic appearance of zari should be protected during cleaning, pressing, wearing, and storage.


# 8. Caring for Embroidered Tissue

Embroidered Tissue should be handled more carefully than plain Tissue.

Pay particular attention to:

- Neckline embroidery
- Heavy embroidered panels
- Floral motifs
- Resham work
- Zari
- Pearl embellishments
- Crystal embellishments
- Embroidered borders

During use, avoid contact with:

- Sharp jewellery
- Rough handbags
- Velcro
- Sharp hooks
- Rough furniture
- Abrasive surfaces

These can catch embroidery threads or loosen decorative elements.


# 9. Pearl and Crystal Care

Some Nari18 Tissue products contain pearls and crystals.

When handling such pieces:

- Avoid rubbing embellished areas.
- Do not press them directly with a hot iron.
- Avoid aggressive cleaning.
- Keep decorative sections away from rough surfaces.
- Do not pull loose embellishments.
- Store the garment without heavy pressure on the decorated surface.

If an embellishment becomes loose, avoid attempting a permanent repair without assessing the fabric and embroidery first.


# 10. Ironing Tissue Silk

Tissue has a crisp, reflective surface, so ironing should be approached cautiously.

Before ironing:

1. Check the product-specific care instructions.
2. Confirm that ironing is permitted.
3. Test an inconspicuous area if appropriate.
4. Use controlled, low heat where permitted.
5. Prefer pressing from the reverse side.
6. Use a clean pressing cloth.
7. Avoid prolonged direct heat.
8. Keep direct heat away from embroidery, zari, pearls, and crystals.

### Avoid

- Very high heat
- Direct heat over metallic decoration
- Prolonged contact
- Aggressive back-and-forth ironing
- Pressing heavily decorated areas without protection

If the fabric develops an unexpected change in sheen, texture, colour, or surface appearance, stop ironing.


# 11. Steam and Tissue

Steam should not automatically be considered safe for every Tissue product.

Particular caution is required when the fabric contains:

- Zari
- Pearls
- Crystals
- Heavy embroidery
- Metallic borders
- Decorative finishing

Before steaming:

- Check the product's care instructions.
- Avoid excessive moisture.
- Keep steam equipment at a safe distance where appropriate.
- Do not concentrate steam on one decorative area.
- Allow the fabric to become completely dry before storage.

For heavily embellished Tissue, professional pressing is the safer choice.


# 12. Caring for Tissue Dupattas

A Tissue dupatta can be particularly delicate because it may combine:

- Metallic weave
- Zari
- Embroidery
- Borders
- Tassels
- Sheer or lightweight sections

Nari18's Dusty Rose-Copper Tissue Suit, for example, has a Tissue/woven-zari dupatta with all-over zari floral motifs, broad gold-zari borders, and tassel finishing.

### Dupatta care

- Avoid pulling the corners.
- Protect tassels from tangling.
- Keep the dupatta away from sharp jewellery.
- Avoid folding directly across heavily decorated areas repeatedly.
- Do not place heavy garments on top of it.
- Keep it clean and dry.
- Follow the product's specific care instructions.


# 13. Tissue After Wearing

After wearing a Tissue suit:

1. Check the garment for stains.
2. Check for loose embroidery or embellishments.
3. Remove jewellery and accessories carefully.
4. Allow the garment to air in a clean, shaded area if necessary.
5. Ensure it is completely dry.
6. Do not immediately compress it for long-term storage.
7. Clean it according to the appropriate care instructions.
8. Store it carefully after cleaning.

Do not use aggressive home cleaning simply because the garment has been worn once.


# 14. Tissue Storage

Proper storage helps preserve the fabric's sheen, structure, embroidery, and decorative elements.

### Before storage

Ensure the Tissue is:

- Clean
- Completely dry
- Free from moisture
- Protected from direct sunlight

### Storage environment

Use a:

- Clean cupboard
- Dry environment
- Low-friction storage area
- Suitable protective covering

Avoid prolonged storage in tightly compressed plastic packaging.


# 15. Folding Tissue

Tissue can develop unwanted creases if it is stored under excessive pressure.

For decorated Tissue:

- Avoid repeatedly folding through the same embroidered panel.
- Avoid placing heavy garments on top.
- Protect zari and embroidery from excessive friction.
- Arrange borders carefully.
- Keep tassels and decorative ends from becoming trapped under heavy folds.

For valuable or heavily decorated pieces, careful folding or appropriate professional garment storage is preferable to tightly compressing the fabric.


# 16. Moisture and Humidity

Moisture should be avoided during long-term Tissue storage.

Do not store Tissue when it is:

- Damp
- Recently steamed and still wet
- Exposed to condensation
- Stored in a humid area

Before putting the garment away:

- Allow it to dry completely.
- Check the folds and decorative areas.
- Make sure no moisture remains around embroidery or borders.

Do not place a damp Tissue suit inside sealed plastic packaging for extended storage.


# 17. Sunlight and Colour Protection

Tissue's reflective surface and decorative details should be protected from unnecessary prolonged exposure to direct sunlight.

For long-term storage:

- Keep the fabric away from direct sunlight.
- Avoid leaving it exposed near a window.
- Use an appropriate protective covering.
- Store it in a clean and dry cupboard.

This is particularly important for richly coloured Tissue and products with decorative embroidery.


# 18. Jewellery and Accessory Precautions

Tissue can be vulnerable to snags from sharp accessories.

Before wearing:

- Check necklace clasps.
- Check earrings for sharp edges.
- Check bracelets and bangles.
- Check handbag hardware.
- Avoid rough hooks.
- Be careful when removing jewellery.

This is especially important around:

- Embroidered panels
- Zari work
- Dupatta borders
- Tassels
- Fine decorative motifs

A snag in a heavily decorated area can be difficult to repair without affecting the overall appearance.


# 19. Caring for Unstitched Tissue

Nari18 primarily sells Tissue products as unstitched suit sets.

The fabric should be protected before it reaches the tailor.

### Before stitching

- Keep the fabric clean and dry.
- Do not wash it experimentally.
- Keep decorative panels protected.
- Avoid unnecessary folding and unfolding.
- Preserve borders and embroidery.
- Do not apply chemicals or stain removers without guidance.
- Discuss lining and finishing requirements with the tailor.

The current Nari18 catalog confirms that the brand offers unstitched suits with custom stitching available on many pieces.


# 20. Tissue and Tailoring

Tissue's crisp and reflective character should be considered during tailoring.

Important considerations include:

- Garment structure
- Lining
- Seam finishing
- Neckline construction
- Embroidery placement
- Border placement
- Hem finishing
- Dupatta finishing

For heavily embroidered Tissue, the tailor should understand where the embroidery and borders are located before cutting.

Do not cut or alter embroidered sections without planning the final garment layout.


# 21. Lining Considerations

Depending on the individual Tissue fabric, lining may affect:

- Opacity
- Comfort
- Drape
- Garment structure
- Colour appearance

Before cutting an unstitched Tissue suit:

1. Examine the fabric under the intended lighting.
2. Check whether additional opacity is required.
3. Select a suitable lining.
4. Consider how the lining will affect the garment's fall.
5. Ensure embroidery and borders remain correctly positioned.

Do not assume that every Nari18 Tissue suit requires the same lining.

# 23. Common Tissue Care Mistakes

## Mistake 1: Machine washing

Tissue should not be treated as an everyday machine-wash fabric.

## Mistake 2: Using hot water

Heat and moisture can be unnecessarily harsh on delicate fabric and decorative work.

## Mistake 3: Scrubbing stains

Aggressive rubbing can affect the surface and embroidery.

## Mistake 4: Ironing directly over zari

Direct high heat can damage or alter delicate decorative areas.

## Mistake 5: Pressing pearls and crystals directly

Decorative elements should be protected from direct heat.

## Mistake 6: Storing while damp

Moisture should be removed before long-term storage.

## Mistake 7: Keeping heavy garments on top

Pressure can crush decorative areas and create unwanted creasing.

## Mistake 8: Using sharp jewellery

Jewellery can snag embroidery, zari, and delicate fabric.

## Mistake 9: Treating every Tissue product identically

Different products may contain different combinations of Tissue, Silk, cotton silk, zari, embroidery, pearls, crystals, borders, and tassels.

# 25. Product-Specific Care Rule

The most important care rule for Nari18 Tissue products is:

**The individual product's care instructions take priority over general Tissue-care guidance.**

Nari18's current Tissue example, the Dusty Rose-Copper Suit, combines several different materials and decorative techniques: Tissue kurta fabric, a Tissue/woven-zari dupatta, cotton silk shantoon bottom, zari, resham embroidery, pearls, crystals, borders, and tassels.

Therefore, care should be based on the actual construction of the product rather than the word "Tissue" alone.

- **Cleaning:** Nari18's general guidance is dry clean only.
- **Machine washing:** Avoid unless the exact product permits it.
- **Stains:** Do not aggressively scrub or experiment with harsh chemicals.
- **Ironing:** Use controlled heat only when permitted and protect decorative areas.
- **Zari:** Protect from friction, moisture, chemicals, and high heat.
- **Embroidery:** Handle carefully during cleaning, wearing, and storage.
