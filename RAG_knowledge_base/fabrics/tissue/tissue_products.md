# Nari18 Tissue Products

## 1. Overview

Nari18 offers Tissue and Tissue Silk unstitched suits as part of its premium festive-wear collection. The current Tissue collection is positioned around **shimmer, festive elegance, structured fabric, and statement detailing**.

Nari18's fabric guide describes Tissue Silk as having a shimmering or metallic finish with a crisp structure and rich appearance. The brand recommends Tissue particularly for weddings, Karwa Chauth, poojas, and evening celebrations. The guide lists a general Tissue price range of approximately ₹3,200–₹6,500, although actual product prices vary by design and can change over time.

The current Nari18 Tissue collection page displays a mixture of Tissue and Tissue-related products. Because the catalog is dynamic, product availability, discounts, ratings, and prices should always be treated as current-site information rather than permanent product attributes.


## 2. Tissue Product Characteristics

Nari18 Tissue products generally emphasize:

- Metallic or luminous surface appearance
- Festive and luxurious visual character
- Crisp or structured fabric behaviour
- Zari and embroidery detailing on selected designs
- Decorative borders and embellished dupattas
- Wedding and celebration-oriented styling
- Unstitched construction that allows custom tailoring
- Combination of Tissue with other fabrics in some suit components

Tissue does not necessarily mean that every component of a suit is made entirely from Tissue. A product may use Tissue for the kurta and/or dupatta while using another fabric, such as cotton silk shantoon, for the bottom. The exact product description should therefore be checked before answering questions about fabric composition.

## 14. Custom Stitching for Tissue Products

Nari18 sells Tissue suits as unstitched fabric sets, allowing customers to tailor the final garment according to measurements and preferred styling.

The current Tissue collection FAQ states that custom stitching can be requested where stitching is offered on an individual product. Customers can select available tailoring options during checkout, while bespoke measurement or styling questions can be discussed with the Nari18 team.

Nari18 also describes its broader custom-tailoring service as made-to-measure stitching through its in-house tailoring team. Customers can discuss neckline, sleeve style, and fit preferences with the design team after ordering.

### Tissue-specific stitching considerations

When recommending a Tissue suit for stitching, consider:

- The placement of embroidery or zari
- Border positioning
- Dupatta border finishing
- Lining requirements
- Desired silhouette
- Sleeve length
- Neckline
- The amount of structure desired in the finished garment

Do not assume that every Tissue design supports every stitching option. Product-level tailoring availability should be verified.
