# Nari18 Tissue Silk Fabric Overview

## 1. Overview

Tissue Silk is one of the premium festive fabric categories represented in the Nari18 collection. Nari18 describes Tissue Silk as a fabric known for its **shimmering metallic finish, crisp structure, and rich festive appearance**.

The current Nari18 catalog shows **11 Tissue designs** under its fabric filter. This count is dynamic and can change as products are added, removed, or become unavailable.

Nari18 positions Tissue primarily for occasions where the customer wants a noticeable festive presence without necessarily relying on extremely heavy embroidery.

### Core characteristics

- Shimmering appearance
- Metallic-like sheen
- Crisp structure
- Rich festive character
- Premium visual presence
- Suitable for statement ethnic wear
- Particularly effective for evening and celebration dressing

Nari18's August 2026 fabric guide describes Tissue Silk as having a **shimmering, metallic finish and festive appeal**, making it particularly appropriate for grand celebrations.


# 2. Tissue Silk at Nari18

Nari18 includes Tissue among its curated fabric categories alongside Silk, Organza, Chanderi, Georgette, Muslin, Cotton, Linen, and other fabrics.

The brand's About page specifically mentions **festive Tissue Silk** as part of its premium unstitched-suit collection.

Tissue is therefore best understood within Nari18's catalog as a **festive, occasion-oriented fabric**, rather than an everyday casual fabric.

The collection is primarily presented through unstitched suit sets, allowing customers to select their preferred silhouette, neckline, sleeve style, fit, and finishing through tailoring.


# 3. What Is Tissue Silk?

Tissue Silk is characterized by a noticeable sheen created by its woven structure. The reflective surface gives the fabric a luminous appearance, particularly under artificial or evening lighting.

At Nari18, Tissue is associated with:

- Festive sheen
- Structured appearance
- Rich visual texture
- Traditional Indian occasion wear
- Elegant metallic effect
- Premium celebration dressing

The term "Tissue Silk" should not automatically be interpreted as meaning that every Tissue product has exactly the same fibre composition.

Individual product descriptions should be used to establish the exact material composition.


# 4. Tissue Silk Appearance

One of the strongest characteristics of Tissue is its visual interaction with light.

The fabric can appear:

- Shimmering
- Lustrous
- Metallic
- Festive
- Rich
- Elegant

This makes Tissue particularly effective for celebrations where the outfit needs to stand out without necessarily requiring very heavy surface embellishment.

Nari18's fabric guide specifically describes Tissue Silk as offering **maximum glamour without heavy embroidery**.

However, individual Nari18 Tissue products can still contain substantial embroidery, zari, beads, crystals, borders, or other decorative elements.


# 5. Tissue Silk Structure and Drape

Tissue generally has a more structured character than very fluid fabrics such as Georgette or Chiffon.

Its crispness can help a tailored garment maintain definition.

This can work well for:

- Straight-cut suits
- Structured kurtas
- A-line silhouettes
- Statement festive silhouettes
- Defined sleeves
- Structured dupattas
- Occasion-focused ethnic wear

The final drape depends on the particular Tissue fabric, fabric blend, embroidery, lining, tailoring method, and garment design.

Therefore, the fabric category alone should not be used to guarantee a particular silhouette.

# 10. Tissue Silk as an Unstitched Suit

Depending on the individual product, a Tissue suit may include:

- Kurta fabric
- Bottom fabric
- Dupatta
- Embroidered panels
- Borders
- Zari work

# 14. Tissue Silk vs Silk

Tissue and Silk both have premium festive associations, but they create different visual effects.

| Attribute | Tissue Silk | Silk |
| Main visual character | Shimmering and luminous | Rich and luxurious |
| Surface appearance | Metallic sheen | Natural/rich sheen |
| Structure | Crisp | Can range from structured to fluid |
| Festive impact | High | High |
| Best use | Grand celebrations and evening occasions | Weddings, festive events, premium ethnic wear |
| Decorative potential | Excellent with zari and embroidery | Excellent with embroidery, motifs, weaving |
| Everyday suitability | Usually low | Depends on Silk type/product |
| Care | Generally delicate; Nari18 guide says dry clean only | Generally delicate; Nari18 recommends dry cleaning |

The exact behaviour depends on the individual fabric and product construction.


# 15. Tissue Silk vs Organza

Both fabrics can create structured festive outfits, but their visual identities differ.

| Attribute | Tissue | Organza |
| Surface | Shimmering/metallic | Crisp with refined sheen |
| Primary effect | Luminous festive appearance | Structured, elegant appearance |
| Typical styling | Grand celebrations | Evening and party wear |
| Embellishment | Zari, embroidery, metallic detailing | Embroidery, prints, decorative work |
| Visual weight | Rich and reflective | Light and airy-looking |
| Best for | Weddings, poojas, festive celebrations | Parties, receptions, evening events |

Nari18's fabric guide places Tissue with wedding and grand celebration dressing, while Organza is positioned more strongly toward evening and party wear.


# 16. Tissue Silk vs Chanderi

Chanderi and Tissue can both be used for festive ethnic wear, but they communicate different styles.

### Tissue

Best when the customer wants:

- Metallic sheen
- Strong festive presence
- Structured appearance
- Wedding or celebration glamour

### Chanderi

Best when the customer wants:

- Lightweight elegance
- Subtle festive sophistication
- Daytime comfort
- Wedding-guest styling

Nari18 describes Chanderi as lightweight luxury suitable for wedding guests, Roka, Mehendi, and festive brunches. Tissue is positioned more toward weddings, poojas, Karwa Chauth, and evening celebrations.
