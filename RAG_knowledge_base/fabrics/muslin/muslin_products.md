# 19. Important Distinction: Muslin vs Muslin Cotton

Nari18's storefront also references **"MUSLIN COTTON SUIT"** in its style/reel content.

This should not automatically be treated as identical to every product classified simply as "Muslin."

For RAG purposes:

- **Muslin** = use when the product or category explicitly identifies Muslin.
- **Muslin Cotton** = preserve the exact product/source terminology.
- **Mul Cotton / Mul-Cotton** = treat as a separate category unless Nari18 explicitly establishes an equivalence.

Do not merge these categories simply because they may all be lightweight or summer-friendly.
