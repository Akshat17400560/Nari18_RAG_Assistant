# Nari18 Muslin Care

## 1. Overview

Muslin is a lightweight, soft, and airy fabric that Nari18 positions for daily wear, office wear, brunches, intimate gatherings, and warm-weather dressing.

Because Muslin is relatively fine and lightweight, proper washing, drying, ironing, and storage help preserve its softness, drape, colour, and overall appearance.

Nari18's general fabric guidance recommends **gentle hand washing** for Muslin. Its summer-fabric guide gives a more specific recommendation of **hand washing or using a delicate cycle, avoiding wringing, and rolling the fabric in a towel to remove excess water**.

Care requirements can vary depending on the exact Muslin product, printing, embroidery, embellishment, lining, and construction. Product-specific care instructions should take priority over general Muslin guidance.


## 2. Basic Muslin Care Rules

For a typical Nari18 Muslin suit:

- Prefer gentle hand washing.
- A delicate washing-machine cycle may be appropriate where the product permits it.
- Use cold or cool water.
- Use a mild detergent.
- Avoid aggressive rubbing.
- Never wring or twist the fabric.
- Remove excess water gently.
- Dry in shade.
- Avoid prolonged direct sunlight.
- Iron from the reverse side when required.
- Use controlled, gentle heat.
- Store the fabric clean and completely dry.
- Take additional care with embroidery and decorative work.

The safest general approach is to treat fine Muslin as a delicate fabric rather than washing it like heavy everyday cotton.


## 3. Hand Washing Muslin

### Recommended approach

1. Fill a clean basin with cool or cold water.
2. Add a small amount of mild detergent.
3. Gently immerse the Muslin fabric.
4. Move the fabric carefully through the water.
5. Avoid vigorous rubbing, brushing, or scrubbing.
6. Rinse gently until detergent is removed.
7. Do not twist or wring the fabric.
8. Remove excess water using gentle pressing.

Hand washing is the preferred general method for delicate Muslin because it gives greater control over mechanical stress placed on the fabric. Nari18 specifically recommends gentle hand washing for Muslin.


## 4. Using a Washing Machine

Nari18's summer guide allows a **delicate cycle** as an alternative to hand washing for Muslin.

If the individual product permits machine washing:

- Use cold or cool water.
- Select a delicate or gentle cycle.
- Use a mild detergent.
- Avoid aggressive spin settings.
- Do not overload the machine.
- Consider placing a delicate Muslin garment inside a laundry bag.
- Remove the garment promptly after the cycle.
- Never assume that every Muslin product is machine washable.

For embroidered, heavily embellished, hand-worked, or particularly fine Muslin, hand washing is generally the safer choice unless the product's care instructions state otherwise.


## 5. Water Temperature

Use **cold or cool water** for Muslin whenever possible.

Avoid hot water because delicate fabrics and decorative elements may be more susceptible to:

- Shrinkage
- Colour changes
- Embroidery distortion
- Thread tension changes
- Fabric deformation

Nari18 specifically recommends cold-water washing for Chikankari suits, which is particularly relevant when the Muslin base carries delicate embroidery.


## 6. Detergent Selection

Use a **mild detergent** for Muslin.

Avoid unnecessarily aggressive cleaning products, particularly for:

- Fine Muslin
- Coloured Muslin
- Printed Muslin
- Embroidered Muslin
- Chikankari Muslin

Do not automatically use bleach, strong stain removers, or harsh detergents.

For Chikankari Muslin, Nari18 specifically recommends cold water with a very mild detergent.


## 7. Avoid Rubbing and Scrubbing

Muslin should not be aggressively rubbed or scrubbed.

Avoid:

- Hard brushing
- Strong rubbing
- Scrubbing embroidery
- Rubbing stains aggressively
- Repeated friction against rough surfaces

Mechanical friction can affect the delicate surface of lightweight fabric and can be particularly damaging to embroidered work.

If a stain is present, gentle localized treatment is preferable to aggressive rubbing. For uncertain or difficult stains, professional cleaning is safer than experimenting with strong chemicals.


## 8. Never Wring Muslin

**Do not wring or twist Muslin.**

Nari18 specifically advises against wringing Muslin and recommends rolling it in a towel to remove excess water.

Wringing can:

- Distort the fabric
- Stretch the material
- Affect the drape
- Put stress on embroidery
- Create unwanted creasing

### Better method

After washing:

1. Support the fabric with both hands.
2. Allow excess water to drain naturally.
3. Place it on a clean towel.
4. Roll the towel gently around the fabric.
5. Press lightly to transfer excess moisture.
6. Unroll carefully.

Do not twist the towel tightly around the garment.


## 9. Drying Muslin

### Recommended drying method

Dry Muslin in a **shaded, well-ventilated area**.

Nari18's Chikankari care guidance specifically recommends drying embroidered Muslin away from direct sunlight.

Avoid prolonged direct sunlight because it can contribute to:

- Colour fading
- Yellowing of light or white embroidery
- Uneven colour exposure

### While drying

- Support the fabric properly.
- Avoid stretching it while wet.
- Do not leave it folded while damp.
- Ensure it dries completely before storage.


## 10. Drying Embroidered Muslin

Extra care is required when Muslin contains embroidery.

For Chikankari Muslin, Nari18 recommends:

- Cold-water washing
- Turning the fabric inside out
- Avoiding wringing
- Drying in shade
- Avoiding direct sunlight

These practices help reduce stress on delicate threadwork.

If a Muslin suit contains substantial embroidery or other embellishment, follow the specific product's care instructions rather than assuming that plain-Muslin care is sufficient.


## 11. Caring for Chikankari Muslin

Muslin is a traditional base for Chikankari embroidery.

Nari18 explains that fine Muslin was historically used for Chikankari because its fine, somewhat transparent structure allowed shadow-work techniques such as **bakhiya** to create distinctive patterns.

Chikankari Muslin therefore requires additional care around the embroidery.

### Recommended care

- Wash by hand in cold water.
- Use a very mild detergent.
- Turn the garment inside out.
- Avoid rubbing the embroidery.
- Never wring.
- Dry in shade.
- Iron from the reverse side.
- Use a cloth between the iron and fabric.
- Consider professional cleaning for heavily embellished pieces.

Nari18 specifically recommends dry cleaning for heavily embellished Chikankari pieces with detailed work such as murri or phanda.


## 12. Turning Muslin Inside Out

Turning an embroidered Muslin garment inside out before washing can help protect the visible embroidery from direct abrasion.

This is particularly useful for:

- Chikankari
- Thread embroidery
- Delicate surface work
- Embellished Muslin

Nari18 specifically recommends turning Chikankari garments inside out before washing to protect surface embroidery and reduce the risk of delicate stitches catching during washing.

For plain Muslin, turning inside out is optional but can still be useful for protecting prints and surface details.


## 13. Ironing Muslin

Muslin generally benefits from gentle ironing rather than aggressive high-temperature pressing.

### Recommended approach

- Iron from the reverse side where possible.
- Use controlled, moderate heat appropriate for the specific fabric.
- Avoid pressing directly over embroidery.
- Use a thin pressing cloth for delicate or embroidered areas.
- Do not leave the hot iron stationary on one area.
- Test an inconspicuous area when uncertain.

For Chikankari Muslin, Nari18 specifically recommends ironing from the reverse side with a cloth between the iron and the fabric.


## 14. Ironing Embroidery

Do not place a hot iron directly over delicate embroidery.

Instead:

1. Turn the garment inside out.
2. Place a clean pressing cloth over the embroidered area.
3. Use controlled heat.
4. Press gently.
5. Avoid dragging the iron aggressively across raised embroidery.

This reduces the risk of flattening, snagging, scorching, or damaging decorative threadwork.


## 15. Steam and Creases

Muslin's lightweight structure can develop creases during washing and storage.

If steam is used:

- Keep the steam setting appropriate for the fabric.
- Avoid excessive heat.
- Do not hold intense heat directly against embroidery.
- Test delicate or decorated areas first.

For valuable or heavily embroidered Muslin, follow the individual product's care instructions instead of assuming that steaming is automatically safe.


## 16. Caring for Printed Muslin

Printed Muslin requires additional attention to colour preservation.

Recommended practices:

- Wash in cold or cool water.
- Use mild detergent.
- Avoid aggressive rubbing.
- Turn the garment inside out when practical.
- Do not soak unnecessarily for long periods.
- Dry in shade.
- Avoid prolonged direct sunlight.

Do not assume that all prints have identical colourfastness. If a product has specific washing instructions, those instructions should take priority.


## 17. Stain Handling

For a fresh stain:

1. Act gently rather than scrubbing.
2. Blot excess moisture with a clean, soft cloth.
3. Avoid rubbing the stain deeply into the fabric.
4. Avoid bleach unless explicitly permitted.
5. Avoid strong household stain removers without checking fabric compatibility.
6. For difficult or valuable embroidered pieces, consider professional cleaning.

The goal is to remove the stain without creating a larger damaged or faded area.


## 18. Muslin Storage

Store Muslin only after it is completely clean and dry.

Recommended storage conditions:

- Cool
- Dry
- Clean
- Away from direct sunlight
- Protected from excessive moisture
- Away from rough or abrasive surfaces

For embroidered Muslin, avoid placing heavy garments directly on top of delicate embroidery.

If the suit is being stored for an extended period, use a breathable fabric cover rather than leaving delicate fabric compressed in plastic for long periods.


## 19. Storing Unstitched Muslin

Unstitched Muslin deserves special attention because the fabric may remain folded for a considerable period before tailoring.

Before storing:

- Check that the fabric is completely dry.
- Keep kurta, bottom, and dupatta pieces together.
- Protect delicate embroidery or printed areas.
- Avoid extremely tight folding.
- Avoid storing in damp areas.
- Recheck the fabric periodically if stored for a long time.

If decorative work lies directly across a fold, avoid repeatedly creating a sharp crease through the same embroidered area.

## 20. Muslin Before Custom Stitching

If an unstitched Muslin suit will be custom stitched:

1. Inspect the entire fabric before cutting.
2. Identify embroidery, prints, borders, and decorative areas.
3. Check the usable fabric width and length.
4. Decide whether lining is required.
5. Confirm the desired neckline and sleeve design.
6. Plan the placement of borders or motifs.
7. Keep the care requirements in mind when selecting the final construction.

Nari18 provides custom stitching for its unstitched suit range, with measurements and styling coordinated through its tailoring service.


## 21. Lining Considerations

Fine Muslin may require a lining or cotton slip when additional opacity is desired.

Nari18 specifically recommends cotton slips or linings for Muslin kurtas where additional coverage is needed.

Lining can be particularly relevant for:

- Light-coloured Muslin
- Fine or translucent Muslin
- Daytime wear
- Office wear
- Designs requiring greater coverage

The exact lining choice should depend on the individual fabric and desired finished garment.


## 22. Common Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid these common Muslin-care mistakes:

### 1. Using hot water

Hot water can be unnecessarily harsh on delicate Muslin and decorative work.

### 2. Wringing the fabric

Twisting can distort the fabric and affect its drape.

### 3. Scrubbing embroidery

Aggressive rubbing can damage delicate threadwork.

### 4. Drying in harsh sunlight

Extended direct sunlight can fade colours and affect light embroidery.

### 5. Using strong detergent

Harsh detergents can be unnecessarily aggressive toward delicate fabric and coloured or embroidered work.

### 6. Ironing directly over embroidery

Direct high heat can damage or flatten decorative threadwork.

### 7. Storing damp fabric

Moisture trapped in folded fabric can create unpleasant odours and storage problems.

### 8. Assuming every Muslin suit has identical care requirements

Different products can contain different embroidery, printing, blends, or construction details.


## 23. Plain Muslin vs Embroidered Muslin

| Care Area | Plain Muslin | Embroidered / Chikankari Muslin |
| Washing | Gentle hand wash | Gentle hand wash preferred |
| Water | Cold/cool | Cold |
| Detergent | Mild | Very mild |
| Rubbing | Avoid aggressive rubbing | Avoid rubbing embroidery |
| Wringing | Do not wring | Strictly avoid |
| Drying | Shade preferred | Shade, away from direct sunlight |
| Ironing | Gentle controlled heat | Reverse side with pressing cloth |
| Storage | Clean and dry | Extra protection for embroidery |
| Professional cleaning | Product-dependent | Consider for heavily embellished pieces |

Nari18's published care guidance supports gentle handling for both Muslin generally and embroidered Chikankari Muslin specifically.


## 24. When Professional Cleaning Is Preferable

Professional cleaning can be the safer option when the Muslin suit contains:

- Heavy embroidery
- Murri work
- Phanda work
- Extensive embellishment
- Delicate decorative details
- Difficult stains
- Special fabric blends

Nari18 specifically recommends dry cleaning for heavily embellished Chikankari pieces.

Do not automatically dry clean every plain Muslin product if the product's own care instructions permit gentle washing.


## 25. Quick Muslin Care Checklist

### Before washing

- Check the product's care instructions.
- Separate colours where necessary.
- Turn embroidered garments inside out.
- Use cold/cool water.
- Prepare mild detergent.

### During washing

- Wash gently.
- Avoid scrubbing.
- Avoid aggressive agitation.
- Never wring or twist.

### After washing

- Remove excess water gently.
- Roll in a towel if needed.
- Dry in shade.
- Avoid prolonged direct sunlight.

### Before storage

- Ensure the fabric is completely dry.
- Fold carefully.
- Protect embroidery.
- Store in a clean, dry environment.

### Before ironing

- Check the fabric's care instructions.
- Use gentle controlled heat.
- Turn embroidered pieces inside out.
- Use a pressing cloth over delicate work.

## 27. Summary

Nari18's Muslin care philosophy is based on **gentle washing, minimal mechanical stress, careful drying, and controlled ironing**.

The safest general routine is:

**Cold/cool water → mild detergent → gentle hand wash → no wringing → towel-roll for excess water → dry in shade → gentle reverse-side ironing → clean, dry storage.**

For embroidered or Chikankari Muslin, additional care is required. Nari18 recommends turning the garment inside out, using cold water and mild detergent, avoiding wringing, drying away from direct sunlight, and ironing on the reverse side with a cloth between the iron and fabric. Heavily embellished pieces may be better suited to professional dry cleaning.

The individual product's care instructions should always override general Muslin-care guidance.
