# Nari18 Silk Care Guide

## Overview

Silk is a delicate premium fabric that requires more careful handling than everyday fabrics such as Cotton, Mul-Cotton or Muslin.

Nari18's Silk collection includes premium unstitched suits intended for festive occasions, weddings and formal ethnic wear. Proper care helps preserve the fabric's appearance, colour, texture, sheen and decorative details.

The exact care method can vary depending on the Silk type, weave, dye, embroidery, embellishment, lining and overall product construction. Therefore, product-specific care instructions should always take priority when they are provided.

## Recommended Silk Cleaning Method

For Nari18 Silk products, professional dry cleaning is generally the safer care option, particularly for premium, embroidered or embellished pieces.

Silk should not automatically be treated like regular washable cotton.

### Why Dry Cleaning Is Preferred

Professional dry cleaning can help reduce the risk of:

- Colour bleeding
- Loss of sheen
- Fabric distortion
- Shrinkage
- Damage to embroidery
- Damage to decorative details
- Water marks
- Uneven drying

For expensive or heavily decorated Silk suits, professional cleaning is especially appropriate.

## Avoid Routine Machine Washing

Machine washing should generally be avoided for Silk unless the individual product's care instructions specifically state that machine washing is permitted.

Machine washing can expose Silk to:

- Excessive agitation
- Friction
- Temperature changes
- Strong detergents
- Spinning
- Potential colour transfer

These factors can affect the appearance and structure of delicate Silk.

For Nari18 Silk products, customers should check the specific product-care instructions before deciding how to wash or clean the garment.

## Hand Washing

Hand washing should not automatically be assumed to be safe for every Nari18 Silk product.

If the individual product explicitly permits hand washing, use a very gentle approach:

1. Use cold or cool water.
2. Use a mild detergent specifically suitable for delicate fabrics.
3. Avoid vigorous rubbing.
4. Do not scrub embroidered or embellished areas.
5. Do not twist or wring the fabric.
6. Rinse gently.
7. Remove excess water carefully.
8. Dry according to the product's care instructions.

For valuable Silk suits, professional dry cleaning remains the safer general recommendation unless the product instructions specify otherwise.

## Water Temperature

Avoid hot water when caring for Silk.

Hot water can increase the risk of:

- Shrinkage
- Colour changes
- Fabric distortion
- Loss of surface appearance

When washing is specifically permitted, cool water is generally preferable.

## Detergent Selection

Strong detergents should be avoided on delicate Silk.

Avoid products containing aggressive cleaning agents unless the product-care instructions specifically recommend them.

A detergent intended for delicate fabrics is generally more appropriate for Silk that is confirmed to be washable.

Do not assume that a detergent safe for Cotton is automatically suitable for Silk.

## Do Not Rub Silk Aggressively

Silk fibers can be damaged by excessive friction.

Avoid:

- Scrubbing
- Hard brushing
- Repeated rubbing
- Rough handling
- Abrasive cleaning

This is particularly important around:

- Embroidery
- Zari
- Printed sections
- Decorative necklines
- Borders
- Embellishments

## Do Not Wring Silk

Silk should not be twisted or wrung aggressively to remove water.

Wringing can:

- Distort the fabric
- Create permanent creases
- Stress delicate fibers
- Affect embroidery
- Change the garment's shape

If washing is permitted, excess water should be removed gently rather than by twisting the garment.

## Drying Silk

Silk should generally be handled carefully while drying.

### Avoid Direct Harsh Sunlight

Prolonged exposure to strong direct sunlight can contribute to:

- Colour fading
- Uneven fading
- Changes in fabric appearance

Dry Silk according to the product's care instructions and avoid unnecessary prolonged exposure to intense sunlight.

### Avoid High Heat

Do not use excessive heat to dry Silk.

Avoid placing Silk directly against high-temperature heaters or other intense heat sources.

### Maintain Shape

Wet or damp Silk can be more vulnerable to distortion.

Handle it carefully and follow the product-specific drying instructions.

## Ironing Silk

Silk can be sensitive to high heat.

If ironing is permitted:

1. Use a low-temperature Silk setting.
2. Avoid excessive pressure.
3. Turn the garment inside out when appropriate.
4. Use a clean pressing cloth if recommended.
5. Avoid repeatedly pressing the same area.
6. Take extra care around embroidery and embellishments.

A steamer may be preferable for some Silk garments, but the product's care instructions should determine whether steaming is appropriate.

## Ironing Embroidered Silk

Embroidered Silk requires additional care.

Avoid placing a hot iron directly over:

- Embroidery
- Sequins
- Zari
- Beads
- Kundan-style work
- Decorative borders
- Raised embellishments

Direct heat and pressure can damage decorative work or flatten raised elements.

When ironing is permitted, work from the reverse side where appropriate and use protective fabric between the iron and delicate areas.

## Silk and Embroidery Care

Nari18 Silk suits may contain different types of decorative work depending on the individual design.

Examples can include:

- Hand embroidery
- Zari-style detailing
- Kundan-style neckline work
- Decorative borders
- Traditional motifs
- Printed or woven designs

Care requirements can therefore differ considerably between two Silk products.

A heavily embroidered Silk suit should receive more conservative care than a simple, lightly decorated Silk fabric.

## Silk and Colour Protection

Silk colours can be sensitive to washing, moisture, chemicals and prolonged sunlight.

To help protect colour:

- Avoid unnecessary washing.
- Follow the product-specific cleaning instructions.
- Avoid strong detergents.
- Avoid prolonged direct sunlight.
- Store away from excessive moisture.
- Do not expose the fabric unnecessarily to perfumes or chemical products.

For a new or dark-coloured Silk item, do not assume that colourfastness is identical to Cotton.

## Perfume, Deodorant and Cosmetics

Perfume, deodorant, hair products and cosmetics should preferably be applied before wearing the Silk garment and allowed to dry.

Direct contact between chemicals and Silk may result in:

- Staining
- Discolouration
- Surface damage

This is especially important for premium or light-coloured Silk.

## Removing Small Stains

Do not aggressively rub a stain on Silk.

Instead:

1. Stop the source of the stain.
2. Avoid rubbing the affected area.
3. Gently blot where appropriate.
4. Do not apply household stain removers without confirming that they are safe for Silk.
5. For significant or difficult stains, consult a professional dry cleaner.

For embroidered or embellished Silk, professional cleaning is generally preferable.

## Storing Silk

Correct storage is important when Silk is not being used regularly.

### Keep Silk Clean Before Long-Term Storage

Do not store a garment with stains, sweat or food residue.

Residual substances can become more difficult to remove during long-term storage.

### Protect From Moisture

Store Silk in a clean, dry environment.

Excessive humidity can contribute to:

- Odour
- Mildew
- Fabric deterioration
- Damage to decorative work

### Avoid Excessive Compression

Silk should not be permanently compressed under heavy garments.

Give delicate pieces sufficient space so that embroidery and embellishments are not crushed.

## Folding Silk

Unstitched Silk fabric should be folded carefully when stored.

For heavily embroidered or embellished pieces:

- Avoid sharp permanent folds across decorative areas.
- Refold the fabric periodically if it is stored for a long time.
- Avoid placing heavy objects on top of the fabric.
- Keep delicate embellishments from rubbing against rough surfaces.

## Hanging Silk

Some finished Silk garments can be stored on suitable padded hangers, but hanging is not automatically appropriate for every Silk construction.

Very heavy embroidered garments may stretch under their own weight if stored incorrectly for long periods.

For unstitched fabric, careful folding is generally more practical than prolonged hanging.

## Storage Bags

Use a clean, breathable storage solution where appropriate.

Avoid storing Silk for long periods in environments that trap moisture.

The storage method should also protect the fabric from:

- Dust
- Direct sunlight
- Excess humidity
- Rough surfaces
- Insects

## Silk and Insects

Natural fibers can attract certain fabric pests, particularly when garments are stored for long periods.

Good storage practices include:

- Store garments clean.
- Keep storage areas dry.
- Inspect stored Silk periodically.
- Avoid damp storage.
- Keep the storage area clean.

Do not apply chemical insect repellents directly to Silk unless the product and chemical are confirmed to be compatible.

## Care Before First Use

Before wearing a new Nari18 Silk suit:

1. Check the product's care instructions.
2. Identify whether the garment contains embroidery or embellishments.
3. Check whether the fabric is recommended for dry cleaning.
4. Avoid unnecessary washing before first use.
5. Store the fabric safely if tailoring is planned later.

If the product is purchased unstitched, care should also be taken while transporting and storing the fabric before it is sent for tailoring.

## Care of Unstitched Silk

Nari18's Silk collection primarily includes unstitched suit fabrics.

Unstitched Silk should be protected from:

- Moisture
- Excessive friction
- Dirt
- Food and cosmetic stains
- Direct sunlight
- Rough handling
- Sharp objects

Before custom stitching, keep the fabric clean and dry.

Do not wash the fabric simply to "pre-shrink" it unless a qualified tailor or the product's care instructions specifically recommend doing so.

## Silk Before Custom Stitching

If a customer purchases an unstitched Silk suit and plans to use Nari18's custom stitching service or an external tailor:

- Keep the fabric protected until tailoring.
- Do not expose it unnecessarily to water.
- Keep all components together.
- Preserve the original packaging and tags where applicable.
- Share any product-specific care instructions with the tailor.
- Make sure the tailor knows if the fabric contains delicate embroidery or embellishment.

The tailoring process can involve cutting, pressing and handling that should be appropriate for delicate Silk.

## Silk After Custom Stitching

Once a Silk suit has been stitched, care requirements remain dependent on the fabric and decorative work.

The finished garment should be handled according to the most delicate component.

For example, if a Silk suit has delicate embroidery, the care method should account for both the Silk and the embroidery.

Custom stitching does not automatically make Silk machine-washable.

## Silk and Zari

Silk garments may contain zari or metallic decorative work depending on the product.

Zari can be sensitive to:

- Excess moisture
- Strong detergents
- Abrasion
- High heat
- Rough ironing

For Silk products containing zari, professional cleaning is generally safer than aggressive home washing.

## Silk and Kundan or Embellishment

Some Nari18 Silk designs may include decorative neckline or embellished elements.

Such areas should not be:

- Scrubbed
- Soaked unnecessarily
- Ironed directly
- Brushed aggressively
- Exposed to strong cleaning chemicals

Professional cleaning should be considered for heavily embellished pieces.

## Common Silk Care Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Treating Silk Like Cotton

Silk generally needs more delicate handling than everyday Cotton.

### Mistake 2: Machine Washing Without Checking

A washing-machine cycle can expose Silk to excessive agitation and friction.

### Mistake 3: Using Hot Water

Hot water can increase the risk of shrinkage and colour changes.

### Mistake 4: Using Strong Detergent

Aggressive detergents can affect delicate Silk fibers and surface appearance.

### Mistake 5: Scrubbing Stains

Rubbing can spread the stain and damage the fabric.

### Mistake 6: Direct High-Heat Ironing

Excessive heat can damage Silk and decorative elements.

### Mistake 7: Storing While Damp

Moisture trapped during storage can cause odour and fabric deterioration.

### Mistake 8: Ignoring Embroidery

A Silk suit with embroidery or embellishment needs additional care beyond basic Silk care.

### Mistake 9: Using Household Stain Removers

A chemical that works on Cotton may not be appropriate for Silk.

### Mistake 10: Assuming Every Silk Is Identical

Different Silk varieties, blends, weights, dyes and constructions can require different care.

## Silk Care for Different Product Types

### Plain or Lightly Decorated Silk

These products may be simpler to maintain than heavily embellished Silk, but they should still be treated as delicate fabrics.

### Embroidered Silk

Use more conservative cleaning and ironing methods.

### Zari Silk

Avoid unnecessary moisture and friction and follow product-specific care instructions.

### Embellished Silk

Professional cleaning is preferable when the garment contains delicate decorative components.

### Silk Dupatta

Silk dupattas can be particularly vulnerable to snagging and abrasion.

Avoid:

- Rough surfaces
- Jewellery that catches fabric
- Sharp accessories
- Excessive friction

### Silk Bottom Fabric

Store folded carefully and avoid placing heavy objects over the fabric.

## Travel Care for Silk

When travelling with a Silk suit:

- Keep the fabric protected from rough surfaces.
- Avoid overpacking.
- Prevent sharp objects from contacting the fabric.
- Keep Silk away from damp clothing.
- Use clean protective packaging.
- Avoid leaving Silk exposed to direct sunlight for long periods.

For heavily embroidered Silk, additional padding may help prevent decorative elements from being crushed.

## When to Consult a Professional

Professional cleaning or textile-care assistance is recommended when:

- The garment has a difficult stain.
- The Silk is heavily embroidered.
- The garment contains zari or embellishments.
- The colour appears unstable.
- The fabric has been exposed to chemicals.
- The garment has suffered water damage.
- The fabric has become distorted.
- The customer is uncertain about the correct cleaning method.

## Nari18 Product-Specific Care

Nari18's general Silk guidance should not replace care information supplied with an individual product.

Before providing a definitive care instruction for a particular Silk suit, verify:

- Product name
- Silk type
- Fabric composition
- Embroidery
- Embellishment
- Product-specific care instructions

If the product page provides specific care instructions, those instructions should take precedence over this general guide.

## RAG Usage Notes

When answering customer questions about Silk care:

1. Recommend checking the specific product's care instructions first.
2. Do not claim that every Nari18 Silk product must be dry-cleaned unless the individual product instructions state this.
3. Use professional dry cleaning as the safer general recommendation for delicate, embroidered or embellished Silk.
4. Do not guarantee that a Silk product is machine-washable.
5. Do not recommend hot-water washing.
6. Do not recommend aggressive scrubbing or wringing.
7. Do not recommend high-temperature ironing.
8. Do not claim that all Silk products have identical care requirements.
9. Do not assume Banarasi, Ikat, Tussar or other Silk varieties have exactly the same care instructions.
10. Do not assume that a Silk blend has the same care requirements as pure Silk.
11. For stains, avoid prescribing aggressive chemical treatments without knowing the exact fabric and stain.
12. For zari, Kundan, embroidery or other embellishments, recommend extra care.
13. Do not guarantee that a stain can be removed without affecting the fabric.
14. Do not use general care guidance to override product-specific instructions.
15. When the customer asks about a current product, verify the current product page where possible.
16. Do not confuse care instructions with Nari18's return or exchange policy.
17. Custom stitching does not change the basic care requirements of the underlying Silk fabric.

## RAG Summary

Nari18 Silk products should generally be treated as delicate premium garments.

The safest general approach is:

**Clean carefully → avoid unnecessary washing → avoid hot water and strong detergents → avoid rubbing and wringing → use low heat if ironing is permitted → protect embroidery and embellishments → store clean and dry → follow product-specific care instructions.**

For expensive, embroidered or embellished Silk suits, professional dry cleaning is generally the preferred conservative option.

The individual Nari18 product's care instructions should always take priority over this general Silk-care guide.
