# Nari18 Fabrics Overview

## Overview

Nari18 offers premium unstitched suits and ethnic wear built around a diverse range of fabrics. The brand's catalog is strongly fabric-led, allowing customers to choose suits according to fabric characteristics, occasion, season, appearance, comfort, embroidery, and desired styling.

The current Nari18 catalog provides fabric-based filtering and lists categories including Silk, Organza, Tissue, Muslin, Cotton, Linen, Georgette, Chanderi, Crepe, Chiffon, Lycra Net, and Mul-Cotton. The catalog also contains broader categories such as Cotton Suits and Linen Suits. These categories and their product counts are dynamic and may change as products are added, sold out, or removed.

Nari18 describes its products as premium unstitched suits and ethnic wear using handcrafted fabrics, with custom stitching available on many products.

## Fabric and Occasion Guidance

Different fabrics can create very different looks and levels of comfort. Customers should generally select fabric according to the occasion and the desired appearance.

### Silk

Silk is suited to premium, festive, formal, and wedding-oriented clothing. It can provide a rich appearance and works particularly well with embroidery, handcrafted detailing, and elegant traditional designs.

Typical use cases:

- Weddings
- Festive celebrations
- Formal ethnic events
- Premium evening wear
- Special occasions

Nari18's current catalog includes multiple silk-based suits, including embroidered and premium silk designs.

### Organza

Organza is generally associated with a lightweight, structured and dressy appearance. It is suitable for statement outfits, festive events, parties, and evening occasions.

Typical use cases:

- Parties
- Festive events
- Evening functions
- Wedding guest outfits
- Statement ethnic looks

Nari18 currently has a dedicated Organza category and lists multiple organza suit designs in its catalog.

### Tissue

Tissue fabrics are commonly selected for their sheen and dressier appearance. They can work well for festive and occasion wear where a more polished or luminous look is desired.

Typical use cases:

- Festive celebrations
- Wedding functions
- Evening occasions
- Formal ethnic styling

The Nari18 catalog currently contains a dedicated Tissue fabric category.

### Muslin

Muslin is a lightweight fabric that can be suitable for comfortable everyday and warm-weather ethnic wear. Nari18 includes Muslin among its fabric categories and has multiple Muslin suit designs.

Typical use cases:

- Everyday wear
- Daytime outfits
- Summer wear
- Comfortable casual ethnic styling

Nari18's fabric guidance also discusses Muslin in the context of summer ethnic suits.

### Cotton

Cotton is a practical choice for breathable, comfortable and versatile ethnic wear. Cotton suits can be appropriate for everyday use, office wear, daytime occasions and warmer weather.

Typical use cases:

- Daily wear
- Office wear
- Casual outings
- Summer wear
- Comfortable daytime ethnic outfits

Nari18 currently maintains a Cotton Suits category in its catalog.

### Mul-Cotton

Mul-Cotton combines the comfort associated with cotton-based fabrics with a lightweight feel suitable for everyday and warm-weather clothing.

Typical use cases:

- Summer wear
- Daily wear
- Casual outings
- Comfortable daytime styling

Mul-Cotton is currently represented as a separate fabric category in the Nari18 catalog.

### Linen

Linen is valued for its lightweight and breathable character and can be suitable for polished daytime and summer outfits.

Typical use cases:

- Summer wear
- Office wear
- Daytime occasions
- Smart casual ethnic styling

Nari18 currently provides a dedicated Linen Suits category.

### Georgette

Georgette is commonly used for fluid, elegant and festive silhouettes. Its drape makes it suitable for outfits where movement and a softer fall are desirable.

Typical use cases:

- Festive wear
- Evening events
- Wedding guest outfits
- Party wear
- Flowing ethnic silhouettes

Nari18 currently lists Georgette as a separate catalog fabric category.

### Chanderi

Chanderi is associated with traditional Indian textile aesthetics and can work particularly well for elegant festive and daytime ethnic wear.

Typical use cases:

- Festive occasions
- Wedding guest wear
- Daytime celebrations
- Traditional ethnic styling

Nari18's blog specifically includes Chanderi in its summer-fabric guidance, while the catalog provides a dedicated Chanderi category.

### Crepe

Crepe is a versatile fabric category that can be used for dressier ethnic outfits and evening styling. Nari18 currently lists Crape/Crepe as a separate catalog category.

Typical use cases:

- Party wear
- Evening wear
- Contemporary ethnic styling
- Dressier suit designs


### Chiffon

Chiffon is lightweight and suitable for soft, flowing ethnic looks. It can be appropriate for festive and evening outfits where a lighter drape is preferred.

Typical use cases:

- Festive occasions
- Evening events
- Party wear
- Lightweight ethnic styling

Nari18 currently lists Chiffon as a separate fabric category.

### Lycra Net

Lycra Net appears as a specialized fabric category in the Nari18 catalog. Product-level characteristics should be checked on the individual listing because the exact construction, design and intended use can vary.

The current catalog lists Lycra Net as a separate fabric filter.

## Fabric and Styling

Fabric influences the overall appearance and construction of a suit.

A lightweight fabric may create a softer and more relaxed silhouette, while structured or dressier fabrics can create a more formal appearance. Embroidery, printing, borders, zari work, embellishments and dupatta construction can further change the final look.

For Nari18 products, customers should evaluate the complete product listing rather than selecting a fabric in isolation. The product page can provide the specific design, fabric, components, variants, images, price and availability.

## Fabric and Custom Stitching

Nari18 offers custom stitching on many catalog products. Customers can therefore select an unstitched fabric-based suit and have it converted into a finished garment when the product supports stitching.

The catalog states that custom stitching is available on many products and directs customers to the checkout flow or Nari18 support for stitching-related requirements.

Custom stitching can be particularly useful when the customer wants to control aspects such as:

- Garment measurements
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Overall silhouette

Not every product necessarily supports identical customization. Product-specific stitching availability should therefore be verified before making a recommendation.

## Fabric Care

Fabric care requirements vary significantly between materials.

Customers should check the care instructions associated with the specific product before washing, ironing or storing it.

Nari18's blog currently provides fabric-care guidance covering materials such as:

- Cotton
- Silk
- Organza
- Chanderi
- Georgette
- Embellished fabrics

The guidance emphasizes that different fabrics require different washing, ironing and storage approaches.

Delicate, embroidered or embellished fabrics should not automatically be treated in the same way as everyday cotton fabrics.

## Fabric Category vs Product Category

A fabric category describes the primary fabric classification used to organize products.

A product category can describe a broader shopping concept such as:

- Unstitched suits
- Cotton suits
- Georgette suits
- Organza suits
- Designer unstitched suits
- Ethnic wear
- Custom stitching

Nari18's catalog uses both product-oriented and fabric-oriented organization, allowing customers to discover products through multiple paths.

## Important RAG Rules

When answering questions about Nari18 fabrics:

1. Do not assume that every product in a fabric category has the same design, components, embroidery or construction.
2. Do not invent fabric composition when the individual product page does not specify it.
3. Do not treat fabric categories as permanent inventory lists.
4. Do not assume that every fabric supports the same stitching or customization options.
5. Use the individual product page as the source of truth for exact fabric details.
6. Do not invent current price, discount, stock status or product availability.
7. Fabric suitability for an occasion should be presented as guidance, not as a strict Nari18 rule.
8. Do not claim that a fabric is suitable for a particular climate without considering the specific garment construction and local      weather.
9. For care questions, recommend checking the product-specific care instructions when available.
10. If a user asks for current products made from a particular fabric, retrieve current catalog/product information instead of relying on this overview.
11. Product counts shown in the catalog are dynamic and should not be treated as permanent facts.
12. When recommending fabric, consider the customer's occasion, season, comfort requirements, desired appearance and whether custom stitching is available.
