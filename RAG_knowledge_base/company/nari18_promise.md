# Nari18 Promise

## 1. Customer Trust

Nari18 emphasizes simple and transparent policies so customers understand what is covered before placing an order.

The brand focuses on:

- Clear exchange guidelines
- Support for eligible product issues
- Guidance for custom stitching and sizing
- Customer support for order and product questions

## 2. Manufacturing Defects

Nari18 accepts exchange requests for **manufacturing defects within 7 days**.

Customers may be asked to provide clear photographs or videos so the team can verify the issue before approving an exchange.

Once approved, Nari18 guides the customer through the exchange process.

## 3. Custom Stitching

Custom-stitched orders are made-to-measure and **are not eligible for exchange**.

Nari18 encourages customers who need sizing assistance to seek guidance before ordering to help achieve the desired fit.

## 4. Customer Support

Customers can contact Nari18 for assistance with:

- Order-related questions
- Exchange eligibility
- Product guidance
- Sizing assistance
- Custom-stitching questions

## 5. Promise in One Sentence

**Nari18 aims to provide a transparent and personalized shopping experience backed by clear policies, product guidance, and customer support.**
