# Nari18 Company Facts

## Basic Company Information

- **Brand Name:** Nari18
- **Founder:** Richa Singh
- **Founded:** 2018
- **Founded In:** Gurugram, Haryana, India
- **Business Type:** Premium ethnic-wear boutique and online retailer
- **Primary Focus:** Unstitched suits, ethnic wear, premium fabrics, and customized tailoring
- **Primary Customer Segment:** Women looking for premium Indian ethnic wear and personalized-fit clothing
- **Physical Presence:** Gurugram, Haryana
- **Online Presence:** Nari18.com
- **Geographic Online Reach:** India

Nari18 was founded by Richa Singh in Gurugram in 2018. The company operates as a premium destination for unstitched suits, ethnic wear, handcrafted fabrics, and customized tailoring.

## Artisan and Craft Heritage

Nari18 emphasizes Indian textile traditions and artisan craftsmanship.

The company states that its fabrics and collections are sourced from Indian artisan communities and traditional craft clusters.

Craft traditions highlighted by Nari18 include:

- Lucknow Chikankari
- Chanderi weaving
- Jaipur hand-block printing
- Handcrafted textiles
- Traditional embroidery
- Hand-painted fabrics

Nari18 specifically describes Chikankari pieces as coming from Lucknow embroidery clusters and Chanderi fabrics as being woven by families in Madhya Pradesh with generations of weaving experience. The brand also describes its hand-block-printed cotton collections as being produced in Jaipur using carved wooden blocks.

## Custom Stitching

Customized tailoring is one of Nari18's core offerings.

Nari18 states that customized stitching is available for its orders where the product supports the service.

The company's in-house tailoring service provides made-to-measure ethnic wear.

Customers can communicate their tailoring preferences to the Nari18 design team, including:

- Measurements
- Neckline preference
- Sleeve style
- Fit preference
- Silhouette preference
- Other styling requirements

Nari18 instructs customers who purchase an eligible unstitched suit to contact its design team through WhatsApp after ordering for custom-stitching discussions.

## Common Custom-Stitching Measurements

Nari18's tailoring guidance identifies the following measurements as important for made-to-measure ethnic wear:

- Bust
- Waist
- Hip
- Shoulder width
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Bottom inseam

Nari18 recommends taking measurements while wearing the undergarment that will normally be worn with the outfit and avoiding measurement over thick clothing.

## Custom-Stitching Silhouettes

Nari18's tailoring guidance discusses several commonly used silhouettes, including:

- Straight-cut kurta with straight-leg salwar
- A-line kurta with churidar
- Flared Anarkali-style kurta with palazzo
- Short kurta with sharara

The appropriate silhouette depends on factors such as body shape, occasion, and personal preference.
