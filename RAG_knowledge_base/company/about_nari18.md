# About Nari18

## About the Brand

Nari18 is a premium ethnic-wear boutique founded by Richa Singh in 2018 in Gurugram, Haryana.

The brand specializes in unstitched suits, handcrafted fabrics, Indian ethnic wear, and customized tailoring. Nari18 combines traditional Indian textile craftsmanship with contemporary styling and personalized fitting.

The brand's approach is based on the idea that an outfit should begin with a high-quality fabric and be completed with a fit and silhouette that suits the individual wearer.

## The Nari18 Story

Nari18 began from a passion for India's rich textile heritage.

What started in Gurugram has developed into a boutique focused on handcrafted ethnic wear, premium unstitched suit sets, and bespoke stitching. The brand's collections are designed around the relationship between fabric, craftsmanship, personal style, and fit.

Nari18 does not position an unstitched suit simply as fabric to be purchased and later tailored. Instead, the brand presents the unstitched format as an opportunity for customers to participate in creating their own garment.

Customers can select a fabric and design and then customize the resulting garment around their measurements, preferred neckline, sleeve style, silhouette, and overall styling preferences.

## Philosophy

Nari18's philosophy can be summarized through three central ideas:

1. **Quality Fabric**
2. **Indian Craftsmanship**
3. **Personalized Fit**

The brand believes that the quality and character of the fabric are fundamental to the finished outfit.

The second part of the philosophy is craftsmanship. Nari18 highlights traditional Indian textile and embroidery practices and sources collections from artisan communities associated with established craft traditions.

The third part is fit. Through custom stitching and made-to-measure tailoring, Nari18 aims to transform an unstitched suit into a garment designed around the individual customer.

## Indian Textile Heritage

Indian textile heritage is central to the Nari18 brand identity.

The company highlights traditional craft communities and techniques from different parts of India rather than treating ethnic wear as a single uniform category.

Examples highlighted by Nari18 include:

- Lucknow Chikankari embroidery
- Chanderi weaving
- Jaipur hand-block printing
- Traditional embroidery
- Handcrafted textiles
- Hand-painted fabrics

These traditions provide the foundation for different collections offered by the brand.

## Connection With Artisans

Nari18 states that its pieces are sourced directly from Indian master artisans.

The brand specifically highlights its connection with:

- Chikankari embroiderers in Lucknow
- Silk weavers in Chanderi
- Hand-block printers in Jaipur
- Other Indian artisan communities involved in traditional textile crafts

Nari18's Journal describes its Chikankari collections as being sourced from Lucknow embroidery clusters, where artisans create hand embroidery on fabrics including Georgette, Muslin, and Cotton.

The brand also describes Chanderi fabrics as being produced by weaving families in Madhya Pradesh and hand-block-printed cotton as being produced in Jaipur using carved wooden blocks.

## Chikankari and Nari18

Chikankari is one of the traditional craft forms prominently associated with Nari18's collections.

Nari18 describes Chikankari as a hand-embroidery tradition from Lucknow that uses intricate threadwork to create floral, paisley, botanical, and other decorative patterns.

The brand emphasizes the distinction between genuine hand embroidery and machine-produced imitation.

Nari18's Chikankari collections include fabrics such as:

- Georgette
- Muslin
- Cotton

The brand describes authentic hand-done Chikankari as involving individual artisans working stitch by stitch rather than relying on machine-generated patterns.

## Chanderi and Nari18

Chanderi is another important textile tradition highlighted by Nari18.

The brand associates Chanderi fabrics with weaving communities in Madhya Pradesh and presents Chanderi as a fabric suitable for elegant and festive ethnic wear.

Nari18's fabric philosophy emphasizes the relationship between traditional weaving and contemporary styling, allowing heritage textiles to be incorporated into modern silhouettes.

## Hand Block Printing

Nari18 also incorporates hand-block-printed textiles into its collections.

The brand describes its hand-block-printed cotton as being produced in Jaipur using carved wooden blocks.

Because the printing process involves manual application of the blocks, individual pieces can contain subtle differences.

Nari18 presents these variations as part of the character of handcrafted textiles rather than as defects.

## Unstitched Clothing Philosophy

Unstitched clothing is central to the Nari18 business model.

An unstitched suit generally provides:

- Kurta fabric
- Bottom fabric
- Dupatta

Instead of purchasing a finished garment in a standardized size, the customer can have the fabric cut and stitched according to individual measurements.

Nari18 positions this format as providing greater control over:

- Fit
- Length
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Silhouette
- Styling
- Overall garment construction

The brand therefore treats the unstitched suit as a foundation for personalization rather than simply a finished product waiting to be stitched.

## Personalized Tailoring

Customization is a major part of the Nari18 experience.

The company provides in-house custom tailoring and made-to-measure stitching.

After purchasing an eligible unstitched suit, customers can contact the Nari18 design team through WhatsApp to discuss their requirements.

The customization conversation can include:

- Body measurements
- Neckline preference
- Sleeve preference
- Fit
- Silhouette
- Other styling requirements

Nari18's goal is to turn the selected fabric into a personalized garment with a boutique-style finish.

## Modern Styling With Traditional Craft

Nari18 combines traditional Indian textiles with contemporary clothing preferences.

The brand's collections are not limited to a single type of ethnic silhouette or occasion.

Its fabric and product guidance covers different use cases, including:

- Everyday ethnic wear
- Office wear
- Casual wear
- Festive occasions
- Weddings
- Evening events
- Party wear
- Family celebrations

This allows traditional fabrics and craft techniques to be used in both conventional and contemporary wardrobes.

## Fabric as the Starting Point

Nari18 places significant emphasis on fabric selection.

Different fabrics are presented according to their characteristics, appearance, comfort, drape, and suitability for particular occasions.

Examples include:

- Cotton and Mul-Cotton for comfort and everyday wear
- Linen for breathable and polished looks
- Muslin for lightweight everyday clothing
- Chanderi for festive and elegant occasions
- Georgette for festive and evening wear
- Organza for statement and party looks
- Silk and Tissue for richer festive and wedding occasions

The brand's Journal encourages customers to select fabric according to the occasion, weather, desired appearance, and level of comfort.

## Personalized Shopping Experience

Nari18's approach extends beyond selling products.

The brand provides customers with guidance around:

- Fabric selection
- Occasion selection
- Styling
- Custom tailoring
- Measurements
- Silhouette
- Care of fabrics

Customers can shop online through the Nari18 website or visit the physical boutique in Gurugram.

The boutique allows customers to experience fabrics physically and discuss styling and tailoring requirements in person.

The two channels are intended to provide access to the same core Nari18 philosophy: premium fabric, Indian craftsmanship, and personalized fit.

## Nari18's View of Craftsmanship

Nari18's brand communication places importance on the human work behind handcrafted textiles.

The company distinguishes artisan-made products from mass-produced ethnic wear by emphasizing:

- Traditional techniques
- Manual craftsmanship
- Individual artisan work
- Regional craft traditions
- Natural variation in handmade products
- The time and skill required to produce detailed textile work

For handcrafted products, slight variations in embroidery, printing, texture, or finishing can occur because of the manual production process.

These variations are part of the character of handcrafted textiles.

## Brand Promise

Nari18's brand proposition can be expressed as:

**Traditional Indian craftsmanship + premium fabrics + personalized tailoring.**

The company aims to give customers access to handcrafted ethnic fabrics while allowing them to create garments according to their own measurements and styling preferences.

The resulting experience combines the heritage of Indian textile crafts with the flexibility of contemporary personalized clothing.

Nari18 differentiates its offering through the combination of:
