# Nari18 Company Overview

## Company Introduction

Nari18 is a premium ethnic-wear boutique specializing in unstitched suits, handcrafted fabrics, ethnic wear, and customized tailoring. The company was founded by Richa Singh in 2018 in Gurugram, Haryana.

Nari18 operates both as an online boutique and as a physical boutique in Gurugram, serving customers looking for curated Indian ethnic wear and personalized tailoring. The brand combines fabric selection, styling guidance, and custom stitching to provide customers with outfits tailored to their individual preferences.

Nari18's core offering is centered around premium unstitched suit sets that allow customers to select fabrics and designs and have them customized according to their preferred fit, neckline, sleeve style, and overall silhouette.

## Company History

Nari18 was established in 2018 by Richa Singh in Gurugram, Haryana.

The brand began from an interest in India's textile heritage and developed into a boutique focused on handcrafted ethnic wear, premium fabrics, unstitched suit sets, and bespoke stitching.

The company emphasizes sourcing fabrics and handcrafted work associated with traditional Indian artisan communities. Its stated sourcing heritage includes Chikankari embroidery from Lucknow and silk weaving traditions from Chanderi.

## Custom Tailoring

Customized tailoring is a central part of the Nari18 offering.

Nari18 provides made-to-measure ethnic-wear tailoring through its in-house tailoring team. Customers can discuss their preferred styling and fitting requirements with the design team after placing an order.

Customization discussions can include preferences such as:

- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Fit
- Silhouette
- Measurements
- Other finishing and styling requirements

Nari18 describes its tailoring service as a way to transform an unstitched fabric or suit set into a personalized outfit.
