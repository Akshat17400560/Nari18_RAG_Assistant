# Why Choose Nari18

Nari18 is designed for customers looking for premium Indian ethnic wear with greater flexibility, craftsmanship, and personalization than standard ready-to-wear clothing.

## 1. Premium Unstitched Suits

Nari18 specializes in premium unstitched suit sets, giving customers flexibility to create a garment according to their preferred fit and styling.

## 2. Curated Indian Fabrics

The collection includes fabrics such as:

- Silk
- Chanderi
- Organza
- Georgette
- Muslin
- Cotton
- Linen
- Tissue
- Chiffon
- Crepe

Fabric selection can be based on occasion, season, comfort, and desired appearance.

## 3. Traditional Indian Craftsmanship

Nari18 works with textile and craft traditions associated with different regions of India, including:

- Lucknow Chikankari
- Chanderi weaving
- Jaipur hand-block printing
- Traditional embroidery and handcrafted textiles

This gives the collection a strong connection to Indian textile heritage.

## 4. Custom Stitching

Customers can request custom stitching for eligible products. Nari18's tailoring process can accommodate preferences such as:

- Measurements
- Neckline
- Sleeve style
- Garment length
- Fit
- Silhouette

The brand provides guidance through its tailoring team and WhatsApp consultation process.

## 5. Personalized Shopping Experience

Customers can receive guidance when choosing fabrics, styles, and customization options.

The physical boutique in Gurugram also provides an opportunity to examine fabrics and discuss styling and stitching requirements in person.

## 6. Online + Boutique Shopping

Nari18 offers both:

- Online shopping through its website
- In-person shopping at its Gurugram boutique

This allows customers to choose between convenient online purchasing and a more personal boutique experience.

## 7. Focus on Craft and Individuality

Nari18's handcrafted positioning means that subtle variations in fabrics, prints, embroidery, or other handmade details may occur. These variations can be part of the individual character of artisan-made products.
