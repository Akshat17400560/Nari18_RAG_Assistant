# Nari18 How to Order Guide

## 1. Overview

Nari18 allows customers to order premium unstitched suits and ethnic wear online across India. Customers can purchase an unstitched suit as fabric or, where available, use Nari18's custom stitching service to have the suit tailored to their measurements.

The basic online ordering flow is:

**Choose Product → Review Details → Add to Cart / Buy Now → Checkout → Enter Delivery Details → Payment → Order Confirmation → Optional Custom Stitching**

Nari18 currently states that online orders are shipped across India, with free domestic shipping above ₹5,000 subject to current checkout thresholds and exceptions.


# 2. Before Placing an Order

Before clicking **Buy Now** or proceeding to checkout, confirm what you actually want to purchase.

### Decide:

- Fabric
- Occasion
- Colour
- Budget
- Unstitched or stitched format
- Custom stitching requirement
- Desired silhouette
- Required delivery timing

For unstitched suits, also check whether the product contains the expected:

- Kurta/top fabric
- Bottom fabric
- Dupatta

Nari18's buying guidance recommends checking product descriptions and components carefully before ordering.


# 3. Step 1 — Browse Nari18

Start from the Nari18 catalog or a specific fabric collection.

The current catalog allows customers to browse products by categories and fabric families including:

- Silk
- Organza
- Tissue
- Muslin
- Cotton
- Linen
- Georgette
- Chanderi
- Crepe
- Chiffon
- Mul-Cotton
- Other available categories

The exact product selection changes over time.

### Shopping Tip

If you already know your requirement, use a collection instead of browsing everything.

Examples:

**Wedding →** Silk / Chanderi  
**Summer office →** Linen / Cotton  
**Daily wear →** Cotton / Muslin  
**Party →** Organza / Georgette  
**Grand festive →** Silk / Tissue


# 4. Step 2 — Open the Product

Once you find a suitable product, open its individual product page.

Review:

- Product name
- Current price
- Listed/original price where shown
- Discount
- Rating/reviews where available
- Product images
- Fabric information
- Product components
- Design details
- Availability
- Stitching options where offered

Do not rely only on the collection thumbnail.

The individual product page should be treated as the primary source for product-specific information.


# 5. Step 3 — Verify the Product Details

Before ordering, confirm what the product actually contains.

### Check the Fabric

Verify whether the fabric applies to:

- Kurta
- Bottom
- Dupatta

A product may use different materials for different components.

### Check the Design

Look for explicitly stated details such as:

- Embroidery
- Print
- Zari
- Motifs
- Borders
- Tassels
- Embellishments

Do not assume a design feature solely from an image.

### Check Availability

If the product is shown as sold out or unavailable, do not assume that it can be ordered.

Product availability is dynamic.


# 6. Step 4 — Check the Current Price

Review the current product price before adding the item to your order.

Nari18 may display:

- Current selling price
- Listed price
- Discount percentage
- Promotional offer

Prices and discounts can change.

The price shown during checkout should be treated as the final amount applicable to the current order.


# 7. Step 5 — Choose Your Purchase Method

Depending on the product, you may see options such as:

- **Add to Cart**
- **Buy Now**
- Custom stitching/tailoring options where offered

### Add to Cart

Use this when:

- You want to continue shopping
- You want multiple products
- You want to review the entire order before checkout

### Buy Now

Use this when:

- You have selected the product
- You want to proceed directly toward checkout

The exact buttons and available options can vary by product and current website configuration.


# 8. Step 6 — Decide Whether You Want Custom Stitching

Nari18 offers custom stitching for unstitched suits, with the current homepage stating that stitching is available on many pieces and can be selected at checkout where offered.

If you only want the fabric:

**Order the unstitched product without adding custom tailoring.**

If you want Nari18 to stitch it:

**Select the available stitching option or contact Nari18 for tailoring guidance.**

Nari18's custom-stitching guides describe an after-order WhatsApp consultation for measurements and design preferences.


# 9. Step 7 — Prepare Measurements for Custom Stitching

If you want a custom-stitched suit, prepare your measurements.

Nari18's current guides reference measurements such as:

- Bust
- Waist
- Hip
- Shoulder width
- Kurta length
- Sleeve length
- Bottom inseam where required

Nari18 provides measurement guidance if the customer is unsure.

### Measurement Tip

Take measurements while wearing the type of undergarment you intend to wear with the finished suit.

Avoid measuring over thick clothing.

Accurate measurements are particularly important because custom-stitched orders are made to measure.


# 10. Step 8 — Select Stitching Preferences

If custom stitching is selected, Nari18's tailoring process can include discussion of:

### Silhouette

Examples include:

- Straight-cut
- A-line
- Flared Anarkali
- Palazzo
- Sharara
- Short kurta
- Co-ordinated styling where appropriate

### Neckline

Examples include:

- Round
- V-neck
- Boat neck
- Keyhole
- Mandarin collar

### Sleeves

Examples include:

- Full
- Three-quarter
- Half
- Sleeveless

### Fit

Discuss:

- Comfortable fit
- Regular fit
- More fitted styling
- Desired garment length
- Sleeve preference

The final options depend on the product, fabric, construction and tailoring requirements.


# 11. Step 9 — Add the Product to the Cart

After reviewing the product and available options:

1. Select the required product/variant if applicable.
2. Select the required quantity.
3. Select available stitching options if required.
4. Click **Add to Cart**.

If you are purchasing multiple products, repeat the process for each item.


# 12. Step 10 — Review the Cart

Before checkout, carefully review the cart.

Check:

- Product name
- Product quantity
- Colour/variant
- Current price
- Discount
- Stitching option
- Applicable stitching charges
- Product subtotal
- Shipping charge
- Final order value

This is the last simple point to correct an accidental product or quantity selection before completing checkout.


# 13. Step 11 — Proceed to Checkout

After confirming the cart, proceed to checkout.

The checkout process should be used to verify the final:

- Product total
- Shipping charge
- Applicable discount
- Final payable amount
- Delivery details
- Available payment method

Nari18 describes its checkout as using encrypted payment processing for domestic and international cards. 


# 14. Step 12 — Enter Delivery Information

Provide the required delivery information accurately.

Typically verify:

- Full name
- Mobile number
- Email address
- House/flat number
- Building/street
- Area/locality
- City
- State
- PIN code

### Important

The delivery address should be checked carefully before payment.
An incorrect PIN code, incomplete address or incorrect phone number can create delivery problems.


# 15. Step 13 — Check Shipping Charges

Nari18 currently advertises free shipping for domestic orders above **₹5,000**.

The homepage also notes that customers should check checkout for current thresholds and exceptions. 

Therefore:

**Do not assume that every order ships free.**
Always check the shipping line shown at checkout.


# 16. Step 14 — Apply an Available Coupon

If Nari18 provides a promotional coupon that applies to your order, enter it during the applicable checkout stage.

The current Nari18 homepage advertises a first-order welcome offer of **5% off using WELCOME5**. Promotional offers are time-sensitive and can change.


# 17. Step 15 — Select Payment

Proceed with one of the payment methods currently presented by Nari18's checkout.

Before confirming payment:

- Check the final amount
- Check the delivery address
- Check the selected products
- Check any discount
- Check shipping
- Check stitching charges if applicable

Nari18 states that its payment processing is encrypted for domestic and international card payments.


# 18. Step 16 — Complete the Order

After successful payment, the order should be submitted through the website.

Save the available:

- Order number
- Order confirmation
- Payment confirmation
- Product details
- Delivery information

Keep these details available if you later need to contact Nari18 about the order.

# 21. What Happens If You Order Only the Fabric?

If custom stitching is not selected:

1. Place the order.
2. Complete payment.
3. Receive the unstitched fabric.
4. Inspect the complete set.
5. Verify the fabric and components.
6. Follow the product-specific care instructions.
7. Take it to your preferred tailor or arrange stitching separately.

### Important

Do not cut or stitch the fabric before checking it for:

- Correct colour
- Correct product
- Fabric condition
- Embroidery
- Print
- Borders
- Kurta fabric
- Bottom fabric
- Dupatta

Nari18's current fabric-care guidance recommends inspecting unstitched fabric before cutting or stitching.


# 22. Order Timing

If the product is required for a specific event, order sufficiently in advance.

This is especially important when requesting custom stitching.

Nari18's current guide distinguishes stitching time from delivery time. Custom stitching is described as generally taking around **3–7 days**, while online delivery is separately described in the current guide.

### For Event Orders

If you need the suit for:

- Wedding
- Reception
- Sangeet
- Festival
- Travel
- Important function

confirm the expected delivery timeline with Nari18 before relying on a particular date.


# 23. After Receiving the Order

Inspect the parcel before using the fabric.

### For an unstitched suit:

Check:

- Product
- Colour
- Fabric
- Kurta/top piece
- Bottom piece
- Dupatta
- Embroidery
- Print
- Borders
- Visible defects

### For a custom-stitched suit:

Check:

- Overall fit
- Length
- Sleeves
- Neckline
- Stitching
- Finishing
- Fabric handling
- Decorative placement

If something appears incorrect or defective, contact Nari18 promptly.

# 24. Manufacturing Defect Exchange

Nari18's current Promise states that exchange requests for manufacturing defects are accepted within **7 days**.
Customers should provide clear photos or videos so the team can verify the issue.

### Important

A manufacturing-defect exchange is different from simply changing your mind about a product.

Always follow the current Nari18 Terms and Promise for the exact eligibility requirements.


# 25. Custom-Stitched Order Policy

Nari18 currently states that **custom-stitched orders are not eligible for exchange** because they are made to the customer's measurements.

Therefore, before custom stitching:

- Verify measurements
- Confirm fit preferences
- Confirm silhouette
- Confirm neckline
- Confirm sleeves
- Confirm lining requirements
- Ask questions before the tailoring process begins
