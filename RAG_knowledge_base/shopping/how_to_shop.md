# Nari18 How to Shop Guide

## 1. Overview

Nari18 is an online boutique focused primarily on premium unstitched suits and ethnic wear, with fabric-led collections and custom stitching available on many products.

The online shopping process is designed to let customers:

1. Browse the catalog
2. Filter products by fabric, category or price
3. Open individual product pages
4. Review images and product information
5. Check current availability and pricing
6. Add the product to cart or use Buy Now
7. Select available stitching options where offered
8. Complete checkout
9. Contact Nari18 for measurements or tailoring guidance when required
10. Receive the order across India

Nari18's current catalog provides product filters, sorting, search, product images, variants and availability information.

# 8. Open the Product Page Before Buying

Do not purchase solely from the collection thumbnail.
Open the individual product page and verify:

### Fabric

Check exactly what fabric is described.
Do not assume that a product belongs entirely to one fabric just because the collection name suggests it.

For example, a suit may contain:

- One fabric for the kurta
- Another for the bottom
- Another for the dupatta

### Components

Verify whether the set contains:

- Kurta/top fabric
- Bottom fabric
- Dupatta

Nari18 specifically advises online buyers to verify that the top, bottom and dupatta are included and described before purchasing an unstitched set.

### Design Details

Check for information about:

- Embroidery
- Print
- Zari
- Borders
- Motifs
- Neckline
- Dupatta detailing
- Decorative elements

Do not infer these details from photographs alone.


# 9. Inspect the Product Images

Images are particularly important when shopping for unstitched fabric online because the customer cannot physically touch the material before ordering.

Look for:

- Full outfit view
- Close-up of fabric
- Embroidery details
- Dupatta
- Border
- Back/side views where available
- Colour under different lighting
- Decorative work
- Overall pattern placement

Nari18's buying guide recommends looking for multiple product images, different angles, natural-light views and close-ups of embroidery or prints.

### Colour Warning

Screen colours can differ from the physical fabric.
If exact colour is important, contact Nari18 before ordering rather than relying only on the screen image.


# 10. Check Whether the Product Is Unstitched

Nari18 sells unstitched suits as fabric sets.
An unstitched suit normally means that the fabric has not yet been cut and constructed into the final garment.
Before ordering, determine whether you want:

### Fabric Only

You purchase the unstitched set and arrange stitching yourself.

### Custom Stitching

You purchase the unstitched set and request Nari18's tailoring service where it is offered.

### Existing Stitched Product

If the product is already stitched, its sizing and construction should be evaluated differently.

Do not assume that buying an unstitched product automatically includes stitching.

If you purchased only the unstitched fabric:

# 22. Common Shopping Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1 — Buying only from the photograph

A photograph cannot provide complete information about fabric composition, weight or construction.

### Mistake 2 — Assuming the collection name describes every component

A collection may contain products with mixed fabric components.

### Mistake 3 — Assuming stitching is included

Unstitched fabric and custom stitching are separate considerations.

### Mistake 4 — Ignoring the product description

Important details may be available only on the product page.

### Mistake 5 — Ignoring availability

A product that was previously available may become sold out.

### Mistake 6 — Assuming the displayed price is permanent

Prices and discounts can change.

### Mistake 7 — Ignoring the return/exchange policy

Especially important for customized garments.

### Mistake 8 — Cutting the fabric immediately

Inspect the complete set first.

### Mistake 9 — Waiting until after ordering to clarify critical requirements

If colour, fabric, measurements or a special event deadline is important, contact Nari18 before committing.
