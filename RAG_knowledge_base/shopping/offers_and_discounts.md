# Nari18 Offers and Discounts

## 1. Overview

Nari18 offers several types of promotions and savings across its online store. Current website information shows:

- **5% off on the first order** using coupon code `WELCOME5`
- **₹50 saving on prepaid payment** through UPI/card on applicable products
- **Free 30-minute wardrobe styling session on orders of ₹10,000+**
- **Product-level sale discounts** displayed directly on products
- **Free domestic shipping above the current qualifying order threshold**
- Seasonal or promotional offers may change over time

Because Nari18's promotions are dynamic, the **current product page and checkout should be treated as the final source of truth** for the discount applicable to a particular order.

# 9. How Product Discounts Are Displayed

Nari18 generally shows:

**Current price + original/listed price + discount percentage**

For example:

**₹3,315 ₹5,500 — 40% off**

Customers should use the current selling price as the actual product price before any additional eligible checkout promotion.

---

# 10. Product Discount vs Coupon Discount

These are different concepts.

### Product discount

The product is already listed at a reduced selling price.

Example:

- Listed price: ₹5,500
- Current price: ₹3,315
- Displayed discount: 40%

### Coupon discount

A coupon is applied separately during checkout.

Example:

- Eligible first order
- Coupon: `WELCOME5`
- Additional discount calculated by checkout

Whether multiple offers can be combined should **not** be assumed.

The live checkout should determine whether a coupon can be applied to an already discounted product.

# 11. Product Discount vs Prepaid Saving

For example, a product can simultaneously show:

- A reduced product price

However, the customer should not assume that all three benefits will automatically stack.

The checkout should be treated as the final authority for the actual payable amount.

# 18. Sale Price and Original Price

When evaluating a sale product, customers should distinguish:

### Original/listed price

The higher reference price displayed on the product.

### Current selling price

The amount currently charged for the product before applicable checkout-level promotions.

### Discount percentage

The percentage reduction displayed by Nari18.

### Final payable price

The amount after eligible:

- Product discount
- Coupon
- Prepaid saving
- Shipping charges
- Applicable taxes/other checkout adjustments

The final checkout amount is the most reliable amount for the customer's actual order.

# 24. Offers and Returns/Exchanges

A discount does not automatically change Nari18's product policy.

Nari18 currently states that manufacturing-defect exchange requests are accepted within 7 days, while custom-stitched orders are not eligible for exchange.

Customers should therefore check the current return/exchange terms before purchasing discounted or customized products.

Do not tell a customer that a sale item is automatically returnable or non-returnable unless the current applicable policy confirms it.
