# Nari18 Payment Methods

## 1. Overview

Nari18 provides multiple payment options for online orders. Current Nari18 product pages show:

- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**
- **Razorpay**
- **UPI**
- **Cards**
- **Net banking**

Nari18's product pages describe the checkout as safe and secure, with card, UPI and net-banking payments processed through an encrypted checkout.

The exact payment methods available for a particular order can depend on the current checkout configuration, order value, delivery location and other applicable conditions.

---

# 2. Payment Methods at a Glance

| Payment Method | Available on Nari18 | Main Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cash on Delivery | Yes, where offered | Pay when order is delivered |
| UPI | Yes | Fast prepaid online payment |
| Debit/Credit Cards | Yes | Prepaid card payment |
| Net Banking | Yes | Direct bank-account payment |
| Razorpay | Yes | Payment gateway handling online payments |

Current Nari18 product pages explicitly display **“COD available”** and **“Razorpay (UPI / Cards)”**. They also state that cards, UPI and net banking are processed through secure, encrypted checkout.

---

# 3. Cash on Delivery (COD)

Nari18 currently displays **COD available** on product pages.

With Cash on Delivery:

1. Select the product.
2. Add it to the cart or choose Buy Now.
3. Proceed to checkout.
4. Enter the delivery information.
5. Select COD if it is offered for the order.
6. Complete the order.
7. Pay when the order is delivered.

### Important

COD availability should be checked at checkout.

Do not assume that COD is available for every:

- Product
- Order value
- Delivery location
- Order type
- Custom-stitching order

The current product pages show COD availability, but the checkout should be treated as the final authority for whether COD is offered for a particular order.

---

# 4. UPI Payments

UPI is available through Nari18's Razorpay-powered online checkout.

Current product pages specifically display:

**Razorpay (UPI / Cards)**

and also show a prepaid offer where applicable.

UPI can be useful for customers who want:

- Fast payment
- Prepaid ordering
- Mobile-based payment
- Digital payment without entering card details

Depending on the payment interface, the customer may be able to use a supported UPI application or UPI payment flow presented by the checkout.

---

# 5. Debit and Credit Cards

Nari18 supports card payments through its online payment gateway.

Current product pages state:

**Cards, UPI & net banking are processed through secure, encrypted checkout.**

Card payment can be used when customers prefer prepaid payment through:

- Debit card
- Credit card
- Supported card networks presented by the payment gateway

The exact card types and supported networks should be determined by the live payment page rather than assumed from the product page.

---

# 6. Net Banking

Nari18's product pages also explicitly mention **net banking** as a supported online payment method.

A customer selecting net banking can complete the payment through the banking options presented by the payment gateway.

The exact bank list may change and should be checked in the live Razorpay payment interface.

---

# 7. Razorpay

Razorpay is the payment gateway currently shown on Nari18 product pages.

Nari18 displays:

**Razorpay (UPI / Cards)**

on current products.

The payment gateway handles supported prepaid payment methods such as:

- UPI
- Cards
- Net banking

The exact methods shown can depend on the current Razorpay checkout configuration.

---

# 8. Secure Payment

Nari18's product pages include a **Secure payment** section stating that cards, UPI and net banking are processed through secure, encrypted checkout.

Customers should still follow normal online-payment security practices:

- Verify that the checkout is the genuine Nari18 payment flow.
- Do not share OTPs with another person.
- Do not share card PINs.
- Do not share UPI PINs.
- Do not provide banking credentials through WhatsApp or email.
- Complete payment only through the official checkout/payment interface.

---

# 9. Prepaid Payment Offer

Current Nari18 product pages display a prepaid-payment incentive:

**Save ₹50 on prepaid**

For example, a product displayed at ₹3,315 currently shows a prepaid amount of ₹3,265, while another product displayed at ₹6,363 shows ₹6,313 for prepaid payment.

### Important

This should be treated as a **current promotional offer**, not a permanent payment rule.

The offer can change or be removed.

The amount shown at the current checkout should always be treated as the final applicable prepaid price.

# 10. COD vs Prepaid

| Feature | COD | Prepaid |
|---|---|---|
| Payment timing | At delivery | Before order processing |
| UPI required | No | Usually available |
| Card required | No | Can be used |
| Net banking | No | Available |
| Prepaid discount | No | May apply |
| Payment convenience | Useful for customers avoiding advance payment | Faster digital transaction |
| Availability | Subject to checkout | Subject to payment gateway |

If a prepaid incentive is active, prepaid payment may reduce the payable amount. Current Nari18 product pages show a ₹50 prepaid saving.

# 11. How to Pay by UPI

A typical UPI payment flow is:

1. Select the desired product.
2. Add it to the cart.
3. Proceed to checkout.
4. Enter the delivery information.
5. Select the Razorpay payment option.
6. Choose UPI.
7. Complete the UPI payment through the available payment interface.
8. Wait for payment confirmation.
9. Save the order confirmation.

The exact screens can change depending on the current Razorpay checkout.

---

# 12. How to Pay by Card

A typical card payment flow is:

1. Select the product.
2. Proceed to checkout.
3. Enter delivery details.
4. Choose online payment.
5. Select card payment.
6. Enter the requested card information in the secure payment interface.
7. Complete the bank/card authentication if requested.
8. Wait for payment confirmation.
9. Save the order confirmation.

Never share your OTP or card PIN with Nari18 staff or any other person.

---

# 13. How to Pay by Net Banking

A typical net-banking flow is:

1. Proceed to checkout.
2. Select Razorpay/online payment.
3. Select net banking.
4. Choose the bank available in the payment interface.
5. Complete authentication on the bank/payment page.
6. Confirm the payment.
7. Return to the order confirmation page.

The available bank list is controlled by the payment gateway and may change.

---

# 14. How to Use Cash on Delivery

If COD is available:

1. Select the product.
2. Add it to the cart.
3. Proceed to checkout.
4. Enter the correct delivery address.
5. Check whether COD is displayed.
6. Select COD.
7. Confirm the order.
8. Pay according to the COD instructions when the order is delivered.

Do not assume that COD will appear for every order. The live checkout determines eligibility.

---

# 15. Payment and Custom Stitching

Payment for an unstitched product and custom stitching should be understood separately.

A customer may:

### Option A — Buy Unstitched Only

Pay for the product and receive the unstitched fabric.

### Option B — Buy With Custom Stitching

Purchase the product and use Nari18's available tailoring/custom-stitching service.

Where stitching is offered, the applicable stitching option and charges should be reviewed before completing checkout.

Nari18's current product pages show a **Book a Tailor** option on many products, while some products explicitly provide stitched or size-based ordering options.

---

# 16. Payment for Stitched Products

Nari18 also lists products that are already stitched.

For example, current product pages can show:

- Size selection
- Size chart
- S, M, L, XL, XXL
- Quantity
- Select size

A current Mul Cotton stitched product, for example, displays multiple sizes and requires a size selection before ordering.

For stitched products:

1. Select the required size where applicable.
2. Review the size chart.
3. Select quantity.
4. Add to cart or Buy Now.
5. Proceed to payment.

Do not confuse a stitched product with an unstitched product that offers custom tailoring.

---

# 17. Payment Confirmation

After successfully completing a prepaid payment, wait for the website/payment gateway to confirm the transaction.

A successful bank debit alone should not be treated as sufficient proof that the Nari18 order has been successfully created.

Customers should retain:

- Payment confirmation
- Order confirmation
- Order number
- Transaction/reference information where available

If the payment is debited but the order confirmation does not appear, contact Nari18 support with the transaction details.

---

# 18. Payment Failure

A payment can fail because of issues such as:

- Bank decline
- UPI failure
- Card authentication failure
- Network interruption
- Payment gateway timeout
- Insufficient balance
- Bank-side technical issue

If payment fails:

1. Check whether money was actually debited.
2. Check whether an order confirmation was generated.
3. Avoid making repeated payments immediately if the first transaction is still processing.
4. Check your bank/payment application.
5. Contact Nari18 if the payment status remains unclear.

Do not assume that a failed payment automatically means that no amount was temporarily debited.

---

# 19. Amount Debited but Order Not Confirmed

If money has been deducted but the Nari18 order is not confirmed:

### First

Check:

- Bank statement
- UPI application
- Card transaction history
- Email/SMS payment notification
- Nari18 order/account information

### Then

Contact Nari18 and provide:

- Name
- Contact number
- Approximate payment time
- Amount
- Payment method
- Transaction/reference ID
- Screenshot if requested

Avoid sharing:

- UPI PIN
- OTP
- Card CVV unnecessarily
- Full banking credentials

# 21. Payment and Shipping

Payment method and shipping eligibility are separate considerations.

Nari18 currently advertises pan-India delivery on product pages and free domestic shipping above ₹5,000.

The customer should review:

- Product amount
- Stitching charges if applicable
- Shipping charge
- Discounts
- Prepaid savings
- Final payable amount

at checkout before payment.

---

# 22. Payment and Returns/Exchanges

Payment method does not change the basic product policy.

Nari18 currently displays a **7-day exchange for defects** on product pages, while its Promise states that manufacturing-defect exchange requests must be raised within 7 days. Custom-stitched orders are currently stated to be non-exchangeable.

A customer should therefore review the current policy before purchasing, especially for:

- Custom-stitched orders
- High-value products
- Sale products
- Products with special tailoring requirements

# 24. Payment Method Selection Guide

## Choose COD when:

- COD is available for the order.
- You prefer paying upon delivery.
- You do not want to make an advance digital payment.

## Choose UPI when:

- You want quick prepaid payment.
- You prefer mobile payment.
- A prepaid offer is available.

## Choose Card when:

- You prefer debit/credit card payment.
- You want to pay online immediately.

## Choose Net Banking when:

- You prefer direct bank payment.
- Your bank is supported by the payment gateway.

## Choose Prepaid when:

- You want to complete payment immediately.
- A current prepaid discount makes it beneficial.

# 25. Common Payment Problems

## "COD is not showing"

Possible reasons include:

- Order eligibility
- Delivery location
- Product restrictions
- Current checkout configuration

Check the live checkout or contact Nari18.

## "My UPI payment failed"

Check your UPI app and bank status before attempting another payment.

## "My card was charged but order is not confirmed"

Do not immediately pay again. First verify the transaction and order status.

## "I don't see net banking"

The payment methods displayed are controlled by the current payment gateway. Check the live Razorpay interface.

## "The prepaid discount is not showing"

Check whether the current promotion applies to your product/order and verify the final checkout amount.
